### "Strafverteidiger" and BRAO § 43b

- "Strafverteidiger" is not a protected title. The protected title is "Fachanwalt für Strafrecht", and she does not hold it. The firm defends clients in tax criminal cases. So the category is factually true.
- The category is a classification, not an advertising claim. It contains no "spezialisiert" and no quality claim. Risk under § 43b BRAO: low.
- Real risk is a business risk. "Strafverteidiger" also brings general criminal cases (drugs, assault, traffic). The source of truth (tab 01, row 54) warns about "Streuverlust auf 'Strafverteidiger'" for Ads. The baseline scan of 1 Oct shows 0 % for "strafverteidiger". The firm must say if it wants these calls.
- "Anwalt für Steuerrecht": she is Fachanwältin für Steuerrecht since 2021. True. No risk.
