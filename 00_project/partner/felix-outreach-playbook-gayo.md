# Felix Meinhardt: LinkedIn outreach playbook (for Gayo)

Status 13.10.2026 · internal, not for Felix · source: script v3 (Drive doc 1CeGgYQ1ESXliPhFxTbk2rvGcn0niCC0-Ys3fiAvlG5Q), call 25.09.2026, portal account "Felix Meinhardt".

## Who Felix is and what we sell
- Felix is a filmmaker. He and his team build experts in the media: expert film, YouTube, podcast, up to TV placements.
- Entry offer: expert film (5,000–9,000 €). Big deal: mentoring "Sichtbar mehr Erfolg" (24,000 € / 36,000 €). Felix closes the big deal himself.
- We write from Felix's LinkedIn account, in his voice ("ich"), in German.

## Before day 1 (all must be done)
1. Done (Martin, 13.10.): Felix approved the first message and gave LinkedIn access.
2. Felix gives his block list (names and companies we never write to).
3. Felix is careful with logins, so only Gayo logs in to his account, always from the same device.
4. Gayo builds the lead list. He sends 20 rows to Martin; the agent checks them against the rules. Start only after that check.

## Lead list
1. Export Felix's 1st-degree connections: in his account, Settings → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data → Connections. The file comes by e-mail to Felix, usually within minutes.
2. Add role, company, followers, website and media signals for each person.
3. Keep only people who match all rules:
   • DACH, 1st-degree contact, no conversation with Felix before
   • speaker, coach, consultant, trainer, author, expert or owner with a personal brand
   • strong in the media: 1,000+ followers, own book, own stage or keynotes, own podcast or YouTube channel, or press/TV
   • own expert brand with own offer (talks, trainings, programs, book) and website; a job next to it is fine (Felix's example: Dr. Winthir Brunnbauer, BMW Group + speaker/trainer, https://www.winthirbrunnbauer.com, expert film https://youtu.be/D3z02qOibJ0)
   • active on LinkedIn in the last 90 days
4. Remove: filmmakers, videographers, agencies, production companies, employees without their own brand or offer, press, TV editors, broadcasters, Felix's current clients and open offers, his block list.
5. Priority: P1 = strong in the media + almost no professional video. P2 = strong in the media + already uses video. P3 = established, little reach yet.
6. Columns: Name · LinkedIn URL · Role · Company · Followers · Website · Media signals · Video yes/no · Priority · Variant 1/2 · Status · Date · Note.

## Sequence (per person)
| Day | Step | Text |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | First message | Variant 1 (with the Roberto reference) for P1 and P2, variant 2 (short) for P3. Replace [Vorname] and [Thema] by hand. [Thema] must be his real topic, from his profile. |
| 4–5 | Follow-up 1 | Only if no reply. |
| 11–12 | Follow-up 2 (last) | Only if no reply. Contains the video link. After this: stop. |

No price and no link in the first message. Links always with https://www.

## Daily rhythm
- Max 20–25 messages per day in total (first messages + follow-ups). No connection requests: all leads are already contacts.
- Week 1: start with 10 first messages per day for 3 days, then go up to the limit. Human pace: send them spread over the day, not all in 5 minutes.
- Do follow-ups first, then new first messages.
- Morning plan in the portal: messages target for the account "Felix Meinhardt".
- Evening report in the portal for "Felix Meinhardt": pitched, follow-ups, replies, interested. Requests and accepted stay 0.

## Replies
| Reply | What Gayo does |
|---|---|
| Interested ("yes", "tell me more", asks for price or call) | Send the handover text from the script. Tell Martin the same day on WhatsApp: name, LinkedIn URL, the reply. Martin takes over. Never name a price. |
| "Not now" / "maybe later" | Thank him or her, no more follow-ups. Status "later", note the date. |
| "No" | One short polite line, then stop. Status "no". |
| Question we cannot answer | Do not guess. Ask Martin first. |
| Angry or "stop" | Say sorry in one line, stop, status "do not contact". |

Every reply goes in the list on the same day. Felix can see in the list what went out.

## Never
- Never write from Felix's account outside this script without Martin's OK.
- Never change the texts on your own.
- Never write to people on the block list or to Felix's clients.
- Never send more than 25 messages in a day.
