**Rules for Idea 1 derived from the references:**
- Camera: phone at table height or straight top-down; the plate fills at least the lower half of the frame.
- Motion in frame: a hand (fries pulled, knife cut, sauce poured) — never a static product shot.
- Context in the background: wine glass, a second red-rimmed plate, the marble, soft bougainvillea or the sign — the venue must be recognisable as Little Frenchie, not a generic bistro.
- Text: one short on-screen line in the client's red hand-lettering style, top third, like "Lunch, in motion" — the generated image keeps the top third clean for it.
- Light: hard midday sun with stripes (the client's real look), not candle-dark like the London reference.

**Rules for Idea 2 derived from the references:**
- Three to five hero shots, each a different action: yolk cut, coffee pour, croissant tear, the room, the sign. Every frame close and bright.
- One big word per frame in the centre (Martin's pick), white or the client's red, forming one sentence across the cuts, e.g. "BRUNCH / IN / PERERENAN / FROM / 9AM". The generated image keeps the centre uncluttered so the word sits on a calm area.
- The venue's own branded objects in frame: red-rimmed plate with the bulldog, the logo paper, the round sign, rattan chairs, bougainvillea.
- Morning light, not midday: softer stripes, warm.
