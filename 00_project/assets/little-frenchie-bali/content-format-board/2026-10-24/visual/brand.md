## Instagram look (grid, 2026-10-24)
Bio: "neighborhood french-inspired bistro · pererenan, bali · open daily 9am–10pm". Highlights: Steak Frites, Turning 3!, summer'26, partnerships, events, catering, protein bowl.
Grid pattern: alternating red-background flat lays (many red-rimmed plates, dish names hand-written in white) and warm photo posts; hand-lettered red script over photos ("we're turning 3!", "merci merci merci"); polaroid-style collages for events; close-ups of steak frites and fries with "Almost here!" lettering; an Aperol spritz at the bar. Reels and carousels mixed. The restaurant's own voice is playful French ("La Grande Soirée", "encore", "merci").

## Image style · mood (for the prompt style block)
Warm, playful, French-bistro-in-Bali. Hard tropical sunlight with window stripes, white marble and red-rimmed plates, the bulldog logo on plates and the sign, bougainvillea and thatched roofs outside, hand-lettered red script as the only text style. Not: moody fine dining, not stock-photo polish, not cold light.
Mood words: sunlit · playful · French · tropical · red-and-white.
