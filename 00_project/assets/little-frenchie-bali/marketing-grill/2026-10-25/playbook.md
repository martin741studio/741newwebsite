# Little Frenchie (Pererenan, Bali): Marketing Plan, 2026-10-25

Produced by an ad-hoc run of the Marketing Grill method, grounded in the general knowledge base (the Core Four) and Little Frenchie's real review-page and content-board data. Internal document. This is a plan only. It does not send anything to Lovely, change her website, launch an ad, or commit spend. Turning the pitch section into actual client copy goes through `/content-write`; any email goes through the Airmailer flow with Martin's go; any ad spend needs its own reasoned go.

Note on scope: the installed `marketing-grill` skill is currently 741-only (its rules are B2B-agency-specific). This run used the general Core Four / Lead Getter framework from `_derived/visibility-leads-master-reference.md`, not the 741-scoped addendum, because Little Frenchie is a local F&B business, not a B2B agency.

## Phase 1 (Weeks 1-3): close the Google-Maps/SEO gap

Little Frenchie's review (`00_project/prospects/little-frenchie-bali/prospect.json`) found 4.8 stars from 1,421 reviews, more than two of the three restaurants ranking ahead of it, but visibility in the top 20 at only 5 of 49 measured points (average rank 15.0) against real local demand: "best restaurant canggu" gets 2,400 searches a month, and littlefrenchie.id is not in the top 20 for it. This is intent-based traffic already searching for exactly what she sells. Fix the Google-listing categories (currently only "French restaurant," missing "Brunch restaurant," "Bistro," "Cafe") and the page titles/headings (currently social-link text, not place/cuisine text) first, before organic content or paid spend starts. This matches the review page's own roadmap, "Hoch" priority and "Gering" effort for exactly this phase.

Everything below is planned in full now, not deferred as a vague idea, but execution waits for this phase to land, so it doesn't compete for Lovely's attention during the SEO fix.

## Organic reach (Core Four, channel 1 of 2 planned in detail)

**Format:** the 3 ideas content-format-board already drafted (`00_project/assets/little-frenchie-bali/content-format-board/2026-10-24/board.md`), each mapped to a real search gap:
- Single-Scene Hook: one steak frites plate, one take. Targets "French restaurant Bali" (90/mo), her clearest point of difference.
- Fast-Cut Story: the eggs benedict, visuals only, no speaker needed. Targets "brunch Canggu" (590/mo) and "best brunch Canggu" (210/mo).
- Talk-to-Camera: the owner answers "French food in Bali, what should I order first?" Targets "dinner Canggu" (1,000/mo), the highest-volume term, but needs a confirmed speaker.

**Cadence:** 3-5 videos a week, the knowledge base's own rule for this kind of business (`00_project/assets/little-frenchie-bali/content-format-board/2026-10-24/visual/brand.md` and the content-format-board reference library cite the same range for Instagram Reels, max 1-2/day). Starts once the Phase 1 listing/cleanup work lands, not before.

**On camera:** still an open question. Lovely, the owner, is the candidate content-format-board recorded, but it is not confirmed. This gets asked directly, not assumed. The two format ideas that need no speaker (Single-Scene Hook, Fast-Cut Story) can start regardless of her answer.

**Bridge to paid:** once a video proves itself organically, the knowledge base's Content-to-Ad Singularity rule applies directly, take the top-performing clip, add a call-to-action banner, run it as a paid ad rather than briefing new creative from scratch.

## Paid reach (Core Four, channel 2 of 2 planned in detail)

**Platform:** Google Ads first, not Meta. The reasoning is intent vs. interruption: 2,400 people a month already search "best restaurant canggu," an intent signal the SEO/listing work alone will take months to fully capture. Google Ads sits directly on top of that existing demand. Meta/Instagram stays the second platform, built on the organic video content once it exists, per the Content-to-Ad Singularity bridge above.

**Targeting:** a geographic radius around Canggu and Pererenan, on her highest-volume terms, "best restaurant canggu" (2,400/mo), "dinner canggu" (1,000/mo), "brunch canggu" (590/mo), the same keyword set the review page's own demand research already measured.

**Creative:** sourced from the organic video output once it exists, not briefed separately. This is deliberate. It keeps the plan to one production pipeline instead of two.

**Timing:** planned in full now, spend starts after Phase 1 (the listing/cleanup fix) is done. This is a timing decision, not an open question, the platform, targeting and creative source above are specified, so this is ready to execute, not a vague future idea.

**Budget note:** the review page's own interactive calculator carries placeholder slider defaults (`prospect.json`, field `calc`: a 150k Rp/month budget assumption, 1k Rp CPC), built for Lovely to adjust herself in conversation. These are not 741's recommended spend, they are the calculator's starting point, and any real budget figure for a paid-ads brief needs its own fresh number, not pulled from that calculator.

## Where to reach people: get named in the conversations that already exist

The content-format-board research found real Reddit threads and a travel guide already discussing "best brunch Canggu," "French restaurant Bali," and "brunch Pererenan" without naming Little Frenchie. This is a direct, low-cost Lead Getter opportunity, getting named in conversations that already rank, rather than building a new channel from zero. It waits until the Phase 1 listing fix lands, so there is something solid to point people to.
