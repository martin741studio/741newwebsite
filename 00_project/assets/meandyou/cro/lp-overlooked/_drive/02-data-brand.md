## Persona overrides „The Overlooked“ (design guide section 02, copy deck page 06)
- Palette: cool variant. Navy #2B3C52 for hero, method band and closing. Cool off-white #F6F6F4 and light grey #E7E8E8 for the other bands. Dove blue #7E99B6 for numbers, overlines and hairlines. Sand #D8CCB7 only as a small accent (stars, check marks on navy).
- Button: same label and shape as all pages. Deep dove blue #4F6D90 on light bands, cream #F2EDE4 with navy text on navy bands.
- Layout: visible thin grid, numbered steps, diagram before decoration, tables with thin rules, captions with real information.
- Photography: architectural, cool daylight, glass and steel, person in context with distance, black and white allowed.
- Mood words: präzise, glaubwürdig, unsentimental, ruhig, strukturiert.
