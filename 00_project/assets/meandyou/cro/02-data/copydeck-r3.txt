HOW TO USE THIS
READ THIS FIRST
–   German is the live copy. English is only so you know what you are setting.
–   The pipe symbol | is an intended line break. Keep it. It is where the line should turn.
–   German runs roughly 25 percent longer than English. Build every text box with room to grow.
–   Never hyphenate a headline automatically. Turn hyphenation off in Figma for all display type.
–   Umlauts matter: ä ö ü and ß. UNÜBERSEHBAR has Ü, not U. Check after every copy paste.
–   Anything in [square brackets] is a placeholder. It is not final copy.
