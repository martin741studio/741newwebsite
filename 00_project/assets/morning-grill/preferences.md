# Morning grill preferences (Martin's standing rules)

Read at the start of every run. The agent never edits this file. Martin changes it in the Monday review.
Source: grill of 2026-10-13 (spec `00_project/morning-grill-workflow.md` §1).

## Priorities (ranking of cards)
1. Fire: lost access, angry or worried client, broken site or tracking, legal or HWG risk
2. Money and contract: start dates, payments, renewals, new offers
3. Client deadline within 3 days, or a promise made to a client
4. Approval waiting more than 3 days
5. Goal gap (missing goal field, leading number red)
6. Pipeline follow-up due
7. Internal 741 work

## Limits
- Max 7 cards per morning. Max 2 rounds of 4 clickable cards.
- Brief at 06:00 WITA. Evening log draft at 17:00 WITA. Time zone WITA (UTC+8).
- Lookback: since the last grill (bookmarks). Monday covers the weekend.

## Defaults (no card needed)
- Drafts and read-only scans.
- Team tasks that follow the plan Martin approved in the grill.
- Portal task status "needs review" (never "done").

## Always a card
- Anything external: client messages, publishing, live-site changes, outreach sends.
- Money: purchases, data spend outside a running workflow, offers with prices.
- Launches and installs (skills, schedules, portal changes).

## Handoff
- Human task = portal task (after GO-PORTAL; until then a draft in plan.md) + a WhatsApp line for Martin to forward.
- Answer format: clickable cards in chat; short string "1a 2p 3b" also works. Park needs a reason.

## Not in the brief
- The Martin guide character (internal brief).
- Tool or vendor names in any client message draft.
