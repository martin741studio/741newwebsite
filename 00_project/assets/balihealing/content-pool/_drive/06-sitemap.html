Why the core topics are hard to find today

  1. The homepage is thin. It has 452 words. The floor is 612 words (median 874.5 words of the clinic homepages). The service pages have 175 to 1067 words. Most pages exist. The work is prices, proof, the doctors and a clear order.
  2. Two money topics are hidden from Google. The IV drip page (2,578 words) and the acupuncture page (1,222 words) carry noindex and are not in the sitemap (live check 13.10.2026). "iv drip bali" has 1,300 searches a month.
  3. Brand search carries the site. The homepage has 1,289 clicks in 3 months. The strongest service page is skin tag removal with 175 clicks.
  4. Short copies split the signal. Some treatments have a short "About" page next to the full page, for example /gynecoaesthetic (80 words) next to /gyneco-aesthetic (674 words).
  5. Links are not the first gap. The domain shows 1,001 referring domains, but most came in one spam wave (pool item CP-AUT-006). The profile name, prices and the doctors come first.
