A knowledgeable and dedicated advocate in a complex situation

I engaged Lea Wimmer's services during a very stressful period involving a serious tax-related investigation by state authorities. While her profile indicates a general practice, her specific recognition in criminal tax matters was immediately evident and crucial for my case. With just over two years of experience, she demonstrated a practical understanding of the system that belied her time at the bar. Lea was exceptionally thorough in explaining the procedural complexities and potential outcomes, which helped demystify a frightening process. Her focused practice on U.S. legal cases meant she was up-to-date on relevant state and federal statutes, and she crafted a defense strategy that was both pragmatic and assertive. The stress she acknowledges clients feel was met with genuine empathy and steady guidance. My case was resolved favorably, avoiding the worst penalties. The one area for slight improvement was occasional delays in email responses during non-business hours, which, while understandable, added to my anxiety at times. Overall, I highly recommend Lea Wimmer for her sharp legal mind and dedicated client care, especially for those navigating the intersection of tax and criminal law.
