# 741 Marketing Playbook — 2026-10-25

Produced by the Marketing Grill, run live with Martin. This is a plan only. It does not launch ads, send outreach, spend money, or change a live site — each of those stays its own per-change go, outside this document.

## 1. What to do: keep the current channel, fix its execution

LinkedIn warm and cold outreach (Martin's own seat, German market) stays 741's primary marketing channel. This matches the knowledge base's own Core Four priority for a B2B agency: direct outreach and paid acquisition come first, because they cost minimal capital and give immediate, predictable cash flow; organic content needs 12-18 months to become a real lead engine on its own. (Source: `playbook-notes-channel-selection.md` §1, from "The Content Marketing Playbook" notebook.)

Organic content (`personal-posts`, 4 posts shipped as of 24.10.) stays the nurture and legitimacy layer: when a prospect gets outreach, they check the founder's profile. Its job is to pass that check, not to generate leads on its own. (Same source.)

The problem is not the channel. Martin's own 21.10. diagnosis (`revenue-model-100k.md`) found the LinkedIn funnel broke: fewer prospects are connecting, and Martin's own reply speed to inbound requests has been inconsistent. Fixing this comes before anything else.

## 2. Where to reach people: the one seat, not a new channel yet

Depth before width (knowledge base: "Depth-then-Width — maximize profitability and scale on one platform completely before expanding to secondary platforms"). The existing LinkedIn seat gets fixed and proven before a second seat is added, and before any new Lead Getter (an affiliate/partner stream, or the unlaunched YouTube channel for the creative-agency-owner avatar) opens.

## 3. How to focus: this week's commitment

- Same-day reply rule on every inbound video request, starting now.
- Diagnose this week whether the drop in connects is a LinkedIn account restriction or an outreach-tool problem.
- Nothing else pauses. The giveaway page, the YouTube channel groundwork, and the agency-delivery/retainer pivots keep running as they are — only the LinkedIn-seat fix gets priority attention right now.

## 4. How it's structured

- Owner: Martin. It is his own seat and his own relationships with the people requesting videos.
- Tracking: a daily card in the existing Morning Grill / Command Center — same-day reply status and the restriction-vs-tool diagnosis show up there, not in a new separate tracker.
- Definition of done for this week: the restriction-vs-tool question is answered, and every inbound video request from today onward gets a same-day reply.
