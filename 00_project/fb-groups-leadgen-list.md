## How to expand this list yourself

Facebook's own group search returns more than public web search. Paste these into the FB search
bar (logged in) and sort/filter by "Groups":

```
<city or country> + one of: business owners / entrepreneurs / small business / startups /
freelance jobs / gigs / expats
```

Or use Google directly (returns joinable URLs):

```
site:facebook.com/groups <market> small business owners OR entrepreneurs OR freelance jobs
```
