## How to send it (easiest path)
1. Open the CSV ([smartlead-warm-leads-phase1.csv](smartlead-warm-leads-phase1.csv)) in Gmail / Sheets.
2. Send **1:1 from your normal inbox**, replacing `{{Business}}` each time (2 min each, 15 total = under an hour).
3. Space them over 2–3 days so it stays human, not batch.
4. Log replies right back in the CSV `Status` column.
