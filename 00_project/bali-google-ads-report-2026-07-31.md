## What it means
1. **Budget is not the constraint — search volume is.** ~€3 spent over 3 serving days against €12/day capacity. There simply isn't much English search demand for these terms in the Bali geo per day.
2. **CTR is healthy (4%)** at this micro-scale; **Local SEO (5.1%) out-pulls Web Design (2.8%)**.
3. **0 conversions is meaningless at 3 clicks** — but verify the conversion tag (AW-564129015) is firing; Google still nudges "fix tracking."
4. **Demand skews to "agency / marketing / social media agency"**, broader than "local seo"/"web design" — the market frames it as *digital marketing agency*, not *local SEO*.
5. **Some traffic is Indonesian-language / local** ("jasa seo bali") — outside the expat/English target.

## Recommended actions
- **Let it run ~2 weeks** before judging — 3 clicks is noise.
- **Broaden keywords** toward how the market actually searches: `digital marketing agency bali`, `marketing agency bali`, `social media agency bali`, `agency bali` (already converting to clicks).
- **Add negatives** for local/Indonesian intent: `jasa`, and non-target service terms as they appear.
- **Add image assets** to the RSAs (Google flags this — improves eligibility/CTR).
- **Rename "Ad group 1" → "Local SEO"** for clarity.
- **Volume ceiling is structural** for search in this small market → stand up the drafted **retargeting / Demand Gen** layer (DACH + International) for reach beyond the thin search demand. See `741studio-retargeting-plan.md`.
