# Blog rules

No blog workflow existed before 2026-10-11. These rules are new (draft v1, first use with the Stürzl blog pool items). Sources: pool tag `blog-faq`, SEO rules, legal rules, memory `internal-linking`, `behandler-seite` (E-E-A-T).

- B1 **Topics.** A topic list per client comes from pool items tagged `blog-faq` and from PAA and autocomplete. The agent proposes a ranked list (topic, reader question, keyword, volume with date, money page to link, legal flag). Martin approves the list. Each approved topic is one run.
- B2 **Intent.** One post answers one question. The H1 is the question or a clear statement with the keyword. The first 2 to 4 sentences answer it (answer block).
- B3 **Structure.** Answer first, then depth: the short answer, what the law or method says (with source), what it means for the reader, what to do next, FAQ of 3 or more real PAA, CTA. 900 to 1.800 words typical. The real range comes from the light A0 benchmark (70 % of the median is the floor).
- B4 **Author.** The practitioner or lawyer, first person, with name, proven credentials and a link to the person page. The client reviews every post before publish. Without that review the status stays `needs_client`.
- B5 **Dates.** Visible "veröffentlicht am" and "zuletzt geprüft am". Update cadence: law and health posts are checked every 12 months, or when a law or guideline changes. The post carries a `review_due` date in the frontmatter.
- B6 **Sources.** Every legal or medical statement names its source (law paragraph, guideline, ruling) in the text or in a source line. Sources are read on the run date. No source, no statement.
- B7 **Legal mode.** HWG or BRAO rules apply in full. A blog post is not general advice: it carries a short note ("allgemeine Information, ersetzt keine Beratung im Einzelfall") and no case promises. Law posts never give a case assessment.
- B8 **Links.** Link to the money page in the first third and at the end. Link to 2 sibling posts or pages. Anchor text names the topic.
- B9 **CTA.** One CTA, soft, matching the reader's stage: a calm first contact, not a sales push. The reassurance line is the same as on the money page.
- B10 **Cannibalisation.** One keyword, one page. If a money page already targets the keyword the post takes the question form of a related keyword.
- B11 **Slop.** Blog posts are where AI sound is most likely. The gate is the same (5/5 after cleanse, 2 rounds). A post that cannot hold one concrete detail from the client per section is not written.
