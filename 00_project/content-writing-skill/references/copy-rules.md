# Copy rules (merged headline and body rules)

Sources: Martin's headline prompts (`00_project/prompts/direct-response-copy-prompts.md`, called DR below) and the CRO library rules R0, R1, R2, R3, R4, R6, R7, L8 (`00_project/knowledge/cro-library/_derived/rules.md`). One rule page replaces both. Never name the sources in client material. Call the method "741 Content-Raster".

**Contents** · 1 Order of work · 2 Hero and headlines · 3 Sub-headline and pain · 4 Body copy · 5 Section headings · 6 Value props · 7 CTA · 8 Proof in copy · 9 Objections and FAQ · 10 Forbidden · 11 Conflicts resolved

## 1. Order of work

- C1.1 IF you write a page THEN write the copy first and let the design follow. The outline gives each section ONE job (what the reader should feel, think, do). (CRO R0.2, R0.3)
- C1.2 IF you need words THEN take them from Google reviews, first-call questions and the client's own text. Customers already wrote the copy. (CRO R0.4)
- C1.3 IF the page is an entry page from search or Maps THEN treat it like a landing page and spend most of the effort on the first screen. (CRO R0.1, R0.5)
- C1.4 IF the client has own phrases or method names THEN weave them in, also on new pages. (voice sheet)

## 2. Hero and headlines

- C2.1 IF you write the hero headline THEN combine three elements: the outcome the reader wants and its emotional payoff, a concrete service fact, and a reason to believe it (mechanism or proof). Never "who we are". (DR 1, CRO R2.1)
- C2.2 IF the formula asks for a time frame THEN use it only for service facts: reply time, appointment within X days. Never a healing time or a result time. (CRO R2.2, HWG)
- C2.3 IF the headline is clever, a question, wordplay or a buzzword THEN rewrite it clear and definite. (DR 1, CRO R2.3)
- C2.4 IF the page must rank THEN the H1 holds the primary keyword (and the city for local pages). The visible benefit line may sit beside it in a larger style. (CRO R1.8)
- C2.5 IF you need headline angles THEN ask "so that" until you reach the deepest benefit that is allowed (feel safe, understand the process, keep control). (CRO R2.5)
- C2.6 IF the legal mode is HWG or BRAO THEN the outcome is an experience or a service fact, never a result: "verstehen, was als Nächstes passiert", not "Straffreiheit".
- C2.7 IF you give headline variants THEN give up to 3 per section, mark one as recommended, and say why in one line. Mix the formats: statement, pain callout with reframe, warning with a way out. Use questions in at most 2 of 5. (DR 4)
- C2.8 No dashes (— and –) in any copy. Use comma, period, "und" or "&". (DR global rule)

## 3. Sub-headline and pain

- C3.1 IF you write the sub-headline THEN name the pain or situation first, then how you help, with 3 or 4 specifics. 30 to 40 words at most. (DR 2, CRO R2.4)
- C3.2 IF you write a pain block THEN use Problem, Agitate, Solve in this order. Open with 3 recognisable situations, say them with empathy, show the cost of waiting with real facts, then take away the blocking belief and show the way out. (DR 3)
- C3.3 IF you list pains THEN attach the way out in the same section. Situations the reader recognises, never diagnoses or cure claims. (CRO R6.1, R6.2)
- C3.4 IF fear is the topic THEN name the fear calmly and answer it. Never use the fear as a lever (no "Albtraum", "Ruin", "Haft droht"). (L8)

## 4. Body copy

- C4.1 IF a block is for scanning THEN sentences have 15 words or fewer, paragraphs have 2 sentences or fewer, key phrases are bold, and a break comes every 2 to 3 lines. (DR 3, CRO R2.9)
- C4.2 IF you can state a real number THEN use it, with its source in the claim list. Numbers read as objective. (CRO R2.10)
- C4.3 IF the text has jargon THEN use the reader's word. Explain a legal or medical term once, in one sentence, at first use.
- C4.4 IF a block lists benefits THEN use the check-list or card element, not a long sentence. (D2 of the money-page standard)
- C4.5 IF the copy sounds like AI or marketing THEN rewrite it in the words clients use. (CRO R2.11) The slop gate checks this (see de-tells.md).
- C4.6 IF you vary rhythm THEN follow a long sentence with a short one. Keep one concrete detail per section: a name, a place, a step, a number.

## 5. Section headings

- C5.1 IF a heading is a label (Leistungen, Über uns, Bewertungen, FAQ) THEN rewrite it so the heading carries the claim, unless the label is the keyword the section must rank for (then keep it and add the claim in the first line). (CRO R2.6)
- C5.2 IF someone reads only the headings THEN the page still sells. Write headings as benefits that stay true and checkable. (CRO R2.7)

## 6. Value props

- C6.1 IF you list value props THEN find the ones that are really unique (up to 8). A claim any competitor could make is cut. If only 5 are real, deliver 5 and say why. (DR 5)
- C6.2 IF you write a value-prop heading THEN use: action or experience verb + specific feature + emotional benefit, 12 to 18 words, with a concrete fact where one exists. Transformation, not transaction. (DR 6) HWG and BRAO filter applies.
- C6.3 IF you pair feature and benefit THEN put the benefit in the heading and the feature in the line below. The top 3 or 4 get their own section. (CRO R2.12)

## 7. CTA

- C7.1 IF you set the primary CTA THEN there is one clear action per screen, and the button text names the next step and its value ("Vertraulich anfragen"), not "Kontakt" or "Absenden". (DR 7, CRO R3.1, R3.2)
- C7.2 IF the CTA feels like a push THEN soften it ("Kurz schreiben", "Erstgespräch anfragen"). Address the worry: what do I get, what happens next, no pressure. (DR 7, CRO R3.3)
- C7.3 IF you place a CTA THEN a reassurance line sits directly under it: reply time, no obligation, confidentiality, duration of the first contact. (CRO R3.7)
- C7.4 IF the page is long THEN repeat the same CTA at least every 2 sections and in the closer, with the same reassurance. (CRO R3.4, R3.8)
- C7.5 IF the channel is not WhatsApp THEN the same rules apply to phone, form or booking. The channel comes from the brief. Law and health clients may exclude WhatsApp (§ 203 StGB, DSGVO).
- C7.6 IF the first step is a calendar booking THEN offer a lighter first step (message, short form). (CRO R3.10)
- C7.7 IF you want urgency THEN use calm real facts ("Antwort am selben Werktag" only if true). No timers, no scarcity. (CRO R7.5)

## 8. Proof in copy

- C8.1 IF proof exists THEN the first screen holds two forms of it, for example a rating with its count and a credential. Each number is in the claim list. (CRO R4.1)
- C8.2 IF you head a proof section THEN write the benefit, never only "Bewertungen". (CRO R4.8)
- C8.3 IF you quote a review THEN do not change its text. Use experience content (feeling understood, time taken, clear explanation). Never result claims. (CRO R4.3, R4.7)
- C8.4 IF a claim appears THEN place matching proof next to it (CRO R4.11). IF there is no proof THEN weaken the claim or cut it.
- C8.5 IF the practitioner or lawyer is the product THEN a person block with photo, name, qualification (proven) and one own sentence appears early. (CRO R4.14)

## 9. Objections and FAQ

- C9.1 IF you write the FAQ THEN answer the real objections first: price, cost basis, duration, confidentiality, preparation, what happens at the first contact. Price first when the client can state it. (CRO R6.4)
- C9.2 IF the client already has FAQ text THEN keep it, adjust the address form and the legal wording, and only ADD real PAA questions. Never replace it. (money-page standard I)
- C9.3 IF the reader doubts "Will this help me?" or "Am I in good hands?" THEN answer that deeper doubt too, with process and qualification, never with a result. (CRO R6.6)

## 10. Forbidden

Dashes. Healing and result promises. Superlatives and "Experte", "Spezialist", "führend", "beste" unless the legal rule page allows it with proof. Fear wording. Invented numbers, quotes, awards, names. Stock phrases from de-tells.md. Method sources named in client text. Gedanken wie "wir sind leidenschaftlich" without a fact behind them.

## 11. Conflicts resolved

| Conflict | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| DR allows time frames in the hero formula. CRO R2.2 forbids healing times. | Time frames only for service facts. |
| CRO R2.8 says "wir" becomes "du". Law and many practices use "Sie" and "wir". | The voice sheet decides the address form and the speaker. The principle stays: write for the reader and what the reader gets. |
| DR formula puts a number in brackets as proof, for example "(95 % approval)". | Proof in the formula must be in the claim list. Without proof, drop the bracket. |
| CRO R1.8 says keyword small in the H1, big benefit line visible. | Allowed for homepages and money pages. For blog posts the H1 is the keyword-led question or statement itself. |
| DR asks for 10 hero variants. | Outline shows up to 3 per section (workflow decision). More only on Martin's request. |
