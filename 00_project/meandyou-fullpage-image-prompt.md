## PROMPT

Design a full-page desktop website mockup (one tall, continuous page, 1440 px wide, flat front view, no browser frame, no device, no perspective) for **Michael Ehlers / me&you Communication**, a premium German rhetoric trainer who runs 3-day seminars in Mallorca, Barcelona, Dubai, Cyprus, Frankfurt and Berlin.

The attached PDF is the design source of truth. Follow its colours, typography, photography and layout rules. Use the attached logo and photos as they are. Do not redraw the logo. Do not invent a different face for Michael.

### Design direction

- **Feel:** quiet luxury, editorial, warm, adult. Confidence through restraint. Rare, not loud. Think high-end retreat and magazine layout, not coaching funnel.
- **Colours:** cream white #F2EDE4 (main background), light grey #E7E8E8 (alternate bands), sand #D8CCB7 (warm accents), dove blue #7E99B6 (accent, buttons), dark navy #2B3C52 (dark bands, headlines), warm anthracite #47403A (body text). No pure black, no pure white, no other colours.
- **Type:** Libre Baskerville (serif) for all headlines and quotes. Inter (sans) for body, labels, buttons, navigation. Large calm headlines, small uppercase Inter overlines with wide letter spacing.
- **Layout:** generous whitespace, one idea per screen, soft rounded corners, no hard divider lines. Alternate cream, light grey and one or two dark navy bands for rhythm. Mix full-bleed photo sections, split sections (text left, image right, then mirrored) and card grids. Never a plain text column.
- **Photography:** real Michael in action, natural daylight, light film grain, never stiff studio or stock look. Locations at golden hour with long shadows, landscape before people, small groups only. Never: hotel ballroom, stage congress, lanyards, crowds.
- **CTA:** one button style, dove blue pill with white Inter text. Only one CTA visible per screen. No urgency elements, no countdowns, no badges that shout.
- **Text rendering:** all text is German. Render every headline and button exactly as written below, with correct umlauts (ä ö ü ß). Body copy may be set as neat, legible paragraphs.

### Avoid

Funnel or sales-page look, gradients, neon, glassmorphism, drop-shadow-heavy cards, emoji, stock handshake photos, stage and conference imagery, prices, red or orange urgency colours, crowded sections, more than one CTA per screen, placeholder lorem ipsum, misspelled German.
