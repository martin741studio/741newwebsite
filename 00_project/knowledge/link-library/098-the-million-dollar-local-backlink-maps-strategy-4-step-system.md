Summary (own words, not a transcript copy — see the house copyright rule):

A 4-step local-backlink system for local/home-service businesses, framed around Google Maps ranking rather than organic rank.

**Precondition:** only invest in local backlinks once review velocity on the GBP and on-page keyword coverage are already solid. Local backlinks are the next lever after those two, not a first move.

**Step 1 — find the right competitor.** Don't default to the nearest business. If local penetration in the map grid is weak for the obvious neighbor, expand the search across the city/area to find whoever is actually dominating the grid, then study their links instead.

**Step 2 — mine the data.** Pull the actual website linked from the competitor's Google Business Profile — not just any domain found by name — and check for redirects (an acquired/merged business's domain may redirect to the parent's). Export all referring domains (Ahrefs in the source, DataForSEO for us), then run the export through an LLM with a structured prompt to separate genuinely local, geographically-relevant sources from generic directories and national sites with local-sounding pages.

**Step 3 — rank the opportunities, fixed priority order (not a blended score):**
1. Chambers of commerce / long-standing comprehensive local business organizations — ranked first even though they are not niche-specific, because longevity and traffic give them outsized local weight.
2. Niche-specific directories and trade/industry organizations — rarer, worth hunting for, but second.
3. Organizations physically close to the business's own GBP address — proximity itself is a signal, because the map pack algorithm is proximity-based. A Rotary Club two blocks away can matter more than a bigger, farther organization.
4. Everything else locally relevant — sports clubs, charities, local events.

**Step 4 — outreach.** Ask what it costs to become a member or sponsor (quoted range: roughly $100–250 for a standard sponsor/directory listing, up to ~$500 for a stronger one). For publications, pitch real expertise or a contribution to a specific upcoming story rather than a cold link ask.

**ROI framing (for client reporting, not for discovery):** translate a map-grid coverage increase (e.g. 30 %→40 % top-3 coverage) into extra calls, extra closed jobs at the client's close rate, and customer lifetime value, to size whether the backlink spend is justified. Lifetime revenue and lifetime profit are different figures — profit has to cover the investment, not revenue.

**Reusable asset:** the video links a structured Claude/GPT prompt for classifying a raw referring-domains export into local/regional/national/affiliated/unverified buckets with a fixed, evidence-gated priority ranking and proximity tiebreakers.
