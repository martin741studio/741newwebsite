So the first step is to find content in your industry that's already done well in terms of attracting links, social shares, comments, and traffic. One of the easiest ways to do this is just to search for competitive keywords in Google. Because if you find a piece of content that's ranking well in Google, you better believe that there are a lot of links pointing to it. So how can you make something heads and shoulders above what's already out there? Well, there are three strategies that work really well. The first is to make your content more thorough.
