# The Best Landing Page Structure for 2026

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vwhR2M0SQtI

Key topics: Landing page structure, Above the fold, Social proof strategies, Copywriting conversion tactics, Value proposition design

## Source guide (NotebookLM)

Crafting a high-converting landing page requires a strategic layout that prioritizes clear, benefit-driven messaging over clever or overly minimalist design. The above-the-fold section serves as the most critical real estate, demanding an immediate value proposition, multiple forms of social proof, and elements that minimize consumer hesitation. Moving further down, pages should strategically incorporate pain-point addresses, detailed value props, and transparent processes instead of relying on ineffective tactics like hidden testimonial carousels. Ultimately, embracing comprehensive, longer page structures rather than brief layouts significantly boosts overall conversion rates by thoroughly educating and building trust with the audience.
