## Source guide (NotebookLM)

This text outlines a comprehensive five-step framework designed to eliminate cognitive friction—the mental effort required by users to navigate and shop on a website—in order to instantly boost e-commerce conversion rates. The author explains that because human brains possess a limited threshold for processing information and generally scan rather than read, websites must replace cluttered layouts, excessive choices, and dense paragraphs with cognitive ease. To achieve this, the guide advises simplifying navigation menus, leveraging visual elements like infographics and icons instead of plain text, and crafting benefit-driven headlines that immediately answer why a shopper should care. Furthermore, it emphasizes the importance of establishing a clear visual hierarchy to naturally guide user attention and streamlining the product selection process through helpful badges, transparent bundle savings, and immediate cart feedback. Ultimately, the purpose of the source is to provide actionable optimization strategies backed by real-world testing data that prevent mental overwhelm, enhance user experience, and maximize revenue.
