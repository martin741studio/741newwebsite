# The Easiest CRO Tweaks To Boost Ecommerce Sales

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7cQOtkbZF7c

Key topics: Paradox of choice, Intuitive navigation, Scannable content, Above the fold, High converting copy

## Source guide (NotebookLM)

Maximizing online revenue does not require expensive advertising or complete digital overhauls, but rather relies on five foundational website adjustments designed to improve conversion rates efficiently. First, implementing the paradox of choice principle prevents user overwhelm by eliminating clutter and unnecessary page elements, thereby simplifying the decision-making process. Second, enhancing site navigation with visual icons and thumbnails dramatically improves user engagement because the human brain processes images much faster than text. Third, restructuring content into scannable value propositions accommodates modern browsing habits, ensuring visitors can easily digest information without exceeding their cognitive threshold. Fourth, optimizing the above-the-fold area of product pages ensures that critical information remains immediately visible to the majority of users who never scroll further down. Finally, crafting compelling and benefit-driven copy clearly articulates a brand's unique value, transforming passive browsers into active buyers by directly answering what is in it for the customer.
