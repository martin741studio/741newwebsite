## Source guide (NotebookLM)

To maximize profitability without inflating advertising expenses, businesses should prioritize conversion rate optimization over simply driving more traffic, focusing the vast majority of their energy on the above-the-fold section of their landing pages. This strategy relies on continuously refining headlines and hero images to immediately showcase a compelling dream outcome, while actively increasing the perceived likelihood of success through visual social proof and risk-reduction badges. Furthermore, successful pages must clearly articulate how quickly users will achieve results while minimizing required effort and sacrifice. Ultimately, achieving sustainable, scalable growth requires a disciplined approach of running consistent weekly split tests to steadily compound conversion rates and outcompete rivals in paid attention auctions.
