# How to Create a High Converting Landing Page in 2025

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2bFkI0iTxzE

Key topics: Landing page design, Conversion rate optimization, Hero section setup, Customer pain points, Social proof strategies

## Source guide (NotebookLM)

Building a high-converting landing page requires a strategic blueprint designed to maximize customer engagement and turn traffic into revenue. The process begins with setting up proper design and building tools like Figma and Elementor, followed by deeply understanding your target audience, their daily pain points, and their dream outcomes. The most critical portion of the page is the above the fold hero section, which must instantly capture attention using a compelling headline, benefit-driven body text, stacked social proof, and a clear call to action. As visitors scroll further down, you should systematically introduce pain points, multiple value props, and differentiators that explain how your solution outshines the competition. Finally, incorporating how-it-all-works steps, FAQs, and a final urgency-driven recap section will successfully alleviate lingering doubts and drive conversions.
