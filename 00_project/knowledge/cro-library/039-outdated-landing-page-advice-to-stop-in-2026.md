## Source guide (NotebookLM)

The provided text debunks eight outdated landing page myths that frequently cause digital marketers and business owners to lose money. The author stresses that modern audiences suffer from severe skepticism and low attention spans, meaning that long landing pages with in-depth information are often necessary to build genuine trust, unlike the old advice of keeping pages as brief as possible. Furthermore, instead of relying on generic or fabricated praise, marketers must utilize verifiable social proof—such as videos, complete names, and customer photos—paired with clean, professional designs that complement persuasive copywriting. Rather than blindly copying competitors or assuming that launching a page completes the work, creators should rely on data-driven user research and continuous AB testing to optimize conversion rates over time. Finally, the source redefines landing pages to include every entry point on a website, warning that while reducing friction may boost raw lead numbers, strategically placed good friction is vital for filtering out unqualified buyers and protecting long-term revenue.
