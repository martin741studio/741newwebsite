# Alex Hormozi's Advice on Landing Pages

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CNRiNz0YYlM

Key topics: Conversion rate optimization, A/B testing, Above-the-fold optimization, Value equation, Landing page design

## Source guide (NotebookLM)

As advertising costs on major digital platforms steadily climb, mastering conversion rate optimization has become essential for modern businesses seeking to maximize their marketing returns rather than merely burning cash on raw traffic. By focusing improvement efforts above the fold—specifically targeting the initial headlines and supporting visuals—companies can effectively communicate their core value and quickly capture audience attention. Furthermore, successful landing page design must integrate Alex Hormozi's value equation framework by clearly highlighting the dream outcome, minimizing perceived risk, and actively reducing the time, effort, and sacrifice required from the consumer. Ultimately, maintaining a consistent routine of weekly split testing compounds small design adjustments into massive revenue growth while simultaneously generating valuable audience insights that strengthen every other area of the business.
