# How to create a killer landing page (to explode your business)

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fe32Q1It8X8

Key topics: Target audience clarity, Above the fold, Social proof sections, Value prop sections, Conversion action optimization

## Source guide (NotebookLM)

To build a high-converting landing page that successfully grows your business, you must first understand your target audience by analyzing their core motivations and recognizing the specific traffic context that brings them to your site. The most critical component of your design is the above-the-fold section, which demands the majority of your effort because a significant portion of visitors will never scroll further down; therefore, it must immediately feature a compelling, benefit-driven headline, an informative subheadline, earlier social proof, and a clear call-to-action backed by guarantees to reduce user friction. Beyond this top area, you should strategically scatter multiple value propositions and testimonials throughout the page using digestible formatting to continuously engage skimmers. Finally, you must streamline the final conversion process—whether through frictionless forms for B2B or seamless sliding carts for e-commerce—to eliminate user hesitation and maximize your overall sales potential.
