## Findings

- **IF work stops when you leave, THEN the task is not handed over yet; train until it runs alone.** — Src: 012, "when you disappear it stops working".
- IF you hand over a task, THEN you free time for the next priority, so plan that time now. — Src: 018, "if you replace what you were doing you will find something else".
- IF you need a commitment, THEN ask what it would take, not whether they can. — Src: 022, "ask what it would take".
- IF you define a task, THEN write its inputs, its outputs, and how to tell good output from bad. — Src: 016, "how do we know output is good versus bad".
- IF a workflow has many steps, THEN break it down until no step can be split further. — Src: 069, "until there's you can't reduce it anymore".
- IF you brief a team member or an AI agent, THEN describe observable actions, not traits like "charismatic". — Src: 069, "hey I need you to be more charismatic means nothing".
- IF you hand work to an AI agent, THEN train it one activity at a time. — Src: 069, "you have to train it the way you train a human".
- **IF you hand over a task, THEN apply document, demonstrate, duplicate: write the steps, show them, watch the run.** — Src: 019, "Write down the steps required to perform tasks".
- IF a team member misses a standard, THEN give feedback on each run until the standard sticks. — Src: 012, "you keep repeating that until eventually it sticks".
- IF you define success for a task, THEN say what good looks like before the first run. — Src: 012, "first you define what you know success looks like".
- IF a team member must learn a behavior, THEN role-play it and correct it live. — Src: 064, "saying it at them will do literally nothing".
- IF a new team member learns a proven process, THEN have them copy the owner's steps exactly first. — Src: 064, "they should just clone exactly what you're doing".
- IF a standard slips, THEN check whether the leader sets that standard too. — Src: 012, "they're not leading by example".
- IF you set team values, THEN you must model them most visibly yourself. — Src: 012, "the most concentrated version of what your values are".
- IF a role has many steps, THEN write a one-page checklist for that role. — Src: 019, "committing to a single-page advertising checklist".
- IF you pick a sales leader, THEN skip your best closer and choose the well-liked middle performer. — Src: 050, "look for the middle of the pack guys that everyone loves".
- IF you choose a leader, THEN choose the best human, not the best seller. — Src: 022, "prioritize the person who is the best human".
- IF you train leaders, THEN use shadowing and many hours, the way you train sales. — Src: 050, "you have to train it the way that you train sales".
- IF one leader manages more than about six team members, THEN that leader needs a full-time management role. — Src: 050, "after like sixish it's it's a full-time job".
- IF a meeting ends, THEN every action needs an owner and a due date before anyone leaves. — Src: 022, "finish every meeting with who what when".
- IF a team member works alone, THEN hold a short daily start plan and an end-of-day check. — Src: 064, "first thing in the morning what's your plan".
- IF you run a weekly one-to-one, THEN cover the person first, then short-term numbers, then long-term growth. — Src: 064, "we're focused on short-term stuff".
- IF requests reach you all day, THEN set response windows and accept that you cannot answer all. — Src: 022, "a message is an inbound bid for time".
- **IF several areas compete for priority, THEN name one constraint and fix it first.** — Src: 083, "I believe that there is one constraint".
- IF you work on two constraints at once, THEN expect both to finish later than one at a time. — Src: 083, "it ends up taking longer to do both than to do one".
- **IF you plan capacity, THEN multiply hours per client by the number of clients to get a hard cap.** — Src: 079, "10 hours per couple is the cost".
- IF you set a weekly cap, THEN base it on what you are willing to do. — Src: 079, "what are you willing to do".
- IF team members can reach you at any time, THEN give each one a fixed number of emergency calls. — Src: 079, "you've got four 911 calls or two".
- **IF output stalls, THEN check hiring and onboarding capacity before you change the strategy.** — Src: 019, "it was our capacity to execute it".
- IF hiring is the constraint, THEN interview candidates in groups to fill seats faster. — Src: 019, "we would interview candidates in groups".
- IF the constraint sits in your team, THEN spend your own time on it first. — Src: 064, "since it's the constraint it's a good use of your time".
- IF supply is full, THEN pick one lever: raise the price, change the delivery ratio, productize, or hire. — Src: 079, "when you are supply constrained you can either raise the price".
- IF a new team member joins, THEN plan a ramp period of about 30 days with lower output. — Src: 066, "he's got to get up to speed in 30 days".
- IF a side project hurts the core business, THEN stop the side project. — Src: 018, "the main business suffers and then you don't get the W".
- IF you hire a team member, THEN track the cost to acquire that team member against their lifetime gross profit. — Src: 099, "cost to acquire talent versus lifetime gross profit per employee".
- IF you cannot name the actions that raise revenue, THEN your team cannot know them either. — Src: 099, "your employees don't know what it is".
- IF new team members quit early, THEN track their drop-off at weeks one or two, day 30, day 90. — Src: 012, "big drop off in week one or two".
- IF a vendor runs a core step, THEN make sure you can run that step yourself. — Src: 012, "make sure that no vendor owns how my business works".
- IF a function is attract, convert, or deliver, THEN keep it in house, not with a vendor. — Src: 012, "the three elements of business that you do not outsource".

## What is marketing fluff in these sources

- Revenue goals such as "160 million" or "100 million" are sales hooks, not team rules.
- Stories about one person's output, such as a teen who posts many clips a day, show results, not methods.
- Free roadmaps, free tools, and booking calls are lead generation, not operations.
- Motivational lines, such as "pick the hard path", give no rule that a manager can test.
- Life-tip lists in 022, such as earplugs and handshakes, have no link to team management.

## Not transferable to 741 and why

- Hiring targets of 25 reps or more do not fit one owner, three team members, and AI agents.
- Crew-based models, such as solar sales or fence installs, use sales floors that 741 does not run.
- Stage roadmaps for growth from 1 to 500 people fit a company far larger than 741 today.
