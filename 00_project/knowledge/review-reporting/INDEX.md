## Key extracts (own words)

### A. What to report (market practice)
- Standard agency report items: total review count, average rating, new reviews per period, recency, reply rate and reply speed (S16, S18, S19).
- Known tools measure "velocity" as average reviews per month over the last 90 days (S13). Local Falcon gives full velocity score at 10 per month and full count score at 50 total. This is a tool scale, not a Google rule.
- Recency matters to buyers: 74% of a US panel look only at reviews from the last 3 months (S17). There is no DACH study of the same quality in this search.
- Reply speed matters: 19% expect a same-day reply, 81% expect one within a week (S17).
- Competitor comparison is a normal part of the report. Local Falcon includes 3 competitors (S13). BrightLocal tracks up to 4 competitors for rankings (S15).
- Opinion sources warn against sudden spikes from bulk requests (S20). A steady monthly flow looks more natural.

### B. Data sources and limits
- **Own profile, official:** GBP API v4 reviews endpoints (S01, S02). They give every review with its date, its star value and the reply data. That is enough for count, rating, new per month, reply rate and reply time.
- **Access:** apply with a form. The applicant manages a verified profile that is active for 60+ days, and has a website (S05). A forum post reports more than 10 business days of waiting (S06).
- **Quota:** 300 queries per minute default (S04). A daily pull for a small client base uses a very small part of this.
- **2026 changes:** reply state (2026-04-01), media items (2026-04-20), policy-violation status of replies (2026-07-01), reply URL (2026-07-24) (S03). The endpoint is maintained.
- **Competitors, official:** the Places API gives userRatingCount and rating for any place (S09). But Places content must not be stored, except place IDs (S08, S30). A month-by-month competitor history from Places data therefore conflicts with the terms.
- **Competitors, third party:** DataForSEO Business Info costs $0.0015 to $0.0054 per profile (S11). Ten clients × 3 competitors × 4 weekly pulls = 120 profiles per month. That is below $1 per month at the listed prices. Check DataForSEO terms on storage before the build.
- **Detailed analysis:** Local Falcon Reviews Analysis Report, $19 per location with 3 competitors (S13). Useful once per quarter, not weekly.
- **All-in-one tool:** BrightLocal Grow, $65 per month for 1 location (monthly billing), more per extra location (S15). Expensive per client when the portal already exists.

### C. Google policy (all clients)
- Not allowed: incentives for a review, edit or removal. Not allowed: discouraging negative reviews or asking only happy customers (review gating) (S07).
- Not allowed: pressure to review on the premises, or requests for specific content (S07).
- Important for the 2× goal: Google lists "merchants requesting that staff solicit a certain number of reviews" as a prohibited case (S07). The 2× goal can be a business target. It must not become a staff quota per person or per week.
- A filter step ("rate us 0–10 first, then go to Google only if happy") is review gating. Some tools use an NPS step first (S16). We must not build or recommend a filter of this type.

### D. German law firms (STÜRZL)
- Confidentiality covers the mandate itself (S21). A reply must never confirm that the reviewer was a client, and never name case facts.
- § 6 BORA, new version since 2025-12-01: advertising is allowed unless it is not factual, unfair or misleading. Advertising with mandates or clients needs express consent (S22, S23).
- A general, factual request for a review is allowed. A request by e-mail is advertising and needs prior consent (S25, S26). In-person ask or a card with QR code at the end of the mandate is the safe route.
- Use of client reviews on the firm's own website = advertising with clients → express consent of that client (S22).
- Critical reviews are mostly protected as opinion (S24). The report must show them openly.

### E. Health practices (HWG, confidentiality)
- The fact that a person is a patient is confidential (§ 203 StGB). It is also health data (Art. 9 DSGVO). E-mail or SMS requests need explicit consent (S27).
- Ask every patient the same way, for example a QR card at reception. No gifts (§ 7 HWG). No selection or invented reviews (§ 11 Abs. 1 Nr. 11 HWG) (S27).
- Replies: thank neutrally. Never confirm treatment, diagnosis, dates or billing (S27).
- Reviews with healing claims: do not quote them in ads or on the website (S28, S29).

### F. DSGVO points for 741
- Review data includes reviewer names. They are personal data. Reports need only numbers. Show no reviewer names in client reports (data minimisation).
- 741 processes the client's profile data for the client → data processing agreement (AVV) with each client.
- GBP API access runs through a manager role the client grants. Places data must not be stored (S08).
