# L12 BGB § 631 Vertragstypische Pflichten beim Werkvertrag
Source: official-docs/law-bgb-631.md, LAW, gesetze-im-internet.de, fetched 2026-10-12, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__631.html
Reliability: High (statute text). Background only, not legal advice.

## Practice from this source
- In a contract for work (Werkvertrag) the contractor owes the promised result (Werk). The customer owes the fee.
- The result can also be a success brought about by work or service.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- E: If the contract promises a defined result (for example "build 10 pages"), Werkvertrag rules (§§ 642, 643, 648) may apply instead of § 615.

## Limits
- Many agency contracts mix both types. Classification needs legal review.
