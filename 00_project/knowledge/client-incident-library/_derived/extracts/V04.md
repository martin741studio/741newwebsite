# V04 How to Apologize
Source: videos/V04-how-to-apologize.md, VIDEO, uploaded 2026-07-22, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=grihdl-UUmc
Speaker/channel: Don Crawley (The Compassionate Geek) (internal only; never name in client material)
Reliability: Short video by a customer-service trainer for IT teams. He sells online training (bias: course sales). The content is short but exact. It fits the part of our case where the agency must admit a real mistake.

## Practice from this source
- Do not use a vague corporate apology such as "We apologize for any inconvenience." It does not say what happened, who is responsible, or what changes.
- Step 1: say exactly what happened, in the first person. Example: "I installed an update that caused the outage." He says ownership builds credibility.
- Step 2: say what the problem caused for the other person. The apology is about them, not about you.
- Step 3: say how you fix the problem now and what you change so it does not happen again. Customers want to know there is a plan.
- He calls a sincere apology "the first step in rebuilding confidence and trust."

## Use for client-access-lockout
- F Communication with a mistrustful client: use the three steps (what happened, impact on her, fix and prevention) for each real mistake. Example: a missing report or an unclear explanation.
- H Retention vs clean separation: a specific apology for real mistakes makes our calm correction of the false claims more credible.

## Limits
- The video does not say how to handle claims that are false. Use it only for mistakes that we checked and confirmed.
- It does not cover legal risk. In a contract dispute, word the apology so it states facts and does not admit things that are not true.
