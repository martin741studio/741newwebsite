# L07 BGB § 611 Vertragstypische Pflichten beim Dienstvertrag
Source: official-docs/law-bgb-611.md, LAW, gesetze-im-internet.de, fetched 2026-10-12, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__611.html
Reliability: High (statute text). Background only, not legal advice.

## Practice from this source
- In a service contract (Dienstvertrag) the provider owes the promised services. The other party owes the agreed fee.
- Services "of any kind" can be the subject.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- E: A monthly SEO program that promises activities, not a ranking result, looks like a Dienstvertrag. Then §§ 615, 621, 626, 627 matter. Check the contract wording.

## Limits
- The Dienstvertrag vs Werkvertrag line depends on the contract and case law. See articles A22, A23 and A25.
