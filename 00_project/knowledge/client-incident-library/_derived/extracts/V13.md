# V13 Google Can Lose Faith In Your Website (Here's How To Get It Back)
Source: videos/V13-google-can-lose-faith-in-your-website-here-s-how-to-get-it-b.md, VIDEO, uploaded 2026-09-13, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4cB34UticI
Speaker/channel: Edward Sturm (internal only; never name in client material)
Reliability: SEO podcaster who quotes Google's John Mueller and two recovery anecdotes from X; sells an SEO course (bias); about low-quality own content, not about external spam links, so only partly close to our case.

## Practice from this source
- John Mueller warned about programmatic SEO: many generated pages with little user value.
- Mueller: such sites often become "spam, borderline spam, or lowquality."
- Mueller: Google's systems may have "lost faith in your site" because of the old pages.
- Mueller: fixing this "tends to take time and significant effort to show the value."
- The trust loss comes from the site's own content. The source does not say external links cause it.
- The speaker explains low quality with click-through rate and "pogo sticking" (users return to results). This is his interpretation, not a Google statement.
- Anecdote from X: a company deleted about 200 thin AI pages. Its Google traffic later rose from about 1,000 to about 4,000 visits per month.
- Their rule: delete thin pages first, keep the few worth keeping, write new pages from real data and real user language.
- Their rule: a human reviews and edits every page before it goes live.
- Anecdote: a site hit by the December 2025 spam update recovered about 5 months after fixes started.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- F Communication with a mistrustful client: the 5-month example and Mueller's "time and significant effort" help to set honest recovery timelines.
- A Verification of claims: Google's trust problem comes from low-value own pages. This helps to separate a real quality issue from a false "spam link" story.
- E Contract and pausing work: recovery needs steady work over months. Blocked access stops that work and delays any recovery.

## Limits
- No content on backlinks, disavow, or negative SEO.
- Pruning anecdotes are from social media posts, not controlled tests.
- Behavioral signals (CTR, pogo sticking) are the speaker's opinion.
