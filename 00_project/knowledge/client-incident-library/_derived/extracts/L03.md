# L03 BGB § 294 Tatsächliches Angebot
Source: official-docs/law-bgb-294.md, LAW, gesetze-im-internet.de, fetched 2026-10-12, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__294.html
Reliability: High (statute text). Background only, not legal advice.

## Practice from this source
- As a rule, the performance must be actually offered (tatsächlich angeboten) as it is owed.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- E: The general rule asks for an actual offer. § 295 (L04) gives the exception that fits a blocked-access case.
