# A02 88% of Small Business Websites Have Backlinks From Likely Spam Pages
Source: articles/A02-suffdigital-spammy-backlinks-88-percent-small-business.md, ARTICLE (data study), published 2026-09-24, Matt Suffoletto / Suff Digital, https://www.suffdigital.com/resources/data-studies/spammy-backlinks-small-business
Reliability: Medium. Small agency data study (249 US small business sites, third-party spam score). Saved as a summary because the page is JavaScript-rendered.

## Practice from this source
- 88% of small business sites had at least one backlink from a likely spam page.
- On average about 25% of a small business site's backlinks were flagged as spammy.
- One spam domain linked to 204 of the 249 sites. Eight more linked to 185 to 203 sites each.
- Spam links are therefore normal background noise. They are not a sign that a specific agency built them.
- The study advises: do not rush to disavow. Keep disavow for bought links, manual penalties, or sudden floods from one domain.
- Bare URL and "website" anchors are normal.
- Review anchors and spam share once or twice a year. Watch for gambling, pharmacy or foreign-language anchors.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- A/G: Put the client's "spam domain list" into context: most small business sites have such links, from networks that hit hundreds of sites at once.
- F: Use one simple number in plain words ("most small business websites have links like these") instead of a long technical lecture.

## Limits
- US sample, third-party spam score, not Google's judgement. Summary only, not the full text.
