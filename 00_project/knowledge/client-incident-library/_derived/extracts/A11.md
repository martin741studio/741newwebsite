# A11 How to Give and Request Access to Google Business Profile
Source: articles/A11-leadsie-gbp-access.md, ARTICLE, published 2026-10-02 (JSON-LD says 2026-10-07), Sarah Wisbey / Leadsie, https://www.leadsie.com/blog/how-to-give-and-request-access-to-a-google-business-profile
Reliability: Medium. Vendor of an access-request tool. Step-by-step how-to that matches Google's flow.

## Practice from this source
- Business owners should give access without sharing passwords, through People and access.
- "We recommend giving agencies and freelancers 'Manager access'."
- Only the profile owner can grant access.
- Organization accounts are invited by name or group ID, not by email alone.
- An agency can also request access through business.google.com/create > Request Access. The owner then approves.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- B: Use these exact steps when you send the client a re-invitation guide or a handover guide.
- H: For handover, the client removes the agency's Manager role from the same screen.
