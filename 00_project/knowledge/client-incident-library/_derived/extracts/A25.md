# A25 Agenturvertrag kündigen: Was Unternehmen vorher prüfen sollten
Source: articles/A25-avervo-agenturvertrag-kuendigen-pruefen.md, ARTICLE, published 2026-08-07, AVERVO (independent agency-review service), https://avervo.de/fachwissen/agenturvertrag-kuendigen/
Reliability: Consultancy trade article, not a law firm, AI-assisted per the page. Fresh (2026-08-07). Client-side view of how to leave an agency. Useful for evidence and separation. Background, not legal advice.

## Practice from this source
- The article tells clients to separate three levels: agreed work, delivered work, and result.
- An SEO agency can meet its duties even when enquiries stay low. A weak result is not the same as poor work.
- A disappointing cooperation is not automatically a legal ground for immediate termination.
- Clients should document problems: agreed work, delivery, dates, errors, feedback, agency reaction.
- Clients should give the agency a chance to fix issues: what, who, by when, how to measure.
- Before termination, clients should check ownership and admin rights for each system, including Search Console.
- Recommended order: check ownership, set up own access, export data, then remove agency access last.
- Search Console data should be exported before any change. "Ein vorhandener Zugang ersetzt keine Datensicherung."
- A handover log should list systems, access, open work, open invoices and the end of responsibility.
- The article warns that a new provider has an interest to stress the old agency's weaknesses.
- Clients should involve a lawyer when accounts or data are withheld or when the agency disputes a termination.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- D: Mirror the client view. Keep our own record of agreed vs delivered work, so a client review finds clear proof.
- H: Offer an orderly handover log if the client wants to separate. Do not withhold data or access.
- E: The client side is told that poor results alone do not justify immediate termination. This supports our position on the contract term.
- D: A third-party "audit" from a competitor can be biased. The article itself says a new provider has this incentive.
