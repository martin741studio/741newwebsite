# V08 How I Use a Free 5-Minute Audit to Get Agency Clients (No Sales Calls)
Source: videos/V08-how-i-use-a-free-5-minute-audit-to-get-agency-clients-no-sal.md, VIDEO, uploaded 2026-07-15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=413uyU4HaIw
Speaker/channel: Adam Erhart (internal only; never name in client material)
Reliability: Agency owner who sells an affiliate trial and an "AgencyOS" snapshot for a marketing software (strong sales bias). He explains how an agency uses an automatic audit to get clients. This is the method behind the competitor fake-audit pattern, seen from the sender's side. That makes it useful for group G.

## Practice from this source
- The audit is a sales tool. The sender wants a low score with "a real gap, a real problem with a real number attached."
- The software makes the full audit with one click. He says you do not need to be an expert, "you just needed to look at the audit."
- The first message contains only the audit and the score, for example "31 out of 100", with "No strings attached". It has no offer and no booking link.
- When the business owner asks "what does that mean?", the sender offers to show the fix. Then the sender sells a monthly service.
- He frames the audit as an x-ray: "nobody argues with an x-ray." The aim is to replace "please trust me" with "just look at this."
- His own warning: the score must be real. Do not make up a low number or inflate problems. He says inflation destroys reputation fast.
- If the owner does not reply, the sender sends one or two short reminders with one specific finding, and then moves on.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- G Competitor fake audit poaching: this is the core use. The scary backlink report probably follows this pattern: a one-click tool output, a scary number, no offer at first, the sale later. Explain this calmly and without attack on the sender.
- A Verification of claims: the speaker says himself that audit scores are often inflated. So check each claim in the report by hand before we accept or reject it.
- D Evidence and documentation: use the "x-ray" idea in our favour. Show our own evidence (Search Console data, work log, before and after) instead of asking for trust.

## Limits
- The video is about local-business marketing audits (reviews, speed to lead), not backlink or spam audits.
- It does not say how the receiving agency should react. It shows only the sender's tactics.
- The speaker sells software and a system, so the success claims are not independent.
