# A31 Client offboarding checklist for agencies
Source: articles/A31-chaser-client-offboarding-checklist-agencies.md, ARTICLE, published 2026-07-22 (visible "Updated July 2026"), Chaser (trychaser.com), https://www.trychaser.com/checklist-articles/client-offboarding-checklist-for-agencies
Reliability: Checklist page from a Slack task tool vendor. No named author. The content is a generic but complete agency checklist. It is not specific to SEO or Germany.

## Practice from this source
- Start from the contract: end date, notice, final scope, fees, IP terms, data duties and transition support.
- Name one offboarding owner. Send a dated schedule within two business days of the confirmed end.
- Freeze new requests that nobody approved. Record every open commitment with an owner and a date.
- Build a handover index: files, assets, reports, credentials, documentation, decisions and open risks.
- Export client data in the agreed format. Check that the client can open the files.
- The client must confirm admin access and recovery details before the agency removes itself.
- Send a final report with the measurement period and the known data limits.
- Get written acceptance of the handover. Record any paid transition support after the end date.
- Use a numbered access inventory and keep proof of each removal. Removing a visible user can leave tokens or shared links active.
- Do not withhold client files over an unpaid invoice without legal advice. The page warns against "an unsupported threat".
- Keep the contract end date separate from any paid transition period.
- Close with an internal review. Feed the lessons into scoping, access and account-management playbooks.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- H Retention vs clean separation and handover: use the 17 steps as the backbone of our separation option.
- D Evidence and documentation: the final report with data limits and the written acceptance close the evidence file.
- E Contract: start every separation from the contract terms, not from emotion.

## Limits
- Vendor checklist with no case examples.
- It assumes a calm, agreed exit. It says nothing about a client who removes access first.
