# O10 Google Ads Help: About access levels in your Google Ads account
Source: official-docs/google-ads-access-levels.md, OFFICIAL, living page fetched 2026-10-12, https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9978556?hl=en
Reliability: High. Google Ads Help.

## Practice from this source
- Access levels: Email-only, Billing, Read-only, Standard, Admin.
- Only Admin can give access, change access levels, accept or reject manager link requests, and unlink manager accounts.
- Standard can edit campaigns but cannot manage users or links.
- If a manager account has ownership, its admins can also edit user access, managers and product links in the client account.
- "If your account has only one administrator, you may lose access to your tags if that user becomes unavailable." Add a second admin.
- Payments profile users cannot be changed by Google Ads users. Support must be contacted by the primary contact.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- B: The client should hold Admin. The agency should be linked as manager, ideally without ownership.
- H: For a handover, check who is Admin and who owns the account before the last day.

## Limits
- No dispute guidance. Google support helps with access, not with the contract (see A09).
