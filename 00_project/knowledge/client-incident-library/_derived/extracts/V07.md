# V07 A Service Business With 3% Annual Churn
Source: videos/V07-a-service-business-with-3-annual-churn.md, VIDEO, uploaded 2026-08-05, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EYfsOP1QLJk
Speaker/channel: MoreMozi (Hormozi Hotline) (internal only; never name in client material)
Reliability: WEAK. Short coaching call between a well-known business investor and a content-agency owner. The investor sells a free "scaling roadmap" (lead magnet). The call is about growth and client acquisition. Only two side remarks touch contracts and quality control. US view on contracts.

## Practice from this source
- He says contracts are not really enforceable in the US. Their main value is the client's mental commitment.
- He suggests a 3-month minimum term, not 12 months. Reason: a client who expects results in 30 days is the wrong client.
- An upfront payment of about two to three months of costs is fine as protection.
- For quality at scale, use a checklist and check random samples of the work against it. Improve the checklist over time.
- Expect more churn when you sell to cold audiences instead of referrals.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- E Contract and pausing work (weak): a minimum term mainly creates commitment. Do not plan the response around legal enforcement. Note: German contract law differs from the US view.
- H Retention vs clean separation (weak): a quality checklist with random checks gives prevention and evidence that the work meets a standard.

## Limits
- The source does not cover client conflict, mistrust, access, apologies or SEO.
- The contract remark is a US opinion. It is not legal advice and does not apply to German law.
