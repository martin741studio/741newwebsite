## Practice from this source
- Google released the September 2026 spam update on Thursday 24 September 2026, around noon.
- Google said the rollout takes about 2 full weeks. Earlier 2026 spam updates took about 2 days.
- Google's John Mueller confirmed the longer rollout: "this is likely to take longer than some of the previous ones."
- Google said there is "nothing different" about this update. It is a normal spam update.
- Ranking volatility started on 23 September, one day before the announcement.
- This was the fourth spam update of 2026. The year also had two core updates and one Discover update.
- The video does not say what type of spam this update targets. Do not claim it targets links.
- Search Console now has a "multimodal" filter in the performance reports. It shows traffic from Google Lens, Circle to Search and image uploads.
- Google Business Profile now gives only 4 days to reject a user-suggested edit. After 4 days Google applies the edit automatically.
- Google Business Profile now sends emails when it detects a spike of spam reviews. Google removes them and pauses new reviews for a time.
