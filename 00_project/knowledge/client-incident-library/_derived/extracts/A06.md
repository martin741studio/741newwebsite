# A06 Negative SEO: How Attacks Work and What Google Ignores
Source: articles/A06-saasylinks-negative-seo-what-google-ignores.md, ARTICLE, published 2026-09-21, Vikas Kalwani / SAASY LINKS, https://saasylinks.com/blog/negative-seo/
Reliability: Medium. Link-building agency (bias: sells links). Quotes Google statements; own client data not verifiable.

## Practice from this source
- Spam-link attacks rarely work in 2026. Google's December 2022 link spam update lets SpamBrain neutralize such links.
- Quoted Mueller (Feb 2023): agencies that sell both attacks and clean-up "are just making stuff up".
- Attacks that still matter: hacking and injected pages, content scraping, fake link-removal requests, smear campaigns and fake reviews.
- Triage in order: Google update dates, technical errors, Security issues report, Manual actions report. Stop at the first yes.
- The author reports 40 client drops blamed on negative SEO: 37 had other causes, 3 were hacks, none were spam links alone.
- Link penalties are mostly self-inflicted, often from cheap white-label link providers.
- Real attack signs: unknown pages indexed under your domain ("site:" search with casino, loan words), Security issues warning, strong backlinks lost in a batch.
- "Services that sell attacks and services that sell toxic link protection are two sides of the same business."

## Use for client-access-lockout
- A: Run the four-step triage and also the "site:" search for injected pages. A hack is the real risk; a link list is not.
- G: The "scare and sell clean-up" model is a known business pattern.
- A/F: If the agency used a white-label link provider, check that honestly. That is where real penalties come from.

## Limits
- The 40-case figure is the author's own claim. Not independently checked.
