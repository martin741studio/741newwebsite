# A07 Toxische Backlinks erkennen & entfernen
Source: articles/A07-werbeagentur-nuernberg-toxische-backlinks.md, ARTICLE (German), published 2026-08-26, Werbeagentur Nürnberg (Gestaltung unserer Tage), https://www.werbeagentur-nuernberg.de/toxische-backlinks-der-stille-rankingkiller-und-wie-ihr-ihn-stoppt/
Reliability: Low-medium. German agency blog that sells backlink audits and disavow work. Useful as an example of the German market message.

## Practice from this source
- States that Google "ignoriert offensichtlichen Spam meist automatisch" since Penguin.
- Names two real risk cases: a manual action in Search Console, or a link profile so unnatural that Google distrusts the whole domain.
- Says one bad link rarely harms. A pattern does.
- Recommends a tool-based audit with spam scores, removal requests, then disavow.
- Sells regular audits and disavow work as an ongoing service.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- G: Shows how German agencies market "toxische Backlinks" audits: the text admits Google ignores most spam, then sells ongoing disavow work. Expect clients to receive such offers.
- A: The two named risk cases match Google's docs (manual action). Use the same check.

## Limits
- Advice to disavow routinely contradicts Google's disavow page (O02). Do not follow that part.
