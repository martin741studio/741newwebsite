# L06 BGB § 314 Kündigung von Dauerschuldverhältnissen aus wichtigem Grund
Source: official-docs/law-bgb-314.md, LAW, gesetze-im-internet.de, fetched 2026-10-12, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__314.html
Reliability: High (statute text). Background only, not legal advice.

## Practice from this source
- Each party can end a continuing obligation for an important reason (wichtiger Grund) without notice period.
- An important reason exists if continuation is unreasonable after weighing all facts and both sides' interests.
- If the reason is a breach of contract, termination is allowed only after a deadline to remedy (Frist zur Abhilfe) or a warning (Abmahnung) failed, with exceptions.
- The party must terminate within a reasonable time after learning the reason.
- Damages claims stay possible after termination.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- E/H: Before any termination for an important reason, set a written deadline to remedy (for example, restore access by a date). Do not terminate on the same day as the lock-out.
- E: Act within a reasonable time. Do not let the situation run for months.

## Limits
- For service contracts, § 626 (L10) is the more specific rule. Which rule applies is a legal question.
