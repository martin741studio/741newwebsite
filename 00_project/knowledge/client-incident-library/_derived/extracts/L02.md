# L02 BGB § 293 Annahmeverzug
Source: official-docs/law-bgb-293.md, LAW, gesetze-im-internet.de, fetched 2026-10-12, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__293.html
Reliability: High (statute text). Background only, not legal advice.

## Practice from this source
- The creditor (here: the client) is in default of acceptance (Annahmeverzug) when the creditor does not accept the offered performance.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- E: If the agency offers its agreed work and the client does not accept it (for example by blocking access), Annahmeverzug can start. § 615 then deals with the fee for services.

## Limits
- Read together with §§ 294 to 296. Whether the facts meet the conditions needs a lawyer.
