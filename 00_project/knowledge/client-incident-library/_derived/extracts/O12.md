# O12 Tag Manager Help: User permissions
Source: official-docs/gtm-user-permissions.md, OFFICIAL, living page fetched 2026-10-12, https://support.google.com/tagmanager/answer/6107011?hl=en
Reliability: High. Google Tag Manager Help.

## Practice from this source
- Account level: User or Administrator. Container level: Read, Edit, Approve, Publish.
- Access goes only to Google Accounts.
- "If your account has only one administrator, you may lose access to your tags." Add a second administrator.
- Google says: "make sure your account is managed by someone in your organization, not an external agency".

## Use for client-access-lockout
- B: Google's own text tells the client to own the Tag Manager account. The agency gets container rights only.
- H: In a handover, confirm the client is Administrator before the agency leaves.

## Limits
- No guidance on disputes.
