# L04 BGB § 295 Wörtliches Angebot
Source: official-docs/law-bgb-295.md, LAW, gesetze-im-internet.de, fetched 2026-10-12, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__295.html
Reliability: High (statute text). Background only, not legal advice.

## Practice from this source
- A verbal or written offer (wörtliches Angebot) is enough if the creditor said she will not accept the performance.
- It is also enough if the performance needs an action of the creditor (Handlung des Gläubigers).
- "Dem Angebot der Leistung steht die Aufforderung an den Gläubiger gleich, die erforderliche Handlung vorzunehmen." A request to the client to do the needed action counts as the offer.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- E: Restoring Search Console access is an action only the client can do. A written message that (1) offers the agreed work and (2) asks her to restore access is, by the statute text, an offer under § 295.
- D: Send this offer in writing, with a date, and keep a copy.

## Limits
- Whether the access is really "required" for the agreed services depends on the contract scope. Check with a lawyer before you rely on it.
