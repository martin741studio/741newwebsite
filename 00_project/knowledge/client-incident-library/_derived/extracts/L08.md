# L08 BGB § 615 Vergütung bei Annahmeverzug und bei Betriebsrisiko
Source: official-docs/law-bgb-615.md, LAW, gesetze-im-internet.de, fetched 2026-10-12, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__615.html
Reliability: High (statute text). Background only, not legal advice.

## Practice from this source
- If the client is in default of acceptance of the services, the provider can claim the agreed fee for the services not performed, without a duty to perform them later.
- The provider must deduct what it saves, earns elsewhere, or maliciously fails to earn.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- E: In a Dienstvertrag, a blocked client may still owe the fee for the blocked period, minus savings. The agency must first put the client in default (offer under § 295).
- F: Do not lead with this in the conversation. Use it as background for a calm, written status: "work is paused because access is missing; the agreement continues".

## Limits
- Savings deduction and proof are complex. Legal advice needed before any claim.
