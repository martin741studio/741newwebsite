# L14 BGB § 643 Kündigung bei unterlassener Mitwirkung
Source: official-docs/law-bgb-643.md, LAW, gesetze-im-internet.de, fetched 2026-10-12, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__643.html
Reliability: High (statute text). Background only, not legal advice.

## Practice from this source
- In a case of § 642, the contractor can set the customer a reasonable deadline to perform the action, with the statement that the contract ends if the action is not done in time.
- If the deadline passes without the action, the contract counts as cancelled (gilt als aufgehoben).

## Use for client-access-lockout
- E/H: This is the statutory model for "restore access by date X, otherwise the work part ends". The message must name the action, the deadline and the consequence.
- F: Use a reasonable deadline (for example 10 to 14 days), not a same-day ultimatum.

## Limits
- Werkvertrag only. The legal effect is strong. Use only after legal advice.
