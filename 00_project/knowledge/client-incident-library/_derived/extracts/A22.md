# A22 Internet-System-Vertrag (United Media, WN Online-Service od. wwwe GmbH) über 48 Monate zu 529 € fristlos kündigen?
Source: articles/A22-anwaltde-internet-system-vertrag-48-monate-kuendigen.md, ARTICLE, published 2026-10-06, RA Lars Hämmerling on anwalt.de, https://www.anwalt.de/rechtstipps/internet-system-vertrag-united-media-wn-online-service-od-wwwe-gmbh-ueber-48-monate-zu-529-eur-fristlos-kuendigen-285033.html
Reliability: Lawyer's Rechtstipp, written for clients who want to leave. Fresh (2026-10-06). It shows the arguments a client lawyer will use against a long-term web/SEO contract. Background, not legal advice.

## Practice from this source
- The BGH classifies "Internet-System-Verträge" (website build, hosting, support over a term) as work contracts (Werkvertrag).
- As a work contract, the client can terminate at any time (§ 648 BGB). A minimum term does not stop this.
- After such a termination, the provider gets only the pay for the part already done. It must deduct saved costs.
- The provider must show its saved costs in concrete terms. The article cites BGH VII ZR 146/10 (24.03.2011).
- BGH VII ZR 133/10 (27.01.2011): a 36-month term did not exclude free termination.
- Termination for good cause (§ 648a BGB) normally needs a prior deadline for remedy or a warning.
- Hidden terms (term written as a word, no total price) can make clauses invalid for lack of transparency (§ 307 BGB).
- Oral promises such as "top Google placement" weaken the provider's position later.
- The lawyer advises clients to send the termination through a lawyer. Often it is combined with rescission or retention.
- Unclear or contradictory clauses go against the party that wrote the standard terms.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- E: If a court sees our program as a work contract, the client can end it at any time. Then we keep only the pay for work done, minus saved costs. Our program text should show a service character.
- E: Do not promise rankings or "top placement" in sales talks or offers. Such promises push the contract towards work-contract rules.
- D: Keep a per-month record of delivered work. It is the base for any partial-pay claim.
- E: Put the term and the total price clearly in figures in the contract.

## Limits
- The article targets cold-call website vendors, not a cooperative SEO program with a health practitioner.
- It is written from the client side and is partly promotional.
- It does not deal with service contracts or default of acceptance (§ 615 BGB).
