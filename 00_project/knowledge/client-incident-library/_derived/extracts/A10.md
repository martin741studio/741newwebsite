# A10 Who Should Own Your Google Business Profile? (2026)
Source: articles/A10-searchpod-who-should-own-gbp.md, ARTICLE, published 2026-09-23, Mousa H / SearchPod, https://searchpod.com/answers/who-should-own-my-google-business-profile
Reliability: Medium. Agency answer page. Consistent with Google's role model (O08).

## Practice from this source
- The business should hold Primary Owner with an email on its own domain, not an agency domain or a personal Gmail of one employee.
- An agency needs only Manager access for local SEO and review work.
- Reviews stay with the listing when access changes, unless the listing is suspended or merged.
- If an agency holds Primary Owner and does not cooperate, Google has an ownership request process. It needs proof of the business.
- New agency: claim the listing under the business email from day one, then add the agency as Manager.
- Keep at least one Owner login inside the business that does not depend on one employee or provider.
- The same principle applies to domain, hosting and Google Ads.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- B: Confirm the client is Primary Owner of her profile. Say so in writing. It removes one source of fear.
- H: In a separation, the agency leaves as Manager. Nothing of the client's reviews is lost.
