# L10 BGB § 626 Fristlose Kündigung aus wichtigem Grund
Source: official-docs/law-bgb-626.md, LAW, gesetze-im-internet.de, fetched 2026-10-12, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__626.html
Reliability: High (statute text). Background only, not legal advice.

## Practice from this source
- Each party can end a service relationship without notice for an important reason, if continuation to the end of the notice period is unreasonable.
- The termination must happen within two weeks after the party learns the decisive facts.
- On request, the terminating party must state the reason in writing without delay.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- E/H: The two-week window matters for both sides. Note the date when the lock-out became known.
- D: Keep a dated timeline. A termination for cause needs facts that a third party can check.

## Limits
- Whether a lock-out after a scare is an "important reason" for either side is a legal judgement.
