# L15 BGB § 648 Kündigungsrecht des Bestellers
Source: official-docs/law-bgb-648.md, LAW, gesetze-im-internet.de, fetched 2026-10-12, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__648.html
Reliability: High (statute text). Background only, not legal advice.

## Practice from this source
- The customer can terminate a Werkvertrag at any time before completion.
- The contractor then keeps the claim to the agreed fee, minus savings and other earnings.
- The law presumes 5 % of the fee for the unfinished part.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- H: In a clean separation of a Werkvertrag part, the open fee question follows this rule. Document what is finished and what is not.

## Limits
- Werkvertrag only. Contracts often regulate this differently.
