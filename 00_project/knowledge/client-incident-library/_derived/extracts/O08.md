# O08 Google Business Profile Help: Manage owners and managers
Source: official-docs/gbp-manage-owners-managers.md, OFFICIAL, living page fetched 2026-10-12, https://support.google.com/business/answer/3403100?hl=en
Reliability: High. Google Business Profile Help.

## Practice from this source
- Each user needs an own Google Account. Do not share passwords.
- Owner: full control, can add or remove users and delete the profile. One primary owner, several owners possible.
- Manager: almost the same rights as owner, but cannot add or remove users or remove the profile.
- Managers can edit name, category, hours, phone, services, posts, photos, and respond to reviews.
- Only owners can remove owners or managers. "When you remove a user, they get an email."
- After removal, the removed user's past review replies and posts stay on the profile.
- A primary owner cannot leave before transferring primary ownership.
- New owners and managers must wait 7 days before they can delete the profile, remove other users, or transfer primary ownership.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- B: The client should be primary owner. The agency should be manager. This is Google's own role split.
- H: In a separation, the agency removes itself ("Stop managing"), or the client removes it. Past work (posts, replies) stays on the profile.
- D: The removal email is a dated record of when access ended.

## Limits
- No guidance on agency disputes or ownership recovery in this page.
