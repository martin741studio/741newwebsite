# L01 BGB § 241 Pflichten aus dem Schuldverhältnis
Source: official-docs/law-bgb-241.md, LAW, gesetze-im-internet.de, fetched 2026-10-12, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__241.html
Reliability: High (statute text). Background only, not legal advice.

## Practice from this source
- Para. 2: a contract can oblige each party to respect the rights, legal interests and interests of the other party (duty of consideration, Rücksichtnahmepflicht).
- This duty binds both sides: the agency and the client.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- E/F: The agency must protect the client's interests also in a conflict. Do not take steps that harm her site or data (for example no re-verification against her will, no withholding of her data).

## Limits
- General clause. Its concrete content depends on the case and on case law not covered here.
