# V16 Black Hat SEO in 2026: Why PBNs Still Work (With the Guy Who Owns 200,000 Domains)
Source: videos/V16-black-hat-seo-in-2026-why-pbns-still-work-with-the-guy-who-o.md, VIDEO, uploaded 2026-09-26, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BMVgbnaLiFE
Speaker/channel: Edward Sturm podcast, with a domain and link seller (200,000 domains, 30,000 sites), a known black-hat SEO, and an SEO consultant (internal only; never name in client material)
Reliability: Insider view of link-selling networks from people who sell domains, links and courses (strong bias); openly against Google's rules; useful only to understand how spam link networks work, never as advice.

## Practice from this source
- One guest runs a PBN of about 30,000 websites with "literally zero traffic." He does not try to rank them.
- About 12,000 of these sites are old sites restored from web archives. The rest are WordPress sites for iGaming link sales.
- He says selling PBN links is "just the easiest way to make money" in SEO. This is his opinion and sales pitch.
- Networks hide footprints with Cloudflare, many registrars, many hosts and CDNs. AI now writes the content for free.
- Definition from the guests: "if you're selling the links publicly, it's a link farm."
- Publicly sold networks are easy to detect, "including Google engineers" who can buy the links. Many such networks were "wiped out."
- Domain sellers find expired domains with old backlinks by scraping broken links on strong sites.
- The guests treat link sites as a cheap commodity: "They're not pets... They are cows."
- They describe a "churn and burn" approach for iGaming. Sites are disposable.
- A guest warns about negative SEO in AI: mass posts that call a brand a "scam" could shift AI output. He says this is untested theory.
- A guest describes dormant fake Trustpilot accounts for later fake reviews. This is abuse, recorded here only as a threat pattern.
- Contradiction with Google: the guests say PBN links pass value. Google's spam policies call this link spam and say its systems neutralise such links.
- A guest says a separate parasite page cannot be traced back to his own site. This shows that link spam is often built far from any real business.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- C Disavow and spam links: explains the model behind domains like seozira.shop or backlinker.shop. Mass sites, no traffic, links for sale, built to be thrown away.
- A Verification of claims: Google catches publicly sold networks. This supports the point that such links are typical web noise that Google knows well.
- G Competitor fake audit poaching: the people who sell links and domains also profit from fear about links. Read any "toxic link" list with that bias in mind.

## Limits
- It does not explain why an unrelated small site receives unpaid spam links. Do not claim that from this source.
- It says nothing about disavow or Google ignoring links; all link-value claims are from link sellers.
- Most of the 86 minutes is mindset, economics and AI theory; off-topic for our case.
