# L09 BGB § 621 Kündigungsfristen bei Dienstverhältnissen
Source: official-docs/law-bgb-621.md, LAW, gesetze-im-internet.de, fetched 2026-10-12, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__621.html
Reliability: High (statute text). Background only, not legal advice.

## Practice from this source
- Statutory notice for non-employment service contracts depends on how pay is measured.
- Monthly pay: notice by the 15th of a month, effective at month end.
- Quarterly or longer pay: six weeks' notice to the end of a calendar quarter.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- H: If the contract has no own notice rule, these defaults apply. Check the contract first; contract terms usually take priority.

## Limits
- Fixed-term contracts and individual clauses can change this. Not covered here.
