# A01 Google Says The Disavow Tool Does Have Effects
Source: articles/A01-ser-google-says-disavow-tool-does-have-effects.md, ARTICLE, published 2026-09-02, Barry Schwartz / Search Engine Roundtable, https://www.seroundtable.com/google-disavow-tool-effects-42004.html
Reliability: High for what Google's John Mueller said (direct Reddit quote). Context: a site that bought guest posts and links.

## Practice from this source
- Mueller: the disavow tool "has its place & time, it does have effects".
- Mueller: "disavowing a handful of links is not going to make your site drop out of search."
- Mueller's advice after a drop: fix the disavow file to what you really need ("might be empty, or might not be empty").
- Then look for other causes: a spam update in the same period, or big technical issues (for example the whole site blocked).
- Recovery from bigger algorithmic changes "can take quite some time - sometimes many months".
- The author repeats that most sites should not disavow, and that Google may remove the tool one day.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- C: A disavow file is not neutral. It has effects. Never upload or change one in panic or because a third-party list says so.
- A: If the client blames a ranking drop on links, check update dates and technical issues first.

## Limits
- One Reddit thread, specific to a site with bought links. Not a general rule.
