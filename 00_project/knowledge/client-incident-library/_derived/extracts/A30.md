# A30 SEO-Agentur wechseln: Anleitung und Übergabe-Checkliste
Source: articles/A30-dama-seo-agentur-wechseln-uebergabe.md, ARTICLE, published 2026-07-19, David Malewski / DAMA Solutions, https://dama-solutions.de/wissen/seo/seo-agentur-wechseln
Reliability: German SEO consultancy writes for clients who want to leave their agency. It sells a "second opinion" and audits. The guide is calm, factual and close to our German market. It is written from the client side, not the agency side.

## Practice from this source
- The article says unhappy clients have three possible causes: the agency, the expectation, or the market. Only the first one is fixed by a change.
- It recommends an independent second opinion before the client gives notice. Offer the client this route instead of a fight.
- Target state for Search Console: the client owns the verified property. The agency is only an added user.
- The same rule applies to GA4, Google Ads and Google Business Profile. The client is owner, the agency is invited.
- The client needs a link-profile document: target page, linking page, date and how the link came to exist. Keep this list ready for each client.
- Notice must be in the form the contract asks for. Proof of receipt matters, for example registered mail.
- Ongoing SEO support is usually a service contract (Dienstvertrag), not a contract for a result. No agency owes a ranking.
- A written handover protocol has four parts: list of measures with dates, open tickets, redirect list, content pipeline.
- Add an access overview and the last report with a raw-data export.
- The article says the handover must be "ein Dokument, keine Gesprächsnotiz". Oral handovers get lost.
- Key rule after a change: delete nothing and keep all redirects. Deleted pages lose their positions.
- Define who acts on outages or security updates during the transition weeks.
- Notice of termination should stay factual and without blame. The handover works better when the tone stays professional.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- H Retention vs clean separation and handover: use the four-part handover protocol and the access overview as our template for a clean separation.
- D Evidence and documentation: our list of measures with dates and the link documentation are the proof the client will ask for.
- B Account access: the client owning Search Console is normal and correct. Say so calmly. The issue is only that blocked access stops the agreed work.
- A Verification: the "agency, expectation or market" split helps us check which complaint is real before we answer.

## Limits
- Written for clients who leave an agency. It gives no wording for the agency side.
- It is not legal advice. The author says each contract clause needs a lawyer's check.
