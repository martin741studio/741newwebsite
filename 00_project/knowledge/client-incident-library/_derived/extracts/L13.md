# L13 BGB § 642 Mitwirkung des Bestellers
Source: official-docs/law-bgb-642.md, LAW, gesetze-im-internet.de, fetched 2026-10-12, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__642.html
Reliability: High (statute text). Background only, not legal advice.

## Practice from this source
- If the work needs an action of the customer and the customer fails to act and so is in default of acceptance, the contractor can claim reasonable compensation (angemessene Entschädigung).
- The amount depends on the duration of the default and the agreed fee, minus savings and other earnings.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- E: For Werkvertrag parts (for example a page build that needs CMS or Search Console access), the client's cooperation is legally relevant. Name the needed cooperation in writing.

## Limits
- Applies to Werkvertrag. For Dienstvertrag see § 615.
