# L11 BGB § 627 Fristlose Kündigung bei Vertrauensstellung
Source: official-docs/law-bgb-627.md, LAW, gesetze-im-internet.de, fetched 2026-10-12, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__627.html
Reliability: High (statute text). Background only, not legal advice.

## Practice from this source
- For "Dienste höherer Art" that are given on special trust, either side can terminate at any time without an important reason, if there is no permanent relationship with fixed pay.
- The provider may not terminate at an unsuitable time (zur Unzeit) without an important reason. Otherwise the provider owes damages.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- E/H: If the agency's services count as "Dienste höherer Art" based on trust, the client may be able to end the contract at once. Fixed monthly fees may exclude this. Get legal advice before you argue either way.
- H: If the agency ends the relationship, it must give the client time to get the services elsewhere (handover, notice).

## Limits
- Courts decide case by case whether SEO or marketing services are "höherer Art" and whether fixed pay excludes § 627. Not answered by the statute.
