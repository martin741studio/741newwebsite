# V12 You Don't Need 1,000 Backlinks to Rank on Google
Source: videos/V12-you-don-t-need-1-000-backlinks-to-rank-on-google.md, VIDEO, uploaded 2026-07-29, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8dwPZu9rjt8
Speaker/channel: Edward Sturm (internal only; never name in client material)
Reliability: SEO podcaster who reads and comments on a Reddit thread; sells an SEO course (bias); most points are Reddit opinions, not tests; medium distance to our case (link quality, not spam attacks).

## Practice from this source
- A small site with 3 backlinks asked how to compete with rivals that have 500 to 1,000. The answer: relevance matters more than count.
- "If you have three very high quality backlinks, that could be enough." He adds that more is probably better.
- A Reddit commenter says to ignore domain authority and domain rating. These are third-party metrics "which Google doesn't use."
- To judge a linking site, look at its traffic over time. Avoid sites with sharp drops to near zero.
- Good links sit on pages that Google indexes. Better links sit on indexed pages that also get clicks.
- The best links "feel super relevant" to the business.
- Likely spam links are the opposite: "why is this business even getting this backlink?"
- Spam links can also look "inserted by a bot."
- Genuine editorial digital PR is "generally treated as a legitimate link rather than link spam."
- Local example: sponsor a local event and get a link that sends real visitors.
- Daily practice: answer one journalist request per day on expert-quote platforms.
- Buying links is "not mandatory at all." Some commenters still buy links.

## Use for client-access-lockout
- A Verification of claims: scary audits often use DA/DR or "toxic" scores. The source says these are third-party metrics, not Google metrics. A high-risk score in a tool is not Google's view.
- C Disavow and spam links: the source gives a simple test. Irrelevant, bot-like links with no traffic are spam links. They are not links that a serious agency builds for a Heilpraktikerin.
- D Evidence and documentation: the agency can show its own links as relevant, indexed, local pages. This separates its work from random spam domains.
