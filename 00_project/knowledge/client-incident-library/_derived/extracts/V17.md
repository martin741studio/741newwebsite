Reliability: Medium-low. Analogy from another service trade, very high reach. One owner's view. Useful only for trust and mistake handling.

## Practice from this source
- Trust is "the best asset you have". It comes only from work that earns it.
- He does not blame the other shop in public. He focuses on how a job should go.
- The other shop lost the customer when it dismissed a real complaint ("AC is ice cold, I don't know what you're talking about"). Dismissing the client's report is the error.
- He separates an honest mistake from lack of care. Mistakes happen; skipped final tests and hidden shortcuts break trust.
- Test the result before handover. A final check would have caught the fault.
- If his shop makes a mistake, he says it directly: "We done messed up. No excuses." Then he fixes it at his cost.
- He recommends against work the customer does not need, even if he loses the job. Honest advice keeps trust.
- "Sometimes you have to lose business-wise to keep that trust."

## Use for client-access-lockout
- F: Never dismiss the client's concern ("nothing is wrong"). Check it and show the result.
- F/A: If the check finds a real agency mistake, own it in one sentence with no excuse, then fix it.
- H: Honest advice that costs the agency revenue (for example "you do not need a disavow") builds trust.
