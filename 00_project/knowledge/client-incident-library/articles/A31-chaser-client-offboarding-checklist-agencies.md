# Client offboarding checklist for agencies

Client offboarding closes commercial, operational, data, and access obligations without interrupting the client's business. A weak exit leaves the agency paying for a software seat, a former contractor inside the ad account, or the client asking for a design source file after the retention period has passed.

This client offboarding checklist covers an agency engagement from confirmed end date to final delivery, settled account, transferred ownership, revoked access, retained records, and internal review. It is written for account leads coordinating delivery, finance, legal, operations, and specialist teams.

## The 17-step checklist

1. Confirm the termination or expiry date, notice, final scope, fees, intellectual-property terms, data duties, and transition support from the contract.
2. Name the offboarding owner and issue the client-approved schedule with deliverables, meetings, access changes, invoices, and contacts.
3. Freeze unapproved new requests and record every remaining commitment in [your project system] with an owner and acceptance date.
4. Complete or formally transfer open deliverables, revisions, campaigns, incidents, and vendor commitments.
5. Prepare the final handover index for source files, approved assets, reports, credentials, documentation, decisions, and outstanding risks.
6. Export client-owned data in the agreed format and verify that the client can open the files.
7. Transfer ownership of domains, analytics, advertising, social, email, hosting, repositories, design files, and other client-controlled accounts.
8. Have the client confirm administrator access and recovery details before agency administrators are removed.
9. End or transfer agency-funded subscriptions, media commitments, phone numbers, licenses, and subcontractor services.
10. Send the final performance or project report with the agreed measurement period and known data limitations.
11. Issue the final invoice, credits, reimbursable costs, and account statement through [your billing system].
12. Collect written acceptance of the handover and record any paid transition support that continues after the end date.
13. Remove agency employees, contractors, integrations, tokens, shared links, and devices from client systems on the approved date.
14. Apply the contract and privacy rules to retained, returned, deleted, or anonymized client data and record completion.
15. Request structured client feedback and permission for any testimonial, reference, case study, or portfolio use.
16. Close the client record in [your CRM] with end reason, final value, contacts, documents, and future re-engagement date.
17. Run the internal review and assign changes to scoping, delivery, billing, access, or account-management playbooks.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long should agency client offboarding take?

Plan one to four weeks for an ordinary agency exit, depending on notice, active campaigns, account transfers, data volume, and client availability. Issue a dated schedule within two business days of confirmation. Keep the contractual end date separate from any paid transition period so responsibilities, access, and billing remain clear.

### Who should own client offboarding at an agency?

The account lead should own the client schedule and communication, with delivery leads confirming work, operations transferring assets and access, finance settling the account, and legal or privacy owners handling data terms. Name one final approver internally and one administrator on the client side so account transfers do not depend on a general inbox.

### When should agency access to client accounts be removed?

Remove access on the approved end date after the client confirms administrator ownership, recovery methods, and required files. Revoke contractors and unused integrations earlier where they no longer support delivery. Use a numbered access inventory and capture completion evidence, since removing the visible agency user can leave API tokens or shared links active.

### Should an agency withhold files when the final invoice is unpaid?

Follow the signed contract and obtain legal advice for the applicable jurisdiction before withholding client property or access. Separate undisputed client-owned data and operational credentials from disputed work where the agreement requires it. Finance should document the balance, notice, due date, and collection route while the account lead avoids making an unsupported threat.

### How long should an agency retain former client data?

Use the contract, privacy commitments, legal requirements, insurance needs, and the agency retention schedule to set the period by data type. Some financial and contract records may remain for years while working files and personal data are deleted sooner. Give each retained category an owner, location, deletion date, access rule, and legal-hold process.
