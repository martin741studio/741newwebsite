## Key points
- Selection criteria: markup quality, file types, approval depth, versions, audit trail, guest access.
- Four version-compare modes exist in the market: side by side, swipe, overlay, pixel contrast.
- Good tools always open the current version for every reviewer.
- Reviewers can select exact words or phrases to comment on copy.
- Assets can route to the next stage automatically when a stage completes.
- Audit trail: every comment, change and approval with time and named person.
- The audit trail can be exported.
- Guests can annotate and approve without a full login.
- Ziflow ReviewAI checks assets against brand rules before a human looks.
- Per-user pricing gets expensive when many clients review.
- Bias note: vendor ranks its own product first.
