## Key points
- Reminders go out before, at, and after a proof deadline (Enterprise).
- Standard and Pro send one reminder at the deadline, with no setup.
- After the deadline, reminders repeat at a set frequency.
- Only decision-makers get reminders. Comment-only reviewers do not.
- Once a reviewer submits a decision, reminders stop for that person.
- Admins can also send a manual reminder outside the schedule.
- Deadlines can be a fixed date, or relative to stage start (e.g. 48 hours).
- Relative deadlines can count business days only.
- Each deadline has a set time zone.
- Workflows have stages; a stage can be skippable or locked.
- Templates save reviewers and stages for repeat jobs.
- Reviewers choose how often they get comment and decision emails.
