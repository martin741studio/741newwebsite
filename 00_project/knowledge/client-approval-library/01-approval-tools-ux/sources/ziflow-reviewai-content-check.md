## Key points
- Ziflow wrote nine plain-English checks and ran them with ReviewAI on four ads.
- One was their own ad. The first version passed all checks.
- The revised version fixed a crop issue but failed a new safety check.
- Lesson: compare old and new versions; a fix can add a new problem.
- Each AI result came with a written reason, pinned to the exact frame or page.
- Results: 36 checks, 25 passed, 11 failed, at least one false positive.
- The AI did not block anything. A human made each final call.
- Ziflow's docs say AI checks can be wrong and humans stay in control.
- Ziflow suggests testing a check on at least 10 proofs before live use.
