# Planable Help: How to get client approval for content

- URL: https://help.planable.io/hc/en-us/articles/22581435847324-How-to-get-Client-Approval-for-content-in-Planable
- Type: official help center (living doc)
- Fetched: 2026-10-13

## Key points
- "Required approval": nothing publishes without at least one approver.
- "Multi-level approval": internal steps first, then the client.
- Invite clients as "Client" members with "Approver" rights.
- New posts can start hidden ("internal") until the team is ready.
- A striped bar marks posts the client cannot see yet.
- The team releases many posts at once with "Make visible to clients".
- The client gets one saved view: all items with status "Not approved".
- The view works like an inbox. Approved items leave it ("inbox zero").
- The same view link works month after month.
- Client email options: when idle, always, immediately, or hourly.
- Public links need no login, but only allow viewing and comments.
- Public links cannot approve. Approval needs a known identity.
