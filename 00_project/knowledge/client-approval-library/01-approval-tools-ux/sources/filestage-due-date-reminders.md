# Filestage: stay on top of your due dates as a reviewer

- URL: https://help.filestage.io/en/articles/3161157-stay-on-top-of-your-due-dates-as-a-reviewer
- Related: https://help.filestage.io/en/articles/3161155-set-up-and-manage-due-dates
- Type: official help center (living doc, no date shown)
- Fetched: 2026-10-13

## Key points
- Reminders are one daily email, a roundup of all pending reviews.
- Sent each morning, 7:00 to 8:00, not on weekends.
- The roundup lists items due soon and items overdue.
- Only items due between 2 days ago and 5 days ahead are included.
- Reminders stop for a file once the reviewer approves or requests changes.
- Reviewers who joined by link without an email get no reminders.
- They are asked for an email when they write their first comment.
- Due dates are set per file version, by the team, not by reviewers.
- Set the due date at upload, so it arrives with the "new file" notice.
