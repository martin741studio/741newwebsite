## Key points
- Assembly: the team assigns tasks to the client for approvals and content reviews.
- Assembly: clients log in with a magic link. No password.
- Assembly: messages, files, contracts, invoices and tasks sit in one portal.
- Assembly: contracts carry built-in e-signatures as a formal approval.
- Assembly: automated reminders are shown for payments, not for approvals.
- SuperOkay: an "Approve" button asks the client to sign off a project, doc or brief.
- SuperOkay: the team can share several versions and ask for approval on them.
- SuperOkay: tasks for team or client, with due dates and attachments.
- SuperOkay: portal branded with the client's colors and logo.
- Gap: neither page documents deadlines, auto-approval, digests or audit logs for approvals.
