## Key points
- Four fixed decisions: Approved, Approved with changes, Changes required, Not relevant.
- "Approved with changes" means small edits, no new version needed.
- "Changes required" means edits plus a new version.
- Stage result: one "Changes required" beats everything else.
- Next, any "Approved with changes" beats plain "Approved".
- The final proof result uses the same rule across all stages.
- Admins can rename labels, but the logic behind them stays fixed.
- Ziflow warns: keep new labels close to the old meaning.
- If the proof is not locked, a reviewer can change a decision later.
- A changed decision makes the stage and final result recalculate.
- A revoked decision puts status back to "In progress".
- The final decision shows on the proof's Activity tab.
- Stages without decision-makers just show "Completed".
