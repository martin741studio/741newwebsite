# Do You Struggle With Making Decisions and Choice Overload?

- URL: https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/flourish-and-thrive/202609/do-you-struggle-with-making-decisions-and-choice-overload
- Author: Ilene Berns-Zare, PsyD (Psychology Today, "Flourish and Thrive")
- Published: 2026-09-02
- Type: Expert blog summary of older studies
- Underlying studies named: Iyengar & Lepper 2000 (6 vs 24 or 30 options); Bui et al. 2025 (UBC, climate actions); Schwartz, "The paradox of choice"

## Key points

- People act more often with 6 options than with 24 or 30 (jams, chocolates, optional essays).
- In a 2025 UBC study, students given fewer climate-action options picked a larger share of the actions.
- Many options can raise worry and regret after the choice.
- Abundant choice can end in paralysis or no choice at all.
- Advice for small decisions: limit the time and energy spent, choose from fewer options, accept "good enough".
- Avoid second-guessing after the decision.
- Caveat: a blog for general readers. Use it as support, not as the main evidence.
