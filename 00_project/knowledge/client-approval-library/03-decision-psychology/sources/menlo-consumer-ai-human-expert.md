# 2026: The State of Consumer AI

- URL: https://menlovc.com/perspective/2026-the-state-of-consumer-ai/
- Authors: Shawn Carolan, Amy Wu Martin, Sam Borja (Menlo Ventures)
- Published: 2026-09-16
- Type: Industry survey; 5,067 U.S. adults, fieldwork July 2026. Investor report, not peer-reviewed.

## Key points

- In all nine situations tested, people preferred a human expert to AI.
- Medical diagnosis: 63% human vs 19% AI. Mental health: 61% vs 19%. Legal advice: 59% vs 21%.
- Even for learning a skill, 44% still prefer a human vs 28% AI.
- AI users rank accuracy (45%) and trustworthiness (40%) above ease of use (32%).
- Among non-users, 70% distrust AI information and 83% would rather deal with a person.
- 32% of AI users have already let AI act for them without a final sign-off.
- For us: our clients work in exactly the domains where human expertise wins (health, law). "741 recommends" should read as a named human expert decision, not as "AI suggests".
- Accuracy and trust matter more to users than convenience. Speed of approval must not cost trust.
