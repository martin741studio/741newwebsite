# When is the right moment for a reminder or a nudge?

- URL: https://tre-up.com/research/when-is-the-right-moment-for-a-reminder
- Author: Tre-Up Research Notes (no named author)
- Published: 2026-10-07 (modified 2026-10-10)
- Type: Research summary of just-in-time intervention studies
- Underlying studies named: Nahum-Shani et al. 2018 (just-in-time adaptive interventions); Mehrotra et al. 2016 (10,372 real notifications); Okoshi et al. 2018 (holding notifications for a good moment); Iqbal & Bailey 2008 (natural breakpoints); Bidargaddi et al. 2018 (1,255 users, timing trial); Klasnja et al. 2019 (step-count prompts)

## Key points

- A person's ability to act on a reminder changes from moment to moment.
- Even useful notifications are disruptive at the wrong moment (field study of 10,372 notifications).
- Holding a notification until a good moment made people respond faster.
- Waiting for a natural break in work reduced frustration.
- Same message, different time: about 4% more app use the next day overall, about 12% more at 12:30 on weekends.
- Prompts work strongly at first and weaker over weeks. Repeated reminders lose power.
- For us: send the digest at a fixed time when practice owners have a break (for example before consultation hours or at lunch). Keep reminders rare so they stay effective.
