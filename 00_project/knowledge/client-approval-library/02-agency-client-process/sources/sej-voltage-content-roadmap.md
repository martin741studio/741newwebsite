## Key points

- Step 1 is the business objective. Every keyword must map to a business outcome such as leads.
- "Search competitors" differ from the competitors the client names in sales talks. The agency chooses them.
- Raw tool output is not a to-do list. Remove branded, off-intent and irrelevant terms by human judgment.
- Prefer winnable, high-intent terms over the tempting head term.
- Cluster by business meaning, not only by the tool's statistical grouping. Map each cluster to one page.
- Prioritize by difficulty, potential, intent and AI Overview presence. No tool does this scoring for you.
- Hand each cluster to a brief: primary keyword, supporting terms, intent, target page, angle.
- Write citable content: direct 2-3 sentence answers under headings that mirror real searches.
- Real expertise, concrete detail and data must come from humans. AI can draft but cannot fake them.
- Capture a baseline first. Test the method on one cluster before changing the whole operation.

## Relevance for 741

- Supports keeping keyword and page-structure decisions on the agency side (SEO lock). The client contributes expertise and real examples, not the keyword choice.
