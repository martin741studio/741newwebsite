# Agency Client Retention: Stop Month 6 Churn

- URL: https://agencydashboard.io/blog/agency-client-retention-month-six
- Publisher / author: Agency Dashboard (reporting tool vendor)
- Published: 2026-07-22
- Source type: vendor blog with cited studies

## Key points

- Clients who cancel at month 6 often decided in month 2 or 3 (cites OnboardMap).
- SEO has about 38% annual churn and typical 6-12 month contracts (cites Focus Digital 2026).
- About 43% of client churn happens in the first 90 days (cites GigRadar 2026).
- Reasons for leaving: 68% lack of proactive strategic guidance, 57% poor communication, 53% no visible value. Only 37% named price.
- State realistic SEO timelines in writing: ranking movement takes 3-6 months.
- Report leading indicators (technical fixes done, content published, links earned) before rankings move.
- Fixed cadence: written onboarding summary in week 1, short update every 2 weeks, monthly report session, quarterly milestone review.
- Flag wins at once in a short message. Do not wait for the monthly report.
- Share insights before the client asks, for example a competitor move plus a suggested reply.
- Use a signed scope and a standard onboarding checklist. Define the first milestone for the first 30 days.
- Do not use discounts to save accounts. Discounts do not fix silence or unclear value.

- The continuous item stream and daily digest are a form of "proactive guidance". Each item should show why it matters, not only what changed.
