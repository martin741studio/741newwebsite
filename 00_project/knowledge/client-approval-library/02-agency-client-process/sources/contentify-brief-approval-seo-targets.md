# Content Brief Client Approval: How To Streamline Sign-Off

- URL: https://contentify.ai/blog/content-brief-collaboration-client-approval/
- Publisher / author: Contentify (tool vendor), author not shown
- Published: 2026-07-19
- Source type: vendor blog, SEO content workflow

## Key points

- Put the keyword research inside the brief. The client approves strategy and SEO targets in one step.
- Finish keyword research before the brief goes out. The client must never approve a plan that SEO later overturns.
- Each brief has an "SEO targets" block: primary keyword, secondary keywords, search intent.
- Use a short yes/no checklist (6 questions) for the client. Every "no" must name the element and the wanted direction.
- Keep briefs short and scannable (2-3 key messages, bold the critical details). A 5-minute read gets reviewed sooner than a 20-minute read.
- Review windows: 48 h for first review, 24 h for re-review. Reminders at 24 h and 48 h, then escalate by phone.
- For chronic non-responders, use an assumed-approval clause after a set number of business days.
- Cap revisions at 2 rounds. Three or more rounds point to a gap in kickoff or template.
- Use "approved with noted changes" for small keyword swaps. Production continues.
- Strategic changes (new audience, new angle) need a full revision, not a conditional approval.
- Lock the brief after approval. Keyword changes after approval undermine the whole step.
- One client contact consolidates SEO feedback. Senior people get a one-page summary with the primary keyword and the action needed.
