# AI agent interviews are a workflow fix, not a content strategy

## Key points

- Expert content often stalls because the expert has no time for an interview. This is an operations problem, not a writing problem.
- An interview agent can collect expert answers asynchronously and ask follow-up questions.
- Good follow-ups ask for customer examples, trade-offs, exceptions, and what competitors get wrong.
- Ask a second question when the expert makes a claim without an example.
- Expert content usually fails at intake, not in the prose.
- Do not publish a raw transcript. Experts use shorthand, overstate certainty, and may mention customer details that must stay private.
- Keep a human approval gate. The AI interviewer alone is not enough.
- Track four layers apart: what the expert said, what AI inferred, what the writer drafted, what the expert approved.
- Roles: the expert owns the substance, the writer owns argument and voice, the editor owns standards.
- Measure input quality (specific examples captured, fewer review cycles, claims actually approved), not output volume.
- Start small: one recurring format and one expert.
