# 20 Ways Agencies Can Adapt To Evolving Client Needs

- URL: https://www.forbes.com/councils/forbesagencycouncil/2026/10/12/20-ways-agencies-can-adapt-to-evolving-client-needs/
- Publisher / author: Forbes Agency Council, expert panel of 20 agency leaders
- Published: 2026-10-12
- Source type: agency-owner panel (short statements, paid council membership)

## Key points

- Tools can produce huge lists of recommendations. The agency's value is to prioritize the few that move results (Brian Reilly, BankBound).
- Clients now use AI to question agency recommendations before they hear them. Bring judgment and clear opinions, not only data (Vin Sonpal, CS Web Solutions).
- Clients shift from "What are you doing?" to "What outcomes are we getting?" Do not sell activity (Nital Shah, Mavlers).
- Compete on judgment that the algorithm does not have (Mitchell Leiman, iPromote).
- AI makes publishing easy, but brand expression drifts. Agencies stay valuable by protecting brand coherence (Goran Paun, ArtVersion).
- Compliance and visibility pull in different directions. The agency must hold the line on both (Peter Boyd, PaperStreet Web Design, law-firm sites).
- Clients want proof of influence beyond traffic: AI citations, brand mentions, assisted revenue (several panelists).
- Clients want to be described accurately in AI answers, not only mentioned.
