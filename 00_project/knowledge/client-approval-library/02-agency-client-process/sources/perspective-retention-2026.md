## Key points

- Plan the first 90 days on purpose. Early churn is high in services.
- Build an early win into onboarding.
- Run quarterly reviews with the real decision-makers: last goals, results, business changes, next plan.
- Send short value reviews between quarterly meetings.
- Do not let the relationship depend on one day-to-day contact. Hold periodic owner-level check-ins.
- Ask for the reasons behind satisfaction scores. A high score can hide a coming cut.
- Follow up on vague answers instead of accepting them.
- Track first-90-day churn and a client health score, plus qualitative signals.
