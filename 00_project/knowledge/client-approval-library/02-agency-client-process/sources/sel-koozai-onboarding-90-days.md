# What effective SEO client onboarding looks like: The first 90 days

- URL: https://searchengineland.com/effective-seo-client-onboarding-first-90-days-492666
- Publisher / author: Search Engine Land, Kelly-Anne Crean (Head of Operations, Koozai, SEO agency)
- Published: 2026-10-02
- Source type: agency practitioner article with a client example

## Key points

- Most client problems start with how the partnership is set up, not with the SEO work.
- Ask early: how the business measures success, what it tried before, who owns website, content and development, what the client expects at month 1, 3 and 6.
- Document everything from the kickoff, send it to the client for confirmation, and store it where both sides can see it.
- The agency owns strategy, recommendations, delivery and communication. It flags issues early, not at the next report.
- The client owes access (analytics, Search Console, CMS) and business context (offer, customers, priorities).
- Name one person or team as the go-to for sign-off on content and recommendations, with a reasonable response time.
- Case: the client's developers had little capacity and fixed release dates. The agency moved low-effort changes first and placed large ones into the client's roadmap.
- A roadmap shaped by the client's real capacity gets more buy-in than an ideal list.
- Report business outcomes first. Rankings and traffic are context.
- Technical work is invisible to the client. Without a steady update rhythm, the client assumes nothing happens.
- Never promise a result or timeframe before you know the start point, implementation speed and market.
