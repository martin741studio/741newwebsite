# Google Business Profile's New 4-Day Deadline: What Clinics Need to Know

- URL: https://propelyourcompany.com/google-business-profiles-4-days/
- Publisher / author: Propel Marketing & Design (clinic marketing agency), with a podcast episode (The Clinic Marketing Podcast)
- Published: 2026-09-24
- Source type: agency blog + podcast, health-practice niche

## Key points

- Members of the public can suggest edits to a Google Business Profile.
- When a suggested edit needs owner review, the owner has 4 days to accept or reject it.
- If the owner does not respond, Google may publish the change when other public sources (such as the website) support it.
- Some suggested edits may go live without any owner review.
- Fields at risk include name, address, phone, hours, website, categories, services and appointment links.
- The clinic should keep ownership of its profile. Agencies get appropriate access, not ownership.
- Someone must monitor the email account linked to the profile. Four days is short.
- Keep the website and the profile consistent. Google uses the website to check edits.
- After a move, rename, new phone or rebrand, list every place that needs an update.
- A profile is not "set and forget". Review it on a schedule.

## Relevance for 741

- Google itself uses a short "silence = may publish" window. This supports fast reminders for GBP items, and it means the portal must alert on external suggested edits within hours, not days.
