# Standardizing Approval Deadlines: An Agency Framework

- URL: https://blog.postly.ai/how-to-standardize-approval-deadlines-without-making-every-client-the-same/
- Publisher / author: Postly Blog (tool vendor), Paul Onu
- Published: 2026-08-08
- Source type: vendor blog, agency operations

## Key points

- Standardize the logic of deadlines, not one fixed duration for all clients.
- Put each client into a tier at onboarding: agile (24-48 h), standard (3-5 days), regulated (10-14 business days).
- Healthcare, finance and other regulated clients sit in the slowest tier. Plan the review lag into the calendar as a fixed feature.
- Each tier has a defined "failure mode" for missed deadlines. Do not invent a new reaction each time.
- Regulated tier: a missed deadline moves the item to the next cycle. No emergency approvals.
- Standard tier: a "silent approval" clause applies, but only if the contract defines it.
- Late feedback that forces big rework after the deadline can trigger an "out-of-cycle" fee.
- Do not ask the client "How much time do you need?". Present the tiers as the agency's operating system.
- Validate every item internally (format, dimensions, fit) before the client sees it. This removes back-and-forth.
- Review the last 3 months of approvals to find which clients are always late, then re-tier them.
- Quote: "standardize the logic of your deadlines, not the duration."

## Relevance for 741

- Health and law clients are "regulated" by this model. A 7-day auto-approval is short for that tier, so the contract and onboarding must state it clearly.
