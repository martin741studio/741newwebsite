- Publisher / author: Mohr Marketing (law-firm marketing agency)
- Published: 2026-10-05
- Source type: agency blog, US bar rules (German law differs; use only the process logic)

## Key points

- The firm names one responsible attorney for legal questions and sets an escalation path.
- The agency documents each item: channel, asset, audience, claims, and evidence for the claims.
- The agency prepares the material. The firm's reviewer judges legal issues only.
- Before publication, the reviewer approves the specific version. The team saves the evidence, reviewer name and review date.
- The agency publishes only reviewed versions and tracks later edits.
- An approval is not permanent. Re-review when claims, targeting, landing pages or rules change.
- Defined handoffs say who owns each step.
- The firm keeps legal judgment and professional responsibility. The agency supports execution but does not give legal advice.

## Relevance for 741

- For law and health clients, some items must not auto-approve: claims about results, fees, or treatments need an explicit yes from the named professional. Store version, approver and date for each approval.
