# Extract: How agencies involve clients in decisions without hurting results

Area 02: agency-client process. 14 sources, published 2026-07-15 to 2026-10-12.
Research date: 2026-10-13. Freshness rule: only sources from 2026-07-13 or later.

Strength: "2+" = two or more independent sources support it. "1" = one source only.
Most sources are agency blogs or tool-vendor blogs. Vendor numbers are directional, not proven.

## A. Presenting options

1. **Show one clear recommendation plus at most one or two real alternatives.** The alternatives must differ in substance, not only in style. If all variants look equally polished, the client sees no recommendation.
   Sources: storyflow-presenting-concepts, forbes-agency-council-client-needs. Strength: 2+

2. **Develop the recommended variant more fully than the others.** Show alternatives in less detail and say why they rank lower.
   Sources: storyflow-presenting-concepts. Strength: 1

3. **Do not show rejected or killed options in the decision view.** Keep them in the record with the reason. Showing them invites the client to bring them back.
   Sources: storyflow-presenting-concepts. Strength: 1

4. **Give a judgment, not a menu.** Tools produce long lists. The agency's value is to filter and rank them. Clients now test agency advice with AI, so the reasoning must be visible in one line.
   Sources: forbes-agency-council-client-needs, sej-voltage-content-roadmap, agencydashboard-month-six-churn. Strength: 2+

5. **End every item with the one decision needed and what happens next.**
   Sources: storyflow-presenting-concepts, sej-studiohawk-first-90-days. Strength: 2+

## B. SEO lock and pushback

6. **Decide keywords before the client sees text variants.** Put the target keyword into the brief or item. The client approves wording, not the keyword.
   Sources: contentify-brief-approval-seo-targets, sej-voltage-content-roadmap. Strength: 2+

7. **Lock a decision after approval.** Changing the keyword after approval undermines the step. A later change is a new item, not an edit.
   Sources: contentify-brief-approval-seo-targets, mohr-bar-compliant-review (re-review when claims change). Strength: 2+

8. **Separate small from strategic changes.** Small wording swaps get "approved with noted changes" and work continues. A change of direction becomes a full new round.
   Sources: contentify-brief-approval-seo-targets, storyflow-presenting-concepts. Strength: 2+

9. **Answer "it doesn't feel like us" with the reasoning, not with taste.** Show the path from the data or insight to the variant. Ask which layer fails: strategy, idea or wording.
   Sources: storyflow-presenting-concepts. Strength: 1

10. **Treat GBP name, categories and address as locked, high-risk fields.** Keyword names, unrelated categories and rapid edit bursts can trigger suspension. One named person approves edits before they publish.
    Sources: goodfellas-gbp-suspension-edits, propel-gbp-4-day-edits. Strength: 2+

11. **Pace GBP changes.** Do not push many approved GBP edits at once. Rapid changes raise trust flags.
    Sources: goodfellas-gbp-suspension-edits. Strength: 1

## C. Approval stages, deadlines, silence

12. **Run internal quality checks before the client sees anything.** The client only sees pre-checked, valid variants.
    Sources: postly-approval-deadline-tiers, sej-voltage-content-roadmap. Strength: 2+

13. **Name one approver per client.** Others may comment, but one person decides. Map the approver at kickoff.
    Sources: sel-koozai-onboarding-90-days, sej-studiohawk-first-90-days, contentify-brief-approval-seo-targets, goodfellas-gbp-suspension-edits, mohr-bar-compliant-review. Strength: 2+

14. **Agree response windows at onboarding, in writing.** Present them as the agency's standard process, not as a negotiation.
    Sources: postly-approval-deadline-tiers, contentify-brief-approval-seo-targets, sel-koozai-onboarding-90-days. Strength: 2+

15. **Silence-as-approval needs a contract clause and client acknowledgment.** Without it, auto-approval is not defensible.
    Sources: postly-approval-deadline-tiers, contentify-brief-approval-seo-targets. Strength: 2+

16. **Regulated clients (health, law) need longer windows and explicit approval for claims.** Content with claims about results, treatments or fees should not auto-approve. The named professional approves the specific version.
    Sources: postly-approval-deadline-tiers (10-14 business days for regulated tier), mohr-bar-compliant-review. Strength: 2+
    Note for 741: the 7-day auto-approval is fine for design and wording items. Exclude claim-bearing items (HWG, lawyer professional rules) from auto-approval.

17. **Remind early and on a fixed rhythm.** Sources use reminders at 24 h and 48 h, then escalation. Our 3-day reminder is on the slow side of this range.
    Sources: contentify-brief-approval-seo-targets, postly-approval-deadline-tiers. Strength: 2+

18. **Alert on Google's own deadlines within hours.** Google gives owners 4 days to reject suggested GBP edits, then may publish them. External suggested edits must reach the client and Martin fast.
    Sources: propel-gbp-4-day-edits. Strength: 1

19. **Log every approval with version, approver and date.** Store objections and answers too. The log settles later disputes and reopened decisions.
    Sources: mohr-bar-compliant-review, storyflow-presenting-concepts, contentify-brief-approval-seo-targets. Strength: 2+

20. **Cap revision rounds.** Two rounds is the common cap. Three or more rounds point to a problem in the brief or kickoff.
    Sources: contentify-brief-approval-seo-targets, postly-approval-deadline-tiers (out-of-cycle fee for late rework). Strength: 2+

## D. Collecting client content and expertise

21. **Collect expertise through short, specific questions, then approve claims only.** Ask for examples, exceptions, trade-offs. Ask a second question when a claim has no example. The expert approves substance; the agency owns style.
    Sources: kenashe-expert-interview-intake, mohr-bar-compliant-review (reviewer checks compliance, not craft). Strength: 2+

22. **Keep four layers apart for E-E-A-T content:** what the expert said, what was inferred, what was drafted, what the expert approved.
    Sources: kenashe-expert-interview-intake. Strength: 1

23. **Tell the client plainly what they must supply and by when.** Hidden client workload stalls approved work.
    Sources: storyflow-presenting-concepts, sel-koozai-onboarding-90-days, sej-studiohawk-first-90-days. Strength: 2+

24. **The client keeps ownership of the Google Business Profile.** The agency works as manager. Someone must monitor the linked email.
    Sources: propel-gbp-4-day-edits, goodfellas-gbp-suspension-edits. Strength: 2+

## E. Reporting cadence and churn

25. **Make invisible work visible on a steady rhythm.** Silence reads as inactivity. Weekly or bi-weekly short updates in a fixed format work best early on.
    Sources: sel-koozai-onboarding-90-days, sej-studiohawk-first-90-days, agencydashboard-month-six-churn. Strength: 2+

26. **Interpret, do not dump data.** Lead with the business outcome, then the evidence. Each update says what changed, why it matters, what comes next.
    Sources: agencyplatform-seo-client-dashboards, sel-koozai-onboarding-90-days, forbes-agency-council-client-needs. Strength: 2+

27. **Flag wins at once.** Send a short note when a milestone happens. Do not wait for the monthly report.
    Sources: agencydashboard-month-six-churn, sej-studiohawk-first-90-days. Strength: 2+

28. **Watch approval speed as a churn signal.** Delayed approvals and shorter replies predict churn. Most churn decisions form in the first 90 days, often in month 2-3.
    Sources: sej-studiohawk-first-90-days, agencydashboard-month-six-churn, perspective-retention-2026. Strength: 2+

29. **Set realistic timelines in writing at the start.** SEO movement takes about 3-6 months. Never promise a result before you know the start point and the implementation speed.
    Sources: agencydashboard-month-six-churn, sel-koozai-onboarding-90-days. Strength: 2+

30. **Shape the plan to the client's real capacity.** Do the low-effort items first and schedule the heavy ones. A realistic roadmap gets more buy-in than an ideal list.
    Sources: sel-koozai-onboarding-90-days, sej-studiohawk-first-90-days. Strength: 2+
