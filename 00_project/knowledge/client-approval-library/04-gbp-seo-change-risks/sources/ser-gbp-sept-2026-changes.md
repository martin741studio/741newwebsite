## Key points

- Verification: Google seems to test a fifth verification method, "Submit a business photo". The photo must show the business name on a permanent sign and the full storefront in one frame.
- The other methods are phone/text, email, live video and postcard. Video is now common for new and re-verified profiles.
- Hours: Google tests a feature where the owner describes hours in plain text and AI converts the text into a schedule. The article does not say if the owner must confirm the result.
- Support policy: Google added "Misuse of support channels" to the GBP policies. Floods of invalid requests, false reports and repeated unfounded complaints are misuse.
- Consequence: Google can reject all further complaints from that user without review.
- Map pins: a bug placed pins of some service-area businesses in the ocean. The service area itself looked correct. Google gave no statement.

## Relevance for 741

- A business sign with the exact profile name helps verification. A name change in the profile without a matching sign is a risk.
- AI-converted hours need a human check after save.
- The agency must not send many support requests or appeals for one problem. Repeated requests can block all future support for that account.
- Pin position needs monitoring. A moved pin can change local rankings even when nobody edited the profile.
