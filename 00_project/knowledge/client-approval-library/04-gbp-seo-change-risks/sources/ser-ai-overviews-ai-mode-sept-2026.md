## Key points

- Google is turning the local knowledge panel (the GBP panel) into an AI Overview with a "Show more" link into AI Mode.
- Barry Schwartz saw outdated facts about his own company in that AI panel. He traced them to old content on his website.
- Google tests links at the bottom of AI Overviews that open AI Mode instead of a website. This can reduce clicks to sites.
- AI Mode with Gemini 3.8 Flash showed few or no source links at launch. Google called this a bug and fixed it the next day.
- Search Console now has a generative AI performance report worldwide (since 31 August 2026). It shows impressions, pages, countries and devices, but no clicks or queries.
- Search Console also has a new control to opt a site out of AI features (AI Overviews, AI Mode, Discover AI Overviews).

## Relevance for 741

- The website is now a direct source for the AI version of the GBP panel. Old facts on the website (old hours, old team, old services) can show up in AI summaries.
- Website and GBP data must match. A change in one place needs the same change in the other.
- The AI opt-out control is a high-risk setting. Only the agency should touch it.
- The new AI report gives a baseline for AI visibility before and after changes.
