# SEO Title Length Is Longer Than You Think (Sterling Sky)

## Key points

- Sterling Sky advises against the common "under 60 characters" title rule.
- Joel Headley added neighborhood names to title tags on thousands of healthcare sites. Visibility rose by about 15 percent.
- A personal injury lawyer added the target keyword to the title (232 characters). The page moved from position 6 to 4.
- One client title had 229 characters and still performed well.
- The method is to add relevant location and service terms, not to stuff keywords.
- The post does not test keyword removal or reverts.

## Relevance for 741

- The title tag is an active ranking lever for local health and legal pages. Keyword and location terms in the title have measurable effect.
- The reverse is a reasonable inference: removing the keyword or location from a title can cost rankings. This is not tested in the source.
- An "SEO lock" on the title keyword fits this evidence. The client can change the wording around the keyword.
