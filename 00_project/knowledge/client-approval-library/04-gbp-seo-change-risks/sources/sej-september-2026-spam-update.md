# Google completes September 2026 spam update after nearly 14 days (Search Engine Journal)

- URL: https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-september-2026-spam-update-complete/592283/
- Published: 2026-10-08, Matt G. Southern
- Fetch date: 2026-10-13

## Key points

- The September 2026 spam update started on 24 September and finished on 8 October 2026.
- It applied globally and to all languages.
- It took about 13 days and 16 hours. Earlier 2026 spam updates (March, June, August) took less than three days each.
- Google did not say what the update targeted.
- The last broad core update in 2026 was the May update (about 21 May to 2 June).
- Early data from Diib (32,569 sites): the median share of unpaid Google visits fell from 34 to 31 percent. The share of sites with a drop of half or more rose from 5 to 10 percent.
- SEOmonitor saw false ranking changes in rank trackers from 26 September. Real search results did not show them.
- Advice: compare Search Console data before, during and after the rollout window. Trust drops that last several days and show in clicks and impressions.

## Relevance for 741

- Google ran four spam updates in 2026 so far. Update windows are frequent. A change calendar must mark them.
- A ranking dip during an update window is not proof that a client edit caused it.
- Rank trackers can show noise. Confirm drops in Search Console before any rollback.
