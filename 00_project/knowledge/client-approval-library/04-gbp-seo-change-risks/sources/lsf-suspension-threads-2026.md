## Key points

- The forum help section shows a steady flow of suspension threads from July to September 2026. Topics: "deceptive content", disabled service-area businesses, failed verification, address problems.
- Thread 1: a profile with a visible home address was suspended for "content violation". No edit preceded it.
- An expert reply said Google re-checks older listings. A home-based business that shows its address can be flagged even after earlier verification.
- Advice: appeal first with documents. If the appeal fails, switch to a service-area profile with a hidden address.
- One reply claimed that "new July 2026 rules" allow only one appeal. The thread does not prove this.
- Thread 2: an account-level restriction blocked GBP access for a former lead-gen agency owner. Two appeals were auto-denied.
- Whitespark advised a new account with no connection to the old account. Account trust affects all profiles managed by that account.
- Thread 3: a website URL change showed "accepted" in the dashboard but did not appear on Maps for about a day. It then fixed itself.
- Older 2026 threads (outside this window) report suspensions after a category switch and after edits to services.

## Relevance for 741

- Suspensions also hit profiles with no recent edit. Address type and eligibility are the base risk.
- The agency's Google account is a single point of failure. If it gets restricted, all client profiles it manages can be affected.
- "Accepted" in the dashboard does not mean "live". The workflow must confirm the change on Maps and Search.
- Appeals are scarce. Prepare documents before the first appeal.
