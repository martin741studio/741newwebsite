# Edit your Business Profile + Understand what happens to your Business Profile edits (Google Business Profile Help)

- URL 1: https://support.google.com/business/answer/3039617?hl=en
- URL 2: https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038311?hl=en
- Type: Google official help pages (living pages)
- Fetch date: 2026-10-13 (counts as current)

## Key points

- Google states that a name change after verification may require re-verification.
- Google states that a move to a new address after verification requires re-verification.
- Category edits can also trigger re-verification. You can choose one primary category and up to nine more.
- Google reviews every edit before it goes live. The edit status shows as Accepted, Pending or Not approved.
- Most reviews take up to 10 minutes. Some reviews take up to 30 days.
- Google can reject an edit when it cannot confirm the accuracy of the change. You can appeal some rejected edits.
- The website field needs the full URL. Googlebot must be able to crawl that URL.
- The description has a limit of 750 characters. URLs and HTML are not allowed.
- Google can call, text or WhatsApp the verified phone number to confirm details. The answers can update the profile.
- Google can update a profile itself when it receives reports that the information is wrong. The owner gets a notification.

## Relevance for 741

- Name, address and category edits are "re-verification triggers". During re-verification the client can lose edit access and the agency can lose time.
- A pending edit can take up to 30 days. The approval workflow must track "submitted" and "live" as two separate states.
- Edits from Google itself or from users can change the profile without an agency action. Regular monitoring is necessary.
