# Google Posts cracks down on unverified contact information + Updated Google Maps policy on what merchants can't post (Search Engine Roundtable)

- URL 1: https://www.seroundtable.com/unverified-contact-information-google-posts-42159.html (published 2026-09-25, Barry Schwartz)
- URL 2: https://www.seroundtable.com/google-maps-policy-listings-or-posts-42228.html (published 2026-10-06, Barry Schwartz)
- Fetch date: 2026-10-13

## Key points

- Google updated two Google Posts help documents in September 2026.
- Posts with unverified contact data (phone numbers, email addresses, social handles) can be removed.
- Google allows only contact data that it can verify as belonging to the business.
- Additional phone numbers belong in the profile edit form, not in post text.
- Hiroko Imai, who found the change, advises using the post buttons ("Book", "Learn more") instead of contact data in the text.
- In October 2026 Google also updated the "advertising and solicitation" section of its Maps content policy.
- The listing itself must not contain promotional content such as limited-time deals, prices or direct calls to action.
- Posts must not contain emails, phone numbers, social links or outside links that are not related to the business. Normal offers and updates in posts stay allowed.
- Neither article describes the penalties. Earlier forum reports say repeated rejected posts can lead to restrictions.

## Relevance for 741

- Client-written posts are a medium risk. A phone number or an email in the post text can lead to removal.
- Promotional text in the profile description or name is not allowed. Health clients must also follow HWG rules on promotion.
- Posts need a short pre-check: no contact data in text, no prices or deals in the listing fields, link only to the client's own site.
