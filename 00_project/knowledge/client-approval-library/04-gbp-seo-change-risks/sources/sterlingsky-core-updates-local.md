# Google's 2025-2026 Core Updates Are Destroying Local Businesses (Sterling Sky)

- URL: https://www.sterlingsky.ca/google-core-algorithm-updates/
- Published: 2026-09-30, Joy Hawkins
- Fetch date: 2026-10-13

## Key points

- Sterling Sky audited many local sites in the last three months. Some lost about 90 percent of organic traffic.
- Joy Hawkins calls the December 2025 core update one of the most dramatic updates for local businesses.
- Small businesses no longer have "immunity" from core update damage.
- Correct analysis: use 16 months of Search Console data, filter out branded queries and filter out the GBP landing page.
- Most hit sites had an earlier "warning" drop (June 2025 core update or August 2025 spam update).
- Google can apply a site-wide classifier. It has no Search Console message, and it suppresses nearly all organic pages.
- The organic classifier does not suppress Local Pack rankings. Some businesses kept or improved map rankings after a 90 percent organic loss.
- Main cause in the audits: keyword-rich anchor text in backlinks, including old links built years ago and press release links.
- With a classifier in place, small edits such as new title tags do not help. Recovery is slow, and Sterling Sky has seen no recovery yet for 2025 cases.

## Relevance for 741

- Organic losses and Maps losses can have different causes. The monitoring must track both separately.
- A ranking drop after a client edit is not always caused by the edit. Check the update calendar and the link profile first.
- Link building and press releases with exact-match anchors are a high risk. They need agency control, not client control.
