# Google Business Profiles gives you four days to reject user edits + "Does this look right to you" email (Search Engine Roundtable)

- URL 1: https://www.seroundtable.com/google-business-profiles-4-days-42139.html (published 2026-09-23, Barry Schwartz)
- URL 2: https://www.seroundtable.com/gbp-does-this-look-right-to-you-email-42030.html (published 2026-09-08, Barry Schwartz)
- Fetch date: 2026-10-13

## Key points

- Google updated its help document with a new section about notifications for suggested edits.
- Users on Google Maps can suggest changes to a profile, for example a new business name.
- Google notifies the owner about some suggested edits before they go live.
- The owner has four days to accept or reject the suggestion.
- If the owner does not respond, Google may publish the edit when other public information (for example the website) supports it.
- Google may apply some user edits without prior review by the owner.
- A new email format ("Does this look right to you") asks owners to mark a suggested change as correct or incorrect. The example was a change to the business title.

## Relevance for 741

- Not all profile changes come from the client or the agency. A user edit can change the name, hours or category of a client profile.
- The four-day window is short. The agency needs a daily or near-daily check of profile notifications.
- The website is the evidence Google uses. If the website shows old data (old hours, old name), Google can push that old data back into the profile.
- The approval workflow should also cover "rejected or accepted third-party edits", not only client requests.
