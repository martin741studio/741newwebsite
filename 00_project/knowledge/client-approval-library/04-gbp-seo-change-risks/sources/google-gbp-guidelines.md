# Guidelines for representing your business on Google (Google Business Profile Help)

- URL: https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en
- Type: Google official help page (living page)
- Fetch date: 2026-10-13 (counts as current)

## Key points

- The business name must match the real-world name on signage, stationery and the website. Extra words such as service terms, taglines, cities or phone numbers are not allowed.
- Google says unnecessary information in the name "isn't permitted". Keyword additions fall under this rule.
- A minor name change that keeps the proper nouns, services and category is a normal edit. A bigger rebrand needs a closed old profile and a new profile.
- The address must be a real, staffed location. P.O. boxes, remote mailboxes and virtual offices are not eligible. Co-working spaces qualify only with signage and staff on site during hours.
- Service-area businesses must hide the address. The service area should normally stay within about two hours' drive of the base.
- Hours must be the regular customer-facing hours. Appointment-only businesses should not list hours. Holidays go in special hours.
- Phone and website must represent the individual location. They must not redirect to other places, for example a social media page. The business must control the phone number directly.
- Categories: use the fewest categories that describe the core business. Choose what the business "is", not what it "has". Categories must not act as keywords.
- The description must be honest and relevant. No links, prices, promotions or gimmicky text.
- Practitioners (doctors, lawyers) can have their own profile. Support staff and lead-generation agents must not create profiles.
- Third parties that manage profiles must notify the owner and get consent for some edits (for example a menu URL).
- Violations can lead to suspension, changed information or removal from Google. Google can also suspend access to other Google services.

## Relevance for 741

- The name, address, category and phone fields carry the highest policy risk. A client edit to these fields needs agency review before it goes live.
- Health practices and law firms often have practitioner profiles. A wrong profile setup can put the main listing at risk.
