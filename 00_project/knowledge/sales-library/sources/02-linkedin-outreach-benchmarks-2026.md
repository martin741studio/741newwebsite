## Findings (ranges differ by source; the measured dataset is the floor, the vendor claims the ceiling)
- Connection acceptance: measured 16.4 % across 11,701 requests (Danish Lead Co); vendor benchmarks 25–35 % for cold B2B, 30–37 % average, 40 %+ good; sustained below 20 % = targeting failure or account risk (LeadRiver).
- Reply rate on messages after acceptance: measured 14.4 % of accepted (7.9 % of all messages); vendor 10 % average cold, 15–25 % well-run; marketing agencies 12–20 % (sbl.so); messenger campaigns 16.86 % (Expandi).
- Conversation from request: measured 2.36 % end to end; 83.6 % of the funnel is lost before a message is sent (Danish Lead Co).
- Meeting conversion: 1–3 % of total sends (SalesCaptain), 2–5 % of total messaged (sbl.so), 10–20 % of conversations progress to a meeting (LeadRiver); one case: 26 % reply on 200 sends, 30 % of replies to discovery calls.
- LinkedIn reply rates run about double cold email (10.3 % vs 5.1 %, Expandi).
- The expensive stage is acceptance, not messaging; personalisation of the first touch moves acceptance more than message copy moves replies.

## Marketing and not transferable
- Every source sells outreach tooling or an outreach agency; the measured dataset is 10 clients in one quarter.
- No source covers German local health and law practices; use the ranges as a sanity band, 741's own numbers as the truth.

## Usable for 741
Weekly funnel line per outreach person: requests → accepted (band 16–35 %) → replied (band 8–20 % of messages) → review page sent → call booked (band 1–5 % of messaged). A person below 20 % acceptance two weeks running = targeting or account check, not more volume. The 100k model's "605 messages per deal" is consistent with the measured floor.
