# 21 Hormozi leads and service business extract

Date: 2026-11-03. Scope: sales rules for a small agency Sales Director. The agency sells 90-day local-SEO programmes at 4.999 EUR and 7.999 EUR through LinkedIn DM outreach, review pages, Loom and a 60-minute call.

Files read (folder 00_project/knowledge/hormozi-library/, file numbers only):
- 019: $100m Leads PDF (warm outreach, cold outreach, Rule of 100, referrals, employees, roadmap)
- 064: live consulting transcript (pricing and sales calls)
- 079: live consulting transcript (offer and capacity)
- 099: live consulting transcript (lead sources and subscription economics)
- 065: retention
- 012: sales and lead parts only
- 078: Read. No usable sales rule.

## Findings

- **IF outreach output is low, THEN contact at least 100 people daily across several channels. Src: 019, "Contact at least a hundred people daily"
- IF you run outreach, THEN plan for about one in five contacts to engage. Src: 019, "one in five contacts will engage"
- IF a cold lead does not answer, THEN follow up several times through different channels. Src: 019, "Engage lead contacts multiple times through varied communication methods"
- IF you need fast growth, THEN set 100 actions per day for 100 days. Src: 019, "take at least 100 actions daily over 100 days"
- IF each rep has a daily target, THEN give them a one-page checklist to run it. Src: 019, "committing to a single-page advertising checklist"
- IF your warm list is empty, THEN start with your network and an easy, free-to-accept offer. Src: 019, "Start with an enticing, easy-to-accept offer"
- **IF output drops, THEN check hiring and turnover before you change the script. Src: 019, "sales strategy wasn’t the problem"
- IF hiring is slow, THEN interview candidates in groups, not one by one. Src: 019, "interview candidates in groups"
- IF you add a new rep, THEN document the steps, demonstrate them, then watch the rep work. Src: 019, "Write down the steps required to perform tasks"
- IF you track rep productivity, THEN divide payroll by engaged leads each month. Src: 019, "Total Payroll / Total Engaged Leads"
- **IF a deal closes, THEN ask for a referral at that moment. Src: 019, "Ask Right When They Buy"
- IF you ask for referrals, THEN reward both the referrer and the new client. Src: 019, "Reward both parties involved in the referral"
- IF a satisfied client knows others, THEN ask them to refer those people to you. Src: 019, "Encourage satisfied contacts to refer others"
- IF you choose a lead source, THEN rank referrals first. They brought the most clients. Src: 099, "our highest is referral"
- **IF the owner reviews the team, THEN read one rep's full conversations in depth each week. Src: 064, "weekly I would go deep with one of them"
- IF DM outreach converts poorly, THEN audit the rep's DM conversations line by line. Src: 064, "audited any of their DM conversations"
- IF the team needs more booked calls, THEN let reps book calls and let the owner or closer sell. Src: 064, "SDRs BDRs booking my calendar completely"
- IF a setter books calls by messenger, THEN keep the setter role separate from the closer role. Src: 079, "reach out to people via social media messenger"
- IF outreach volume is the bottleneck, THEN start each day at the platform's send limit. Src: 064, "start every day by maxing out the platform limits"
- IF a discovery call opens, THEN ask why the prospect booked now. Src: 064, "Why'd you book this call today"
- IF the sale takes two calls, THEN give the quote on the first call to aim for one close. Src: 064, "change to a one call close"
- IF a prospect needs context before the call, THEN send a short video before it, as 741 does with Loom. Src: 064, "add a video sales letter before the call"
- IF you set a price from cost, THEN check the claim "five or 10x that cost" against real delivery hours. Src: 064, "five or 10x that cost"
- IF a client prepays in full, THEN offer a 10% discount for the prepayment. Src: 064, "if you prepay all ahead"
- IF a client may change scope, THEN put a price-lock clause with a limit on the change. Src: 064, "not changing the headcount by more than 20%"
- IF you design a payment plan, THEN set the first payment with conversion in mind. Src: 079, "what matters more for the conversion is the first payment"
- IF you add a bonus to an offer, THEN each bonus must be worth the price of the whole offer. Src: 079, "each thing we add on its own should be worth the price"
- IF a call ends with a next step, THEN book the next date before the prospect leaves. Src: 079, "ask them when they're going to come back"
- IF capacity per rep is fixed, THEN calculate the maximum client count from sessions per week. Src: 079, "that means that you'd max out at 60"
- **IF a client leaves, THEN ask why. Overwhelm ranks first after price. Src: 065, "number one reason people churn outside of price is overwhelm"
- IF churn rises, THEN track new clients against cancellations each month. Src: 065, "track those two numbers on a monthly basis"
- IF monthly retention is below 70%, THEN fix retention before you add more sales. Src: 065, "less than 70% retention"
- IF a client raises a price objection, THEN offer a lower tier, not a deeper discount. Src: 065, "add a lower tier or a standard plan"
- IF a new client starts, THEN deliver a visible win within 24 hours. Src: 065, "ideally within the first 24 hours"
- IF the owner is absent from client work, THEN retention falls, so the owner checks in daily. Src: 065, "showed up in the community on a daily basis"
- IF the first months are weak, THEN focus on onboarding, because churn drops with time. Src: 065, "churn drops dramatically with time"
- IF churn is unknown, THEN call lost customers and ask why they left. Src: 012, "get on the phone with"

## What is marketing fluff in these sources

- The gym-software claim of "$10 million a month" in 019 comes with no conversion rates or test design.
- The 019 summary gives no conversion rates, so no outreach benchmark can be taken from it.
- The "80% monthly retention" platform average in 065 comes from one community with no sample size given.
- The "Rule of 100" and "Open to Goal" frames in 019 motivate. They do not show tested results.
- Event, Vegas, and "marriage insurance" pitches in 064 and 079 sell the speakers' own events and roadmaps.

## Not transferable to 741 and why

- Volume and capacity numbers (100 DMs daily, five sessions per day, the 60-client cap) assume a consumer funnel. Test them before use. Check LinkedIn's current limits first.
- Event sponsorships, in-person events, and Vegas retreats need a physical event business. 741 sells remotely by Loom and a 60-minute call.
- SMB subscription economics (450 USD monthly, churn bands, "drop low, go high") fit monthly retainers, not fixed 90-day programmes at 4.999 EUR and 7.999 EUR.
