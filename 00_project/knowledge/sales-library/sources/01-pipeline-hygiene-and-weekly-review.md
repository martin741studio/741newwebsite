## Findings
- Three questions a pipeline review answers: do we have enough, is it moving, is it real (quality, hygiene, age). — ORM
- A metric becomes a KPI only with a target and a response plan ("velocity tracked weekly with a 15 % deviation threshold that triggers a deal review"). — ORM
- Stale rule: a deal in one stage longer than 1.5× the average goes on a watch list; two weeks on the watch list = out of the forecast. — ORM
- Every open deal needs a scheduled next action with a date, no exceptions; in the weekly ritual force a decision on stale deals: advance with a confirmed next step or close lost. — SalesHive
- Weekly review, 30 minutes, same day, same agenda, 3 to 5 priority deals, written commitments; 60-minute all-deal status walks are the failure mode. — Sybill
- Five questions per priority deal: what has the buyer done since last review; what could kill this deal; what evidence supports the close date; what do you need from me. — Sybill
- Coverage ratio floor 3×, standard 3–5× of the target; if you close 25 %, you need 4× coverage. — ORM, SalesHive
- Weekly velocity tracking correlates with 87 % forecast accuracy vs 52 % irregular (Digital Bloom 2025, via ORM). — ORM
- More than 10 % of pipeline untouched in 12 months is normal and must be cleaned; total coverage without context is the most misleading number. — ORM
- Weekly at rep level, monthly at leadership level; when any metric trends wrong two weeks straight, act. — Axis
- Keep the dashboard on one screen: four headline numbers, trends against the 90-day average, a hygiene row that must be resolved by end of week. — ORM

## Marketing and not transferable
- All four are vendor blogs (RevOps tooling, SDR agency, consultancy); the 87 % figure is second-hand.
- Dollar-weighted velocity and 50+ open opportunities assume a bigger pipeline than 741's; the ratios and the ritual transfer, the dashboard tooling does not.

## Usable for 741
The Monday brief = the three questions; every prospect row needs a next-action date (the CRM sheet already has NAD and the red mark); stale = 1.5× the median stage age, two weeks stale = out of the count; the weekly card reviews 3–5 priority prospects with the five questions, not every row.
