# Hormozi closing and sales extract

Files read (all in 00_project/knowledge/hormozi-library/): 009, 072, 003, 010, 013, 007, 112, 117. Files 014 and 015 skipped as duplicates.

## Findings

- **IF your close rate is high, THEN raise your prices until more prospects say no. — Src: 072, "if you're closing at 80% you should raise your prices"
- IF you double the price and your close rate falls 25%, THEN your margin still grows. — Src: 072, "your close rate cuts by 25%"
- IF you never review your prices, THEN review them every quarter, because unreviewed prices earn less. — Src: 072, "people who never look at price least profitable"
- **IF you sell onboarding, THEN bill it in the first month, when the client feels the most pain. — Src: 007, "charge more for the first month and include the onboarding"
- **IF your LTV is short, THEN fix retention and delivery before you raise the price. — Src: 007, "three and a half months is not super long"
- IF clients pay monthly, THEN offer annual billing, because annual churn was far lower in the source. — Src: 007, "if you build annually it's 2%"
- **IF a prospect answers vaguely, THEN ask "tell me more about that" and "can you give me an example". — Src: 072, "tell me more about that"
- IF you want the prospect to buy, THEN spend most of the call on discovery of their pain. — Src: 072, "the discovery process is the most important part of the sale"
- IF a prospect has many reasons to buy, THEN find the one reason that matters to them. — Src: 072, "most buyers are single issue voters"
- IF you run a demo, THEN show one proof point, not every feature. — Src: 072, "the only reason to have a demo"
- IF you qualify a prospect, THEN ask BANT on the first call: budget, authority, need, timing. — Src: 072, "ask bant questions budget authority need timing"
- IF prospects are cold on the call, THEN add education before the call, at the lowest-cost step. — Src: 072, "jam as much education as I possibly can"
- IF you show proof, THEN use recent proof, because recent proof beats old proof. — Src: 072, "recent proof is more valuable than 10 years ago"
- IF you show proof, THEN choose cases closest to the prospect's avatar and outcome. — Src: 072, "someone who's identical to you as the prospect"
- IF cost per lead rises but close value rises more, THEN keep the costlier source. — Src: 072, "are you trying to get the cheapest leads"
- IF prospects book calls but do not buy, THEN fix the offer, not the script. — Src: 072, "it's usually an offer issue"
- IF calls do not start yet, THEN keep the outreach volume until calls start. — Src: 072, "keep going until you get them on the phone"
- IF your offer is easy to compare, THEN win on speed, risk or ease. — Src: 013, "you need to win on one of these three vectors"
- IF a prospect fears a loss, THEN cover that fear with a guarantee or insurance. — Src: 013, "get an insurance policy on whatever their biggest fear is"
- **IF a prospect doubts your result, THEN deliver a free first result and bill after it is seen. — Src: 013, "we'll just get you one two or three for free"
- IF you get too many unqualified leads, THEN add qualification steps, even if lead cost rises. — Src: 072, "more friction to your sales process"
- IF you offer an upsell tier, THEN price it near 5x the base, and expect 20 to 30 percent uptake. — Src: 013, "I'm going to 5x the price relative to the base"
- IF demand is higher than capacity, THEN limit spots with cohorts or waiting lists. — Src: 072, "if you want to increase demand cut supply"
- IF your market is broad, THEN narrow to one niche, because narrow offers support higher prices. — Src: 072, "it is much easier to win in a narrow pond"
- IF you have no clients yet, THEN give the service away free to early clients for proof. — Src: 072, "give service away for free"
- IF you hire salespeople, THEN ask for a 30 to 60 second video before any interview. — Src: 072, "send me a video that's 30 or 60 seconds"
- IF you run group tryouts, THEN check coachability: can the candidate change right after feedback? — Src: 072, "can they take feedback and then immediately change"
- IF a salesperson depends on your lead flow, THEN pay a base plus a variable split, because results are not fully theirs. — Src: 072, "some element of their success is not up to them"
- IF your team needs sales skill, THEN train them with recorded sales videos before books. — Src: 009, "the actual videos I put out there"
- IF outreach feels draining, THEN expect the emotional strain to exceed the mental strain, and plan for it. — Src: 003, "the emotional part is the hard part"
- IF you learn outreach alone, THEN join a peer group that does the same work. — Src: 003, "I promise you'll learn 100 times faster"
- IF you sell a service with proof, THEN publish a risk-free, outcome-based guarantee. — Src: 117, "a risk-free outcome-based guarantee"
- IF progress feels slow, THEN measure it at 30 and 90 days, not day by day. — Src: 010, "you look back at 90 you're like"
- IF you choose a market, THEN pick a thriving market before you tune the offer. — Src: 112, "choosing a thriving market"
- IF LTV to CAC is low, THEN improve the back end before you cut lead cost. — Src: 013, "LTV is the sustainable competitive advantage"

## What is marketing fluff in these sources

- Personal stories about bills, dogs, meals and daily routines (009, 010, 072).
- Motivational quotes from Frankl, Marcus Aurelius and Twain, used as mindset (010).
- Revenue flexes and famous examples such as Henry Ford and Goldman Sachs (072, 013).
- Plugs for the free scaling roadmap, workshops and Vegas events (009, 007, 013).
- Generic talk about reframing reality and a "good day formula" (010).

## Not transferable to 741 and why

- Paid-ad and high-risk niche tactics (peptides, cannabis, banned ads): 741 sells through LinkedIn, review pages and calls, not paid ads.
- Consumer tiers, large testimonial counts and large team scale: 741 runs two programmes with a small client base.
- Membership and subscription churn maths (14,000 subscriptions): 741 sells fixed 90-day programmes, not monthly memberships.
