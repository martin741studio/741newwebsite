## Findings
- Primary output metric = meetings booked that the closer accepts; everything else is an input (Growigami, Vector).
- Daily inputs per person: 10–20 LinkedIn touches, 5–8 conversations; outputs 3–5 meetings per week, 12–18 per month for a full-time SDR (Growigami).
- Diagnose a variance from target as activity, skill or process before coaching (UpliftSales).
- Weekly 30-minute 1:1 per person: 5 min check-in, 10 min metrics and the biggest variance, 10 min one recording or thread with specific feedback, 5 min top 3 priorities and one commitment (UpliftSales).
- Coaching 1:1 and pipeline check-in are separate meetings; "how many meetings did you book" turns coaching into a status update (SalesHive).
- QA scorecard with 5–6 dimensions: opener, qualification questions, objection handling, listening, clear next step; calibrate scorers; one improvement per session (SalesHive).
- Activities per meeting booked is the cleanest efficiency number; track it monthly per person (Vector).
- Speed-to-lead for inbound: respond within five minutes, track the median not the average (Growigami).
- Measure coaching in three layers: output (meetings), input (scorecard scores), retention/burnout (SalesHive).
- Dashboards show target next to actual and balance activity with outcome; weekly reviews turn data into coaching, monthly-only turns it into filed scorecards (Vector).

## Marketing and not transferable
- All are SDR-agency or tooling blogs; dial counts (40–60 per day) are phone-SDR numbers, 741's team works LinkedIn DMs only.
- 741's outreach people are part-time or Upwork; the 1:1 and the scorecard are Martin's, the agent only prepares the numbers and the one variance.

## Usable for 741
Team line per person in the Monday brief: touches, conversations, review pages sent, calls booked, activities per booked call, and ONE variance diagnosis (activity / skill / process). The agent never coaches the team; it hands Martin the one-line diagnosis and the recording or thread to look at.
