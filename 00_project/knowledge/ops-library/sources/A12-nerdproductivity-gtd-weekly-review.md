## Key points (paraphrased)

1. The weekly review resets clarity and brings every list up to date. It takes 60 to 90 minutes.
2. Consistency beats length: fix one weekly slot.
3. Six steps: collect, process, review projects, review calendar (past and next), review someday/maybe, set the week's "rocks".
4. Daily review is a quick check. The weekly review is the full pass.
5. Skip it for two weeks and inboxes and projects drift.
6. Use one trusted inbox for all inputs.
7. Every active project needs a concrete next action.
8. Three rocks per week is the right number.

## Use for 741

The weekly review is where parked cards, stale client goals and the next week's three rocks get handled, so the daily grill can stay short.

Short quote: "Three is the right number for weekly rocks."
