## Key points (paraphrased)

1. Weekly, 90 minutes, at a fixed day and time with a fixed agenda. Start and end on time.
2. Agenda in minutes: segue (5), scorecard (5), rock review (5), headlines from customers and staff (5), to-do list (5), IDS (60), conclude (5).
3. Scorecard: 5 to 15 weekly numbers. Owner reports on-track or off-track, no discussion. Off-track items drop to the Issues List.
4. To-dos are 7-day actions. Done items drop off. Open items carry once more. Nothing stays longer than two weeks. About 90% should drop off each week.
5. IDS: Identify the root cause, Discuss, Solve for good (often creates new to-dos). Take the top three issues by priority, not in list order.
6. Each attendee rates the meeting 1 to 10. Target 8 or higher.
