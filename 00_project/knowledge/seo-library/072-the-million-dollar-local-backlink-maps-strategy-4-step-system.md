## Source guide (NotebookLM)

To improve Google Maps visibility, businesses can implement a four-step local backlink strategy that acts as a strong ranking signal once customer reviews and on-page keywords are optimized. The process begins by mining competitor data using SEO tools and AI to identify existing local connections, which are then prioritized by importance starting with authoritative groups like Chambers of Commerce, followed by trade directories, nearby neighborhood organizations, and smaller community charities. Finally, companies execute a value-driven outreach playbook by offering sponsorships or meaningful contributions to secure these links affordably. This targeted approach generates significant return on investment by capturing more local calls and converting them into profitable customer jobs with minimal financial outlay.
