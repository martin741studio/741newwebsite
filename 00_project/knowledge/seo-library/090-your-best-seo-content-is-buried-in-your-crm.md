# Your Best SEO Content Is Buried in Your CRM

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FZJTSdTlepM

## Source guide (NotebookLM)

To maximize local search engine optimization, home service businesses should transform the authentic field data collected by field crews into website copy rather than relying on generic marketing content. By gathering geolocated project photos, customer inquiries, and crew audio summaries, companies can build highly specific landing pages that prove their local expertise to search engines. Establishing a repeatable content handoff system allows teams to consistently feed real-world project details into their web pages and external profiles, ultimately signaling genuine local authority to modern search algorithms.

Topics: Local SEO content; Crew job details; Geolocated project photos; Google business profile; Repeatable content systems
