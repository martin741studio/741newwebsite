# Google Maps SEO at 5, 50, 500 & 5,000 Reviews

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfoaS1Amd8g

## Source guide (NotebookLM)

Scaling a local home service business on Google Maps requires a customized SEO framework that evolves through distinct operational milestones, ranging from initial legitimacy to complete regional dominance. At the baseline stage of five reviews, companies must establish verifiable existence by securing a physical address and optimizing basic profile details. As businesses reach fifty reviews, the focus shifts to compounding keyword strategies through targeted on-page content and aggressive review generation to capture local search traffic. Once a profile accumulates five hundred reviews, owners should strategically expand to a second location by analyzing customer data and geographic gaps to overcome the limitations of distance. Finally, companies scaling toward five thousand reviews must transition from isolated profile management to a repeatable multi-location operating system that standardizes branding, reviews, and tracking for widespread market control.

Topics: Google Maps SEO; Review milestones; Local ranking strategy; Multi-location expansion; On-page optimization
