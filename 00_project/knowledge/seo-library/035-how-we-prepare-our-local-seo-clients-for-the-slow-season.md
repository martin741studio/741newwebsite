# How We Prepare Our Local SEO Clients For The Slow Season

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Hd_JrQwZoE

## Source guide (NotebookLM)

Multi-trade home service contractors facing seasonal downturns can maintain steady winter revenue by launching targeted Google Business Profiles focused on high-ticket, year-round services like sewer and drain repair. Rather than altering established primary categories on existing accounts—which risks losing current search visibility—contractors should analyze CRM data and consult field technicians to identify lucrative geographic pockets with aging infrastructure. Once an optimal physical office location is established within this target area, businesses must record a continuous, unedited video verification featuring official documentation, branded gear, and operational equipment to secure Google's approval. Finally, newly verified profiles are successfully ranked in weeks rather than months by building localized landing pages, optimizing services, acquiring consistent reviews, and monitoring performance through regular grid scans.

Topics: Google Business Profile; Local SEO Strategy; CRM Data Mining; Video Verification Process; Slow Season Preparation
