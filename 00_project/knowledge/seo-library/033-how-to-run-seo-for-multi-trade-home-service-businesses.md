# How To Run SEO For Multi Trade Home Service Businesses

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B7G1V63XNX0

## Source guide (NotebookLM)

For multi-trade home service contractors struggling to rank for all of their core offerings, a high volume of positive reviews and a great website are not enough to guarantee visibility in local search results if the Google Business Profile primary category is misaligned. Because Google relies primarily on this category to determine a business's core trade, companies attempting to promote multiple services through a single listing will inevitably fail to appear for searches outside of their main designation. To overcome this limitation and capture missed demand, businesses must split their profiles by establishing separate, dedicated Google Business Profiles for each individual trade, supported by a distinct local landing page and consistent Name, Address, and Phone number data. Ultimately, the purpose of this strategy is to help contractors systematically optimize their local search presence across every specific service area so they can successfully drive targeted calls for all of their trades rather than just one.

Topics: Multi-trade SEO strategy; Google Business Profile; Primary business category; Local map pack; Separate profile creation
