## Assessment for 741

Job block anatomy he uses (7 parts): title · trigger (every weekday / when I drop X / each morning) · duties (3–4 verbs with numbers) · "do not" list (message, change live, spend, send, unsubscribe, forward, invent metrics) · approval rule ("leave an approval card for anything irreversible", "ask before any send") · persistence ("remember this") · Friday self-review (shipped / got wrong / needs from me / grade A–F with one sentence / "do not grade yourself higher to please me").

What is new vs. nothing (this is the baseline): the vocabulary "hire, title, manager, Friday review" and the ordered loop: one good day → save as skill (sources, output format, approval boundary) → propose a morning routine that stops for approval on anything external.

What is marketing: "comment HIRE" lead magnet; the compressed Monday is "example data, not real results" by his own disclaimer; "it took me hours, now minutes" is unmeasured; the robots.txt segment is news filler unrelated to job design; the "12-block system for Claude" is name-dropped without link or author (not locatable, see source 12).

What is thin: no manager definition beyond "me"; no definition of "irreversible"; no enforcement (the "do not" list is prose the bot may ignore); self-review is self-graded with no evidence requirement; no roster, no overlap rule between bots, no drift detection other than the weekly grade.

Usable for 741: the 7-part block as the minimum field list; the three-pile triage shape (needs reply today / can wait / ignore) for any inbox-like agent; "what you need from me" as a mandatory review section; "do not grade yourself higher to please me" as a literal line; the skill+routine promotion rule after one good day.
