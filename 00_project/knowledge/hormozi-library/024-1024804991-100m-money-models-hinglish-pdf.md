A Money Model Happened
A Money Model asal mein offers ka ek sequence hota hai. Iske core mein ek hi cheez hoti hai: customer ki problem ko har possible angle se solve karna, aur phir us solution ko offer karna. Isi wajah se Money Models usually ek specific order mein multiple offers rakhte hain. Agar aap sahi time par woh cheez offer kar do jab customer ko realize hota hai ki usey uski need hai, toh aap jitni chaaho utni offers de sakte ho.
Rental car company ka Money Model simple language mein yeh tha: pehle vehicle upgrade ka option, phir late return, uske baad premium insurance, phir minimum insurance ka downsell, aur end mein prepaid gas. Bas itna hi.
Haan, maine zyada pay kiya, lekin saath hi meri zyada problems solve hui. Unhone mere “big man in a small car” problem ko solve kiya by offering a bigger, more spacious vehicle. Unhone mere “late checkout” concern ko address kiya by giving flexibility to return the car later. Unhone mere “car scratch ya ding ho jaane ki tension” ko insurance ke through solve kiya. Aur unhone mere “flight miss ho jaane ke risk” ko prepaid gas ke option se solve kiya, taaki mujhe return ke time fuel bharwane ki tension na ho.
Aur yeh saari problems ka solution tha jiske liye main khushi-khushi paisa dene ko ready tha. Rental car company ne har chhoti nuance ke baare mein socha hua tha. Pehle unhone mujhe problem ka ehsaas dilaya, phir uska solution mere saamne rakh diya. Unhone future mein aane wali badi fees aur hassles ko, present mein thodi si extra fees le kar solve kar diya.
Result yeh nikla ki meri $19 ki rental ek $100 ki rental ban gayi. Maine zyada paisa diya, aur faster diya. Aur ab yeh clearly samajh aata hai ki car rental industry sirf United States mein hi har month billions kyun kama leti hai. Yeh hota hai ek successful Money Model.

Beware: Bad Money Models businesses ko maar dete hain.
Bahut saare businesses ka problem yeh hota hai ki kisi ek customer ko buy karwane mein unka kharcha, us customer se hone wale profit se zyada hota hai. Simple words mein, naya customer lane mein hi unka loss ho jaata hai—and yeh ek bahut badi problem hai.
Phir usually yeh cycle start hoti hai. Pehle business customers laane ke liye paisa kharch karta hai. Month ke end mein realize hota hai ki jitna paisa aaya, usse zyada kharch ho gaya. Phir advertising kam kar dete hain. Customers kam aane lagte hain, kyunki ab afford nahi kar paate. Phir advertising bilkul band kar dete hain. Business ko personal cash, loans, ya credit cards se chalaane lagte hain… aur profit ke liye pray karte hain. Sirf lights on rakhne ke liye business ka kuch hissa bech dete hain. Mahino ya saalon tak wait karte hain apna paisa wapas aane ke liye—agar kabhi aaya toh. Dheere dheere aur peeche girte jaate hain. Aur end mein… sab kuch lose kar dete hain.
Lekin aisa hona zaroori nahi hai. Paisa market mein hai. Bas aapko sahi tareeke se lena aana chahiye.
Traditional business mein, bahut saare customers se thoda-thoda profit aata hai, jo milkar ek customer ki cost recover karta hai. Yeh “slow drip” business ko cash-starved bana deta hai. Iska matlab yeh hota hai ki aap tabhi zyada customers la paoge jab pehle se hi bahut customers hon. Kya aap fries bhi lena chahoge? – McDonald’s Famous Upsell

How Upsells Work
Jab ek offer kisi problem ko solve karta hai, to ek nayi problem saamne aa jaati hai. Tum us problem ka solution upsell karte ho jo tumhara offer reveal karta hai. Is tarah, har offer ek upsell ka door khol deta hai… hatta ki upsells bhi! Kaafi baar, upsells hi majority profit banate hain. Ye Money Model ko bana bhi sakte hain aur bigaad bhi sakte hain. Main tumhe dikhaata hoon ki kitna farq padta hai.

Agar wahi unka sirf ek offer hota, to unhe costs cover karne aur bas ek “living” nikaalne ke liye roz ~10,000 burgers sell karne padte. Good luck. Main ye basic (aur common!) example isliye dikhata hoon taaki ek baat clear ho jaaye—tumhara first offer hamesha profit nahi banata. Dusre shabdon me, jo cheez tum sabse zyada sell karte ho, zaroori nahi ki wahi tumhe sabse zyada profit de. Agar tum jeetna chahte ho, to tumhe apna version figure out karna hoga—“Do you want fries with that?” Agar tum nahi karoge, to koi aur kar lega.

Description

Main reason: current customers ke paas tumhari cheez kharidne ka chance strangers se hamesha zyada hota hai. Aur jab timing sahi hoti hai, customers khud hi upsell ho jaate hain.
The Classic Upsell ka base ye hai ki tum apne customer ki problem ke baare me unse zyada jaante ho. Aur hona bhi chahiye—it’s your business after all. Idea simple hai—tumhara core offer ek problem solve karta hai aur saath hi ek nayi problem create karta hai. Tumhara upsell turant us next problem ko solve karta hai. Bottom Line: Agar koi problem appear hoti hai, aur tum use turant solve kar sakte ho—in exchange for money—to do it!

How Not To Downsell—Ek real story mere ek friend se.
“Main ek car kharid raha tha aur salesman ne car insurance upsell karne ki koshish ki. Jab usne pehli baar insurance bataya, to cost $5,000 thi. Maine no bol diya. Phir usne price kam ki. Maine phir no bola. Wo price kam karta raha jab tak wahi insurance, jo pehle $5,000 ki thi, sirf $400 ki nahi ho gayi! Maine tab bhi no bola. Pehle maine isliye no bola kyunki paisa zyada tha, lekin end me maine isliye no bola kyunki mujhe us bande par trust hi nahi raha. Pura experience dirty laga. Phir mujhe laga, kya wo car ke case me bhi mujhe rip off kar raha tha? Ab main usse car bhi nahi kharidna chahta tha!”
Log sale close karne ke liye price kam kar dete hain. Lekin chahe tum ye ek sale close kar bhi lo, customer uske baad tumhare har price par doubt karega…aur jisko bhi wo batayega. Tum trust ke badle sirf ek buck kamaate ho. Worth it nahi hai.
Note: Tum kam price par kuch different offer kar sakte ho. Lekin same cheez ko kam price par offer nahi kar sakte. Agar usne same insurance sasti nahi, balki different insurance sasti offer ki hoti, to shayad uska trust bhi bana rehta aur sale bhi close ho jaati.
The Rules of Downselling
Yaad rakho, unhone is offer ko no bola hai, saare offers ko nahi. Kabhi-kabhi (kaafi baar) log no bol dete hain…aur ye OK hai. Sirf isliye ki unhone ye offer reject kiya, iska matlab ye nahi hai ki unhone tumhe reject kiya. Jab koi reject karta hai to bura lagta hai. I get it. Lekin ise waise dekho jaisa ye hai—ye ek opportunity hai samajhne ki unhe actually kya chahiye, aur
92
usse profit karne ki. Ret ke andar sar chhupane ke bajay, apni jagah par khade raho aur ek aur offer banao. No ka matlab is cheez ke liye no hota hai, sab kuch ke liye no nahi.
Downsells trades hote hain. Downselling me, tum customer ke saath milkar giving aur getting ke combinations dhoondhte ho jab tak match na mil jaaye. Agar tum kuch de rahe ho, to kuch lo bhi.
Personalize karo, pressure mat dalo. Samjho unhe kya pasand hai aur kya nahi. Phir jo unhe pasand hai uska zyada offer karo, aur jo pasand nahi hai uska kam—price ke saath match karke. Yahan tum personalize kar rahe ho. Agar koi meri large soda upsell ko refuse karta hai, to main alternatives offer kar sakta hoon. Main pooch sakta hoon ki wo small, juice, ya coffee chahte hain ya nahi. Kya main offensive ho raha hoon? Bilkul nahi. Actually, agar main unhe better serve kar sakta hoon aur phir bhi na karoon, to wo zyada offensive hoga.
Same cheezon ko naye tareekon se offer karo. Perfect world me, tumhare paas bechne ke liye bahut saari different cheezein hoti hain taaki sab kuch na kuch khariden. Real world me, downsells ko wahi tak limit rakho jo tumhare paas already hai. Warna tum hundred businesses jitne products (aur problems) bana loge. Ye silly choice hai. Isliye downselling ko aise socho jaise tumhare paas already jo cheezein hain, unhe offer karne ke hundred tareeke.
Sirf kisi ko buy karwane ke liye apni price mat girao. Sabse pehle, price girana actually downselling nahi hai, wo discounting hai. Agar kisi ko tumhari cheez chahiye, bas wo price pay nahi karna chahte—to tough cookies. Haan, tum unhe ye offer kar sakte ho ki wo abhi kam pay karein aur baaki paisa time ke saath—ek payment plan. Lekin jo bhi karo, sirf isliye price change mat karo kyunki tum moment me sale lose hone se dar gaye ho, kyunki…
Customers price ke baare me baat karte hain. Bilkul prices test karo. Pehle se plan karo ki tum apni cheez kis price par, kitne logon ko offer karoge. Ye bilkul alag baat hai, compare to moment me kisi ko sirf isliye sasta dena kyunki tum dar gaye the. Customers baat karte hain. Agar unhe pata chal jaaye ki kisi aur ko wahi cheez “bas aise hi” kam price par mil gayi—tum logon ko upset kar doge. Aur ye ethical problem bhi ban jaata hai, at least mere liye. Isse bacho.

Payment Plan Downsells
Aaj aap kitna amount down kar sakte ho?
August 2013. Ye mera business ka pehla real month tha. Mere paas exactly ek month ka rent savings me bacha tha… aur maine kabhi kisi stranger se paisa nahi liya tha. Aur ab mujhe agle kuch weeks me dozens strangers se paisa lena tha—sirf lights on rakhne ke liye.

Payment Plan Downsells price tag me kitne bhi zeroes ho, kaam karte hain. Maine inse tens of millions dollars banaye hain aur aaj bhi use karta hoon. Lekin payment plans ek gamble hote hain. Isliye tumhe pata hona chahiye ki inhe kaise use karna hai. Mujhe pata hai aur main tumhe exactly dikhaunga kaise.
Payment plans gamble isliye hote hain kyunki ye ek tareeke se paisa kama sakte hain, lekin do tareekon se paisa lose bhi kara sakte hain. Ye zyada paisa tab banate hain jab zyada customers milte hain aur wo apni payments complete karte hain. Ye kam paisa tab banate hain jab log profit aane se pehle cancel kar dete hain. Sabse zyada loss tab hota hai jab wo log, jo full pay kar sakte the, payment plan lete hain—aur early cancel kar dete hain.
Ye chapter maximize karta hai ki payment plans se tum kitna paisa kamao, aur minimize karta hai ki tum kitna lose karo. Main bet tab leta hoon jab mujhe pata ho ki main jeetunga. Is playbook ke saath, tum bhi le sakte ho.
Description
Jab zyada log “downsell” sochte hain, to unke dimaag me lower amount, lower quality, cheaper wagairah aata hai. Fair enough. Lekin main downsell karna pasand karta hoon same product ko dubara offer karke. Mujhe pata hai ye crazy lagta hai, but suno. Kisi different cheez ko offer karne ke bajay, main cost ko spread karta hoon—thoda paisa up front charge karke aur baaki scheduled payments me daal kar. Main ise Payment Plan Downsell kehta hoon. Chalo samajhte hain ye kaise kaam karta hai.
Bahut log offers reject karte hain kyunki “ye bahut mehenga hai.” Kabhi-kabhi ye sach hota hai. Lekin iske response me, business owners aur sales professionals turant discount ya cheaper cheez bech dete hain sirf haan sunne ke liye. Lekin kaafi baar “it costs too much” ka real matlab hota hai “ye abhi ke liye bahut mehenga hai.” Dusre words me, log sochte
96
hain discounts isliye kaam karte hain kyunki product sasta ho jaata hai. Lekin layer hatao to asli reason ye hota hai ki log us moment me kam pay karte hain. Isliye Payment Plans dono worlds ka best le aate hain. Ye zyada buyers laate hain kyunki customers moment me kam pay karte hain. Aur saath hi profits bhi badhate hain kyunki customers time ke saath full price pay karte hain.
Mera Payment Plan Downsell process maximum seven steps leta hai. Ye process zyada up front payment se shift hota hai zyada over-time payment ki taraf. Main tab rukta hoon jab wo buy kar lete hain. Ye rahe steps:
1. Paying in full ke liye reward do, over time pay karne par punish mat karo
2. 3rd-party financing, credit card, layaway options offer karo
3. Half now, half later offer karo
4. Check karo ki wo abhi bhi wo cheez chahte hain ya nahi
5. Three payments me split karne ka offer do
6. Evenly spread payments offer karo
7. Free Trial offer karo
Chalo inhe order me dekhte hain.
Example Of Payment Plan Downsell Process
Step 1) Paying In Full ke liye reward do, over time pay karne par punish mat karo. Jab main payment plan ka risk leta hoon, to main price badhata hoon. Normal businesses interest charge karke karte hain. Main alag tareeke se karta hoon—agar wo full pay karein to discount deta hoon. Socho businesses normally interest kaise charge karte hain—basically kehte hain… “Abhi lo to $10, lekin agar time ke saath pay karoge to $15, kyunki $5 interest lagega.” No fun. Iske bajay, main kehta hoon, “Price $15 hai…lekin agar prepay kar do to $10. Tum $5 save karte ho—zyadatar log yahi karte hain.” Iske liye main pehle interest-included price present karta hoon. Phir prepayment ko discount lene ka tareeqa banata hoon. Same math, par feel better hota hai. Agar wo no bolein, to main downselling start karta hoon. Lekin phir bhi, pehle paid hone ki koshish karta hoon…
97
Step 2) 3rd-party financing, credit card, aur layaway options offer karo. 3rd-party financing: Iska matlab koi aur company mujhe abhi pay karti hai aur customer us company ko time ke saath pay karta hai. Car dealers ye hamesha karte hain. Dealer ko aaj paisa mil jaata hai, aur customer financing company ko kal se pay karta hai. Note: 3rd-party financing set up karne me kaam lagta hai. Par effort ke bilkul worth hota hai.
Credit card: Bas poochho, “Tum chahoge main payment terms decide karun ya tum khud?” Wo kehte hain wo khud decide karna chahte hain. Tab main bolta hoon credit card use karo. Isse mujhe aaj pay mil jaata hai aur wo credit card company ko time ke saath pay kar sakte hain. Mujhe aaj bhi wild lagta hai ki ye reframe kaam karta hai. Par karta hai. Main judge nahi karta, main karta hoon.
Layaway: Layaway ka matlab product milne se pehle uska poora payment karna. Customers jitni chahein utni installments kar sakte hain. Reasonable time le sakte hain. Par product tabhi milta hai jab full payment ho jaaye. Ye unke liye sabse flexible aur humare liye lowest risk hota hai.
Agar wo in sab par bhi no bolein, to main step 3 par jaata hoon.
Step 3) “Half Now, Half Later” offer karo. Main pehle poochta hoon, “Next time tumhe salary kab milti hai?” Phir poochta hoon, “Aaj sirf half down kar do aur baaki jab salary mile tab?” Agar wo ye bhi nahi kar sakte, to main poochta hoon, “Aaj maximum kitna down kar sakte ho?” Jab wo koi amount bolte hain, main kehta hoon, “Great. Aaj wo amount down kar dete hain aur baaki jab tumhe salary mile tab. Fair enough?” Mujhe paychecks ke hisaab se payments schedule karna pasand hai kyunki zyada tar log har do hafte me paid hote hain. Isse 30-day profit monthly payments ke mukable kaafi zyada boost hota hai.
Agar wo ye bhi nahi kar paate…to main pause karta hoon taaki confirm kar sakoon ki wo sach me wo cheez chahte bhi hain ya nahi.
Step 4) Check karo ki wo abhi bhi wo cheez chahte hain ya nahi. Koi bhi payment plan us customer ko satisfy nahi karega jo cheez hi nahi chahte. Isliye pehle confirm karo ki person ko tumhari cheez chahiye bhi hai. Main kuch aisa bol sakta hoon, “Samajh gaya. Abhi paisa tight hai. Ek quick question. Scale of 1–10, tum isse kitna karna chahte ho?” Agar wo 8 ya usse upar bolein, to payment plans continue rakho aur bolo, “Awesome. Don’t worry. Hum koi na koi tareeqa nikaal lenge.” Agar wo 7 ya neeche bolein, to poochho, “10 kyu nahi?” Aur phir kuch aisa bolo, “Tum sahi ho. Shayad humare paas tumhare liye kuch better fit ho.” Phir unhe kuch different sell karo (Feature Downsells—thoda aage).

Step 5) Teen payments me split karne ka offer do. Agar unhone scale par 8–10 bola, to main half down se ek third down par downsell karta hoon. Main three-payment option deta hoon: ⅓ abhi aur ⅓ next do paychecks par—or—⅓ abhi aur ⅓ next do months me.
Step 6) Evenly spread payments offer karo. Agar wo phir bhi manage nahi kar paate, to main baaki service period me payments evenly spread kar deta hoon. Jaise, Gym Launch sixteen weeks ka tha, to main har week charge karta tha (total sixteen payments). Agar ye bhi problem create kare, to main step 7 par jaata hoon.
Step 7) Free Trial offer karo. Main Free Trials ek special tareeke se offer karta hoon. Isliye maine next chapter is par dedicate kiya hai. Par yahin sale khatam hoti hai. Kam se kam abhi ke liye.
Ye Payment Plan Downsell process maximum nine offers tak banata hai. Aur agar tumhe ye crazy lagta hai, to shayad tum abhi kaafi kam paisa kama rahe ho aur kaafi kam customers serve kar rahe ho, jitna tum kar sakte ho.
Important Notes
“Seesaw” Downselling. Agar tumhe fewer steps chahiye, ya tumhare salespeople less experienced hain, to tum ye payment plan downsell process use kar sakte ho. Full amount poochhne ke bajay, bas poochho, “Tum giant monthly payments chahoge ya tiny ones?” Wo tiny bolenge. Phir tum bolo, “Normally ye XXX ka hota hai. Aur agar aaj prepay kar do, to tumhe huge discount milta hai aur zero monthly payments. Ye work karega?” Isse payment plan negative frame hota hai aur prepaying ke benefits highlight hote hain. Phir agar wo bolein ki afford nahi kar sakte, to bolo ki jitna zyada wo abhi down karenge, utni kam unki monthly payments hongi. “Agar up front afford nahi ho raha, main samajh sakta hoon. Hum bas down payment adjust kar denge jab tak tumhe pasand ka monthly rate mil na jaaye.” Isse bigger down payments incentivize hote hain taaki monthly payments lower ho jaayein. Agar phir bhi wo no bolein, to poochho kya wo product abhi bhi chahte hain. Agar haan, to apni chair unki side kheench lo aur options unke saath walk through karo. Sale ek team effort ban jaati hai. Simple.
Payment Plans Have Built-In Upsells: Payment plan ke dauran periodic offers do original paid-in-full discount ke liye. Agar wo balance clear kar dete hain, to unhe abhi bhi original ‘prepay discount’ mil sakta hai. Ye exceptionally well kaam karta hai. Customers bhool jaate hain ki unke paas ye option hota hai. Jab hum dubara offer karte hain, kuch log turant le lete hain. Saath hi, apne sales logon ko bhi same bonus do agar wo balance close karwa dein, taaki follow-up incentivize ho. Aur yaad rakho, agar tum logon ko slow pay karne ka

option doge, to wo slow hi pay karenge. Agar tum unhe fast pay karne ka incentive doge, to wo fast pay karenge. To agar tum chahte ho ki wo faster pay karein, unhe strong reason do.
Get Fewer Declined Payments. Payment schedules ko paycheck schedules ke saath align karo. Jab tum un dinon charge karte ho jab logon ko salary milti hai, payment hone ke chances zyada hote hain. Aur logon ke paychecks different times par deposit hote hain, isliye agar pehli baar decline ho jaaye, to usi din thodi der baad phir se run karo. Maine ye strategy John (mere early mentor) se seekhi. Is chhote se process ko add karke main aksar apni declined payments ka lagbhag ek-third recover kar leta hoon.
How To Make Sure Payment Plans Make You Money. Payment plans implement karne ke baad, tumhara close rate badhna chahiye. Duh. Lekin agar paid-in-full payments ka number gir jaata hai, to problem hai. Matlab tum un logon ko payment plans par daal rahe ho jo full pay kar sakte the. Isliye goal ye hai ki overall zyada appointments close ho, lekin paid-in-full ka percentage same rahe. Ex: Agar main ten leads se baat karta hoon, to shayad teen sell hoti hain. Agar downsell add kar doon, to shayad teen aur sell ho jaayein (total six). Is second scenario me, pehle teen se up front cash milta hai aur baaki teen se payments aati hain. Isse ensure hota hai ki downsells sahi tareeke se 30-day profits badha rahe hain.
Another Reason To Start High Before Working Your Way Down. Profitwell (ek company jo subscriptions manage karti hai) ne 14,000 businesses ka churn data report kiya. Unhone ye valuable insight nikala ki billing cadence monthly churn ko affect karta hai. Monthly (12x per year) billing me 10.7% monthly cancellation rates the. Quarterly (4x per year) billing me 5% monthly cancellation rates the. Annual (1x per year) billing me 2% monthly cancellations the. Main pehle hi pricing ko most cash up front se least ki order me present karta hoon. Coincidentally, ye customers ko long term me bhi zyada valuable banata hai. Isliye high se start karo (fewer, bigger payments) aur phir neeche aao.

Clear kar doon—Continuity Offers aap kahin bhi aur kisi bhi tarah se bana sakte ho. Yeh naye customers attract kar sakte hain, existing customers ko upsell ya downsell kar sakte hain, ya purane customers ko re-engage kar sakte hain.
Saath hi, har cheez Continuity Offer ke liye sense nahi banati. Ek one-day workshop ke liye kisi se hamesha ke liye pay karwana silly hai. Wahan tab tak pay karna sense banata hai jab tak cost cover na ho—aur woh payment plan ho jaata hai. Isi tarah, kisi service ko hamesha ke liye dene ke liye ek single price (chahe bada hi kyun na ho) offer karna bhi aksar mistake hoti hai. Agar customers ko ongoing value mil rahi hai, to unka ongoing payments karna hi sense banata hai.

5. Attraction Offers customers ko attract karte hain by offering something free ya discount par. Often, ye higher price par better deal offer karke bhi money banate hain. Humne paanch cover kiye:
a. Win Your Money Back: Aap customer ke liye ek goal set karte ho aur batate ho kaise achieve karein. Agar wo achieve kar lete hain, to unhe money back ya store credit milta hai.
b. Giveaways: Aap ek big prize jeetne ka chance advertise karte ho contact information ke exchange mein. Winner choose karne ke baad, baaki sabko wahi prize discounted price par offer karte ho.
c. Decoy Offers: Aap free ya discounted offer advertise karte ho. Jab lead aur jaanne ke liye aata hai, tab aap ek zyada valuable premium offer bhi present karte ho. Ismein zyada features, benefits, bonuses, guarantees hote hain.
d. Buy X Get Y Free: Aap customers ko free stuff dete ho jab wo paid stuff buy karte hain. Jitna zyada free aur jitni zyada uski value, utne zyada log buy karte hain.
e. Pay Less Now or Pay More Later: Aap choice dete ho—ya to later full-price pay karo, ya abhi discounted price pay karo aur extra bonuses lo.
157
6. Upsell Offers wo hote hain jo aap next offer karte ho—usually jo abhi buy kiya uska more, better, ya newer version. Ye fast cash laate hain. Humne chaar cover kiye:
a. The Classic Upsell: Jaise hi customer apne next problem ko realize karta hai, aap uska solution offer karte ho. X ke bina Y nahi ho sakta.
b. Menu Upsells: Aap batate ho kaunse options unhe nahi chahiye, aur kaunse chahiye aur kaise value milegi. Tumhe wo nahi chahiye…tumhe ye chahiye.
c. Anchor Upsells: Aap sabse mehenga option pehle dikhate ho. Agar customer hesitate kare, to aap ek kaafi sasta par acceptable alternative offer karte ho. Agar X ki parwah nahi hai, to ye better fit ho sakta hai.
d. Rollover Upsells: Aap customer ke previous purchases ka credit next offer mein de dete ho. Kyunki aap pehle hi $500 spend kar chuke ho, main usko full year ke liye credit kar deta hoon.
7. Downsell Offers wo hote hain jo aap no ke baad offer karte ho. Nos ko Yes mein badal kar aap zyada money banate ho. Humne teen cover kiye:
a. Payment Plan Downsells: Same product, same price—bas payment timing change. Ab kab pay karoge? Half abhi aur half baad mein.
b. Trial With Penalty: Customers ko free trial dete ho jab tak wo aapke terms follow karein. Agar follow karein to paying customer banne ke chances badhte hain. Agar nahi karein, to pay karna padta hai. Agar tum X, Y, Z karoge, main tumhe free start karne dunga.
c. Feature Downsells: Aap price kam karte ho by changing what they get—lower quantity, lower quality, ya optional components remove karke. Agar guarantee ke bina theek ho, to main $400 kam kar sakta hoon.
