# How I Make Big Decisions Without Second-Guessing

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nNAJeMb5uc

## Source guide (NotebookLM)

When facing difficult choices burdened by competing desires, people often suffer from endless mental loops and later regret. To prevent this painful second-guessing, the speaker suggests recording all complete justifications in a written document immediately after making a choice. This acts as a warning letter to your future self, capturing vital negative details that nostalgia and time tend to erase. Ultimately, this strategy allows individuals to rely on past clarity during moments of weakness, saving them from wasting months repeating the exact same errors.
