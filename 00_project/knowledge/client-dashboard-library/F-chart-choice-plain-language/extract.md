## 2. 741 Dashboard-Raster (draft)

Scope: every client page in the portal and every report page. Rules are numbered R1 to R15.
Tag `741 decision` = we choose it, no source sets the number. Tag `unverified` = no source found.

| # | Rule | Reason | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | One question per section. The section title is the question in the client's words ("Do I get more enquiries?"). Do not mix status, plan, scans and tracking in one block. | A dashboard has one task: show critical information fast. Mixed jobs hide the answer. Bali roadmap mixes 7 jobs. | S05, S23 |
| R2 | Summary first. The top of every page answers the question in one screen. Detail sits below or on a second page. Use at most two levels: summary, then detail. No third level. | Readers stop at any point and still get the main point. More than two disclosure levels makes people lose their way. | S03, S07, S08 |
| R3 | At most 5 numbers on the first screen. Suggested slots: enquiries, top-3 share on the map, reviews, work done (x of y), things I need from the client. `741 decision` | Sources say "only what is important" and "size and position show importance". They give no count. 5 matches questions Q1 to Q5 in the inventory. | S04, S05, S03 |
| R4 | Every number and chart has a "so-what" line in plain words. Format: one sentence with the message ("You got 12 more calls than last month"). The exact data description goes in a small subtitle. | People need the message before the data. A headline plus a precise subtitle is the standard. Plain words in the title, precise words in the note. | S01, S13, S23, S26 (weak) |
| R5 | Count per week or month: line chart when there are 7 or more points. Column chart when there are 6 or fewer. A tiny trend line (sparkline) sits in each headline card. More than 3 series: one small panel per series, not one tangled chart. Put the latest value as a label on the line. | Length and position are read fastest. Lines show time well. Overlapping lines become unreadable. A sparkline fits next to a number. Small panels share the same time axis. | S05, S10, S14, S16, S01 |
| R6 | Share (%) and rank: sorted horizontal bars. Sort by value unless time or a natural order applies. No pie, donut, treemap or gauge. "x of y done" and score-versus-target use a thin bullet bar (one bar, one target mark, up to 3 grey bands). Bars start at zero. | Pie, gauge and area charts are read badly. Horizontal bars fit long names and phones. A bullet bar replaces a gauge in less space. Rank bars must be sorted. | S01, S05, S10, S15, S18 |
| R7 | Status, list, flow. Status: show an icon, a word and a colour at once. List: show a table only up to 7 rows. More rows: group them, show the top 5, link to the full list, paginate. Flow (outreach: requests, replies, offers, live links): one horizontal bar per stage with the count, in stage order. No Sankey. The 7-row limit and no-Sankey are `741 decision`. | A status needs two or more cues. Long tables spill off small screens. Simple charts come first, complex ones only for readers who want more. | S17, S03, S10 |
| R8 | Always show a comparison and the period. Every number gets one of: last period, start of the programme, or a target. Show the change with a sign (+12, -3) and an arrow. Write the date range ("30 Sep to 13 Oct"). The current period that is not finished says "so far" and is never compared with a full period. The "so far" part is `unverified`. | A number alone has no meaning. A change needs a "+" or "-" or an arrow, colour is optional. | S06, S04, S17 |
| R9 | Missing is not zero. Zero appears only when we measured zero. A value we do not have shows "Not measured yet" (DE: "Noch nicht erfasst") with the reason and the date we expect it. In a line chart the line has a gap. We never fill with an estimate and never use "NA". | A gap in the line stops a false story. A zero in a tooltip misleads. "NA" can mean two things. An empty chart should say why and what happens next. | S11, S11b (excerpt), S21, S19 (excerpt), S18 |
| R10 | Colour has one fixed meaning on every page. Green = done or on track. Amber = waiting, or needs your answer. Red = problem that blocks results. Grey = not started or no data. Blue = information only. Use red only for risk or a bad change. Never use colour for size of a value. | Fixed meaning builds trust. Colour does not show order or quantity. Carbon keeps the same colour roles for every status. | S17, S05, S18 |
| R11 | Accessibility. Never colour alone: pair each status with an icon and a word. Text contrast at least 4.5:1 (large text 3:1). Chart parts at least 3:1 against the background. Amber and yellow get a dark outline or dark text. At most 4 colours in one chart. Label lines and bars directly, not with a legend. Do not put red and green side by side without a second cue. Every chart has a one-sentence text alternative in the page, under the chart. | About 8% of men and 0.5% of women have colour blindness. Red, amber and green cannot all pass contrast on white. Direct labels remove the legend look-up. | S02, S12, S17, S20, S05, S01 |
| R12 | Plain words. No jargon on the first screen. Use the glossary in section 3. Say a technical term once, with the plain words next to it. No tool names. No internal text ("per SQL/Claude verknüpfen"). One sentence = one idea. Client language comes from `clients.lang`. | Plain language is the first rule for statistics. Precise words go to notes, not headlines. | S23, S13, S03 |
| R13 | Report length. A report page has a one-screen summary on top and then at most 3 detail sections. Target: at most about 3,000 px tall at 1440 px width. Longer content moves to a separate "Detail" page or an appendix. Today the Bali roadmap is 10,529 px. `741 decision` (the px number). | Summary first is the common structure. Sources say a summary is 5 to 10 % of the document, one to two pages. These sources are weak. Long scrolls hurt on mobile. | S03, S07, S25 (weak), S01 |
| R14 | Mobile order, 390 px. One column. Order: (1) the answer headline, (2) up to 5 numbers as cards, (3) what we did, (4) what we need from you, (5) detail. No horizontal scroll. Tables turn into cards. Use horizontal bars, not wide columns. A chart legend, if any, sits above the chart. Do not put key numbers below the fold. | The small screen makes people remember more and tap more. Content must be ranked. Bars grow downward, columns need width. | S09, S03, S10, S18 |
| R15 | Number, currency and date format follow the client language, one locale per page. DE: `1.250.000,50 €`, `42,3 %`, `13. Oktober 2026`. EN: `€1,250,000.50`, `42.3%`, `13 October 2026`. IDR has no decimals: EN `IDR 1,250,000`, DE `1.250.000 IDR`, ID `Rp 1.250.000`. Counts: no decimals. Percent and average rank: one decimal at most. Write the unit in the label. No "k" or "M" for German clients. Use "Mio." Convert IDR to EUR only with a stated rate and date. The conversion line is `741 decision`. | Decimal sign, thousand sign, symbol place and rounding all change by locale. "k" and "M" are not read the same everywhere. Mixed German and English labels are problem 3 in the inventory. | S22, S24 |

## 3. Jargon glossary (15 terms from the inventory)

Plain phrases are for a clinic owner. Use the EN column for English clients and the DE column for German clients.
Check each phrase with Martin before use. Rows marked `unverified` are my reading of how the term is used in our reports.

| Term | Plain English | Plain German |
|---|---|---|
| SoLV (share of local voice) | The share of the map where you show up in the top 3 results. | Der Anteil der Karte, auf dem Sie unter den ersten 3 Treffern stehen. |
| ARP (average rank position) | Your average place in the map results. 1 is the best place. | Ihr durchschnittlicher Platz in der Kartensuche. Platz 1 ist der beste. |
| SAIV (share of AI voice) | How often AI assistants name you when people ask about your topic. | Wie oft KI-Assistenten Sie nennen, wenn Menschen nach Ihrem Thema fragen. |
| NAP | Your business name, plus where you are and how to call you. It must read the same everywhere online. | Ihr Praxisname sowie Adresse und Telefonnummer. Überall im Netz muss alles gleich geschrieben sein. |
| DA (domain authority) | A 0 to 100 score for how strong a website's links look. It is a tool score, not a Google number. Do not show it to clients. | Eine Note von 0 bis 100 dafür, wie stark die Links einer Website wirken. Sie kommt aus einem Werkzeug, nicht von Google. Nicht an Kunden zeigen. |
| PBN (private blog network) | A group of websites built only to sell links. Google does not like this. | Eine Gruppe von Websites, die nur gebaut wurde, um Links zu verkaufen. Google mag das nicht. |
| reclaim (`unverified`) | Mentions of you on other sites that have no link to your site. We ask the site owner to add the link. | Erwähnungen von Ihnen auf anderen Seiten ohne Link zu Ihrer Seite. Wir bitten die Betreiber, den Link zu setzen. |

| SERP | The Google results page. | Die Ergebnisseite von Google. |
| CTR (click-through rate) | Of 100 people who see you on Google, how many click. | Von 100 Personen, die Sie bei Google sehen, wie viele klicken. |
| impressions | How often you appeared on someone's screen. | Wie oft Sie auf einem Bildschirm angezeigt wurden. |
| citation | An entry with your name, address and phone in an online directory. | Ein Eintrag mit Ihrem Namen, Ihrer Adresse und Telefonnummer in einem Online-Verzeichnis. |

## 4. Scorecard: 12 yes/no checks for a finished client page

Pass = all 12 "yes". One "no" blocks release. Test the page at 1440 px and at 390 px width.

| # | Check (answer yes or no) | How to test | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The first screen shows at most 5 numbers, and each section answers one question. | Count numbers above the fold at 1440 px. Read each section title. | R1, R3 |
| 2 | The page has a summary on top. Detail comes after. Nothing is more than two levels deep. | Read the top screen. Count click levels to the deepest detail. | R2 |
| 3 | Every number and chart has a one-sentence so-what line in plain words. | Scan each block for a message sentence. | R4 |
| 4 | Every number shows a comparison (last period, start or target) and a date range. | Check each card for "vs" and dates. | R8 |
| 5 | The unfinished current period says "so far" and is not compared with a full period. | Check the newest bar or point. `unverified` as a source rule, so judge by sense. | R8 |
| 6 | Charts match the shape: lines or columns for time, sorted horizontal bars for share and rank, no pie, donut or gauge. | Compare with the shape lookup table. | R5, R6 |
| 7 | Missing values show "Not measured yet" with a reason. Zero appears only for measured zero. No estimates. | Check each empty cell and chart gap against the data source. | R9 |
| 8 | Every status shows icon, word and colour, with the fixed meaning of green, amber, red and grey. | Grey-scale the page. Read all statuses. Check against the colour table. | R7, R10, R11 |
| 9 | Contrast passes: text 4.5:1 or more, chart parts 3:1 or more. Each chart has a text alternative. | Run a contrast checker. Check alt text under each chart. | R11 |
| 10 | No jargon from the glossary on the first screen. No tool names. No internal text. | Search the page text for the 15 glossary terms, tool names and "SQL". | R12 |
| 11 | The page is about 3,000 px tall or less at 1440 px. Longer content sits on a Detail page or appendix. At 390 px it is one column with no sideways scroll, answer first. | Measure page height. Resize to 390 px. Scroll sideways. | R13, R14 |
| 12 | One language and one number format on the whole page. Currency, decimals and dates all follow `clients.lang`. | Read every label, button, number and date. | R15 |
