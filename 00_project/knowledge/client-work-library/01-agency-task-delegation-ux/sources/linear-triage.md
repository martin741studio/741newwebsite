## Key points
- Triage is a separate inbox for new work. Work from integrations or from people outside the team lands here first.
- New items do not enter the normal workflow until a person reviews them.
- Four actions close a triage item: accept, decline (to Canceled), mark as duplicate, or snooze.
- To ask for more information, you comment and leave the item in triage, or you snooze it.
- A "triage responsibility" setting names who handles new items. It can notify or auto-assign that person, also on a rotation.
- Triage rules run top to bottom. They can set team, status, assignee, label, project, or priority.
- The team can require a priority before an item leaves triage.
- An AI feature suggests assignee and labels and finds likely duplicates. A human still accepts.
- Gaps / bias notes: Built for software intake (bugs, support tickets). Rules, AI and responsibility need paid plans. No client-facing view.
