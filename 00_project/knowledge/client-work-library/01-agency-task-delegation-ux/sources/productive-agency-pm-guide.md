## Key points
- Each piece of work has one owner, a date, and an estimate.
- Roles split by decision: the project manager owns order and dates, the account manager owns promises, the resource lead owns capacity.
- Keep status in one place with four fields: stage, owner, next action, date at risk yes or no.
- Do not rebuild status by hand for client decks. The copy goes stale and conflicts with the team view.
- A client portal is useful only when it reads the same record as the team.
- Send a short written weekly update to the client, also in quiet weeks.
- Plan from real available hours, for example 30 of 40. Treat about 90 percent utilization as a burnout warning.
- Watch four numbers weekly: utilization, budget burn against progress, estimate against actual, client health.
- Slow client replies and more feedback rounds are early warning signs.
- Gaps / bias notes: Vendor content that leads to its own tool. Built for larger agencies with billing by hours.
