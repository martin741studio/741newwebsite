## Key points
- Workload shows how busy each person is across projects. It uses capacity bars and task details.
- Managers set a maximum weekly load per person.
- Effort can be a task count, hours, points, or a custom field.
- A bar turns red when a person is over capacity.
- Managers drag tasks to change the owner or the due date.
- The view filters by person or project. It shows a day, week, month, quarter, half-year, or year.
- Workload opens from a portfolio. Only projects in that portfolio count.
- Gaps / bias notes: Marketing page, no date. Subtasks do not count by default. Paid plans only. Help-center pages did not load.
