# What can a client see on a project?
- URL: https://help.productive.io/what-can-a-client-see-on-a-project
- Type: Product help page (Productive, agency management tool)
- Published: living page, fetched 2026-10-13 (page says "updated over 3 weeks ago")
- Fetched: 2026-10-13
## Key points
- Clients see and work with tasks on projects where they are members. They can change status, dates and assignees when they have the permission.
- Clients can comment on tasks and on shared docs.
- Clients never see worked time logged on tasks.
- Budgets and time estimates are visible only to client leads on higher plans, with extra permission.
- Clients see the project activity feed, limited to items they have access to. They can subscribe to project notifications.
- The agency hides internal items by folder: a folder with "Client access" off is hidden with all its content.
- Custom client permission sets restrict one client without a change for other clients.
- A new client starts with an empty dashboard until the agency adds them to a project.
## Gaps / bias notes
- Help page. It gives mechanics, not design advice.
- It does not cover private notes inside a visible task.
