# Outreach numbers per account: how to enter them

From the go date, the portal records your numbers for each LinkedIn account. Example: Peggy's LinkedIn for Bali Healing. Then we can tell each client the exact numbers for their account.

## Morning plan

1. Write your plan as before. Use one line for each item.
2. Under "Targets — per account", enter two numbers for each account you will work today:
   • Requests target
   • Messages target
3. Leave an account empty if you will not work on it today.

## Evening report

Enter six numbers for each account you worked today:

• **Requests sent**: the connection requests you sent today from this account.
• **Accepted**: the new accepts today on this account. Look at My Network → Connections, or at the notifications.
• **Pitched**: the first messages you sent to new connections.
• **Follow-ups**: the second or later messages.
• **Replies**: the new replies today.
• **Interested**: the replies that want to talk or want an offer.

Rules:

1. Log in to each account and count its numbers alone. Do not add numbers from two accounts.
2. Leave an account empty if you did not work on it.
3. You do not enter totals. The portal adds the accounts and shows the totals on the board.
4. Hours, progress, blockers and "Short plan to start" stay the same as before.

## Gayo only: the account list

You keep the list of accounts in the portal.

1. Add an account with the name of the LinkedIn profile, for example "Peggy LinkedIn".
2. Write the client or campaign next to it, for example "Bali Healing".
3. Click the names of the people who work this account. A person sees only the accounts you give them.
4. Deactivate an account when the outreach stops. Its old numbers stay.
5. Do not rename an account to a new client. Add a new account instead.

## Client status

In "Over the days — per account", select the dates. Click "Status line" next to the account. The portal writes one line with the numbers for that client, and you can copy it.
