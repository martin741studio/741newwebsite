# me&you: journey concept after the to-top.ch model

Date: 2026-10-05. Status: concept only, nothing is built.
Reference: https://www.to-top.ch/en (Webflow, GSAP ScrollTrigger, Lenis, one 3D element). Raw HTML: `00_project/assets/meandyou/referenz-totop/index.html`.
Base for me&you: prototype v2, Copy Deck Round 3, Ehlers.pdf.

## 1. How the to-top page works

The page is one hike. The visitor is the hiker. The scroll is the route. A yellow dashed trail runs through all sections and connects them.

| # | Section | What it does for the visitor | Device |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | One promise and one question: "Where do you want to go?" | Round button that jumps to the next section. Media logos at the foot. |
| 2 | Signpost "Why are you here?" | The visitor chooses a direction: Stability or Future | A signpost with two signs, then two cards with typical concerns |
| 3 | "Are these your concerns? Climb the summit with us" | Four topics, each as one question | Four large cards that stack while you scroll. Each card is a mountain peak. A floating "Start program" button stays in view. |
| 4 | "On the way: offers" | The formats that take you there | Five cards laid out like gear on a blanket |
| 5 | Voices | Proof | Quote slider |
| 6 | The guides | Who walks with you | Two portraits placed in the landscape, then four bars "what makes us special" |
| 7 | "Fancy a taste?" | Small first steps without a commitment | Three stacked cards: reading sample, media, downloads and newsletter |
| 8 | "Let's climb new peaks together" | Call to action | The trail ends at an X mark |
| 9 | Summit | Emotional close: "Every journey begins with the first step." | Peak with a flag |
| 10 | Contact | E-mail, phone, booking link, short form | "Here we go!" |

### Why this flow works
1. **One metaphor from top to bottom.** Every section is a place on the same route.
2. **The visitor chooses early.** Section 2 asks why they came. After that, the page talks about them.
3. **A visible thread.** The trail line tells the visitor that the sections belong together and that there is an end.
4. **One question per card.** Each topic is a question the visitor can answer with yes.
5. **Small steps before the big step.** Section 7 offers a taste before the contact section.
6. **The guides come late.** First the visitor's problem, then the people.
7. **One floating button.** The main action is always in reach.

## 2. The concept for me&you

### The metaphor: "Der Faden"
to-top uses the mountain. me&you has a metaphor that is already in the brand: the thread. The Replayers quote in the Ehlers.pdf says: "Es sollte sich sicher anfühlen zuzugeben, dass man den Faden verliert."

- A thin line runs through the whole page.
- In the problem section the line is loose and tangled.
- In the method section it becomes straight, in three steps: Persönlichkeit, Kongruenz, Wirkung.
- It leads to the place (the seminar location) and ends at the conversation.
- Colour: dove blue or sand from the palette. Thin and quiet. It is not a cartoon trail.

The destination is not a summit. It is the room and the place. This fits the Copy Deck line "Der Ort ist Teil der Arbeit."

### What we take, what we leave
| Take from to-top | Leave out |
|---|---|
| One thread through all sections | 3D signpost, picnic blanket, gear illustrations |
| Early self-selection | Playful hand-written labels |
| Stacking cards for a sequence | Many parallax layers at once |
| A "taste" section before the booking | Dark green and yellow look |
| One floating booking button | Long page of 21 screens |
| Emotional close before the contact | |

The look stays me&you: quiet, warm, serif headlines, one idea per screen, photos with people.

### The choice in section 2 (the "journey around the user")
When the visitor picks a situation, the page remembers it for this visit.

| The page changes | Replayers | Overlooked | Undersold |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accent colour of the thread and cards | Dove blue | Navy | Anthracite and sand |
| First step offered in the taste section | Free training | Online course | Call |
| Seminar teaser photo | Calm, close | Room and group | Place at golden hour |
| Address | du | du | Sie (as approved) |

A visitor from an ad arrives on a landing page. There the choice is already made. The same logic runs with one persona only.

No choice is also valid. Then the page stays neutral, as it is today.

## 4. The journey around the user

| Stage | What the person thinks | Touchpoint | Next step we offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Notice | "That is me." | Ad or reel with one persona headline | Landing page of that persona |
| 2 Recognise | "They know my situation." | Hero and the three situations | Pick a situation |
| 3 Understand | "This is different from other trainings." | Method: three steps | Read on, or watch the video |
| 4 Trust | "He has done this for 30 years." | Michael, voices | Taste, or call |
| 5 Taste | "I want to try it first." | Free training, book, community | Online course, or call |
| 6 Decide | "I want to talk." | Booking section, calendar | 30-minute call |
| 7 Take part | "I am in the room." | Seminar at the location | Next format, community |
| 8 Stay | "I want more of this." | Community, further formats | Recommend, return |

The website covers stages 2 to 6. Ads cover stage 1. The call, the seminar and the community cover stages 6 to 8.
