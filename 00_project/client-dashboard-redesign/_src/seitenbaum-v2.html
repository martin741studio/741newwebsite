Why is core visibility only 1 of 7?

DataForSEO shows 72 keywords ranking for balihealing.com (Indonesia). That looks healthy until you check where they land: 79% (57 of 72) funnel through the homepage, and only 7 are actual named-service terms — the rest is generic "healing"/spiritual/brand noise. Three real reasons:

  • No blog / content layer at all. 58 live pages, zero articles. Every "how does it work / is it safe / benefits" question — exactly what AI Overviews and ChatGPT cite — currently has nowhere on the site to be answered.
  • Terminology mismatch on IV/infusions. Real demand runs on "IV drip" (390+ combined variants, CPC up to $33) — the site only says "infusions." Demand is being missed at the wording level, not the service level.
  • Zero comparison/aesthetic content. Nucleofill vs Rejuran/Profhilo, Microneedling vs Dermapen etc. — real commercial-intent searches, decent volume, currently unanswered anywhere on the site.

→ This is what the new Content branch (§12) and the Infusions terminology update (§10) fix. A fourth, separate issue — 18 duplicate/legacy pages diluting all of the above — is flagged in §14.
