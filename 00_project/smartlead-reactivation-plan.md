## 2. The hard constraints (don't skip)

1. **Subscription is expired** — you cannot send anything from Smartlead until you resubscribe. AI features (SmartAssistant / SmartAgents Reply Agent) are also gated behind that.
2. **Two campaigns were auto-paused for "bounce protection"** — the list already threw too many bounces *when it was fresh*. 16 months later a large share of those 22k addresses are dead. Blasting them again = instant domain reputation kill.
3. **The sending domain (join741studio.com) has been dormant ~16 months.** Reputation has decayed; it needs re-warming (or a fresh domain) before any volume.

## 3. Treat this as TWO assets, not one

### Asset A — Warm conversations (~few hundred "Interested" leads) → **the gold**
People who *replied positively* to a human. These convert with 1:1 re-engagement, **not** bulk email.
- Do NOT run these through a cold sequence. Reply **in-thread** ("wir hatten letztes Jahr kurz Kontakt wegen…").
- Can be done from a **normal mailbox (Gmail/domain inbox)** — no Smartlead needed, zero deliverability risk, and it looks personal.
- Export: Master Inbox → filtered to Interested → export leads/replies to CSV.

### Asset B — Cold list (~22k that never replied / openers) → **deliverability minefield**
- Cannot be reused as-is. Must be **re-verified & cleaned first** (MillionVerifier / ZeroBounce) — expect to lose 20–40%.
- Only send to cleaned addresses, from **warmed** domains, on a **re-permission / re-intro** sequence — never the old cold pitch.
