Formula used (from `.agent/PromptsforCopyandHeadlines.md`):
- **Headline** = [Emotional Dream Outcome] + [Functional Benefit + Timeframe] + [Unique Mechanism] + ([Proof Element])
- **Subheadline** = [Pain Point] + "the only [mechanism] that [how] to guarantee [benefit 1], [benefit 2], and [outcome] within [timeframe]"
- No em/en dashes. Only "&", commas, or "and".

> Timeframes for Slides 2 & 3 (45 days / 14 days) and all guarantee terms are PROPOSED. Confirm what you're willing to promise before this goes public.
