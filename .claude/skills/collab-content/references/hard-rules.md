# collab-content: Hard Rules (full text with incident history)

The body of SKILL.md holds the one-line version of each rule. This file holds the reasons and the incidents.

1. **Screen the given partner, do not replace it.** Assess the named partner on all six criteria and report the result, including a failed geographic test. The decision stays with Martin at Checkpoint 0. Local partners (client's town > nearby > region > same country with an audience link) are the standard; a nonlocal partner is written up with its real value and limits, not refused.
2. **Do not assume recommendation fit.** A shared wellness audience does not mean a partner wants to recommend the client's treatments. A traditional wellness philosophy does not imply support for IV therapy, ozone therapy or other modalities. When willingness is unclear, mark it **"requires partner confirmation"** and say what must be confirmed.
3. **Evidence, not invention.** Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences and unanswered questions. Cite the page that supports each assessment. Do not invent treatment benefits, credentials or brand values. Flag missing information.
4. **One fetch per URL.** Before any crawl, run `site_cache.py get <url>`. On HIT, read the cache. On MISS, crawl once and run `site_cache.py put`. Never fetch the same URL twice in one run, and reuse cached pages up to 30 days old.
5. **Honest SEO value.** Separate local audience relevance, organic and referral potential, and possible contribution to local visibility. Distinguish backlinks from local citations and from proximity. Never promise map-pack gains. Never call a nonlocal link's value "automatically zero"; explain its likely relevance and limits.
6. **Plain text in client-editable boxes.** No raw HTML tags or inline styles in article headings or body. Markdown headings, bold, bullets and `[anchor](url)` links only.
7. **Contextual links only.** No footer, sidebar or 1:1 homepage swaps. Every link sits inside 1,200+ words of useful editorial copy.
8. **YMYL compliance.** No cure claims. "Supports hydration", not "cures fatigue". Reliable sources for health statements; a partner's marketing copy is not medical evidence.
9. **Checkpoints are AskUserQuestion cards.** Checkpoint 0 (fit of the given partner), Checkpoint 1 (angle), Checkpoint 2 (copy). No drafting before Checkpoint 1. The skill ends at Checkpoint 2 with two linted Markdown files. What happens with them (CMS, sending) is outside this skill.
10. **Fact and claim check is blocking (added 2026-10-21, same standard as `content-writing`'s G1 facts gate + fresh-reviewer step).** Every number, duration, statistic, named credential or named practitioner in either article must trace to a cited source (the client baseline/review, the partner-fit file, or a cached page). No invented benefit, statistic or name. A fresh subagent with no drafting context checks both articles against the source dossier before Checkpoint 2 and reports correctness gaps only (not style). Below-5/5 slop or an unresolved fact gap blocks Checkpoint 2, same as a legal or fact block in `content-writing`.
11. **Reciprocal backlink check is blocking (added 2026-10-22 after a partner caught the gap before we did).** `lint_collab.py` must be run with `--client-domain <client-domain> --partner-domain <partner-domain>`. A "valid Markdown link" is not enough: `article_client.md` (published on the client's site) must contain a contextual link to the **partner's own domain**, and `article_partner.md` must contain one to the **client's own domain**. A link to a business's own pages from its own article does not satisfy this. Four article pairs passed the old lint while `article_client.md` only linked to the client's own service pages and never once to the partner's site — the partner noticed before Martin did. Never relint without both `--client-domain` and `--partner-domain` set.
12. **Only link to a page that is actually in the crawl cache.** A URL that was never fetched (a guessed sub-page like `/class`, `/product/sound-asleep`, `/blog.php`) must not appear as a link target, even if it looks plausible. If only the home page was crawled, link the home page; if a specific sub-page is needed, crawl it first via `site_cache.py` before writing the link.
13. **The partner-hosted article must show the client's full relevant offer, not only the lead service (added 2026-10-30 after Heartbeat Bali asked for it before we did).** `article_partner.md` runs on the partner's site and is the client's one page there. Lead service in depth, plus a short section naming every other client service that fits that partner's audience, each linked to its own crawl-cached page on the client's domain. Minimum 2 distinct client-domain URLs, aim for 3-4. `spec.json` records them as `secondary_client_pages`. Checkpoint 1 picks the angle and its depth; it never narrows the link set to one service. Do not mirror this on `article_client.md`: on the client's own site the piece is about the partner and links to the partner, client self-links there are optional.
