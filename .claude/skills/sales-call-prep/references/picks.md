# Picking the gap, the proof and the objections (step 3 — high freedom, judgment)

## The one new gap to lead with
Read `booking/baseline.md` (the review page) and `booking/m1.txt`, `m2.txt`, `m3.txt` (what has
gone out since). Anything already in a WhatsApp message is "shown" — mention it only as a
one-line callback ("du kennst das schon aus meiner Nachricht"), never re-explain it. Pick the
single clearest NOT-yet-shown finding from the baseline's critical/opportunity list as the
lead for the call. If the booking folder is thin (no `baseline.md` — see `UC3` in
`00_project/workflow-builds/sales-call-prep/use-cases.md`), do one small fresh look (her site,
her LinkedIn if reachable) instead of inventing a review-page history.

## Proof: one case, one in reserve
Pick from `00_project/prospect-mini/case-library.json` (same source booking-sequence uses) or
from the proof wall already shown to her on the review page. Match the case to her business
shape, not to the best-looking number:
- solo / premium / consulting positioning → a solo-consultant or practitioner case (e.g. Maria
  Abel, Sven Böhme)
- law firm / high-ticket B2B → a comparable high-ticket case (e.g. Dennis Dörfler)
- local practice / health → a local-practice case
If she has already seen one case via WhatsApp, open with it directly ("du kennst Maria Abel
schon") rather than re-pitching it, and hold a second, different case in reserve for if she
asks a follow-up question.

## Package recommendation
Memory `741studio-pricing.md` sets the fixed order: name the Power Engine (7.999 € + Ads)
first as the premium anchor, recommend the 90-Tage-Erfolgspaket (3.699 €) by default — it is
the only tier with the result guarantee — and mention the Starter (1.999 €) only as the low
anchor, never as the recommendation. Deviate from "recommend the 90-Tage tier" only when
Martin's own notes for this prospect say otherwise (e.g. she explicitly needs the calendar
full THIS month → Power Engine becomes the real recommendation, not just the anchor).

## Objections: rank, do not list all 11
`00_project/sales-einwandbehandlung.md` has 11 classic objections plus the capacity-objection
pattern (#11, "wir sind ausgelastat" / "wir wachsen schon über Empfehlungen"). Pick 2-4 that
fit THIS prospect's situation, most-likely first:
- Solo operator with a strong existing network/referral flow → lead with the capacity/
  referral reframe (#11 pattern: "mehr Qualität statt mehr Menge", recruiting-style reframe
  adapted to her funnel), not a generic price objection.
- A prospect who already pushed back once on the review page or in a prior chat (see
  `baseline.md` for any visible reply, or ask Martin) → lead with whichever objection she
  already raised, never start fresh on an objection she has moved past.
- Default fallback order when nothing else fits: "zu teuer" → "muss nachdenken" → "funktioniert
  das für meine Nische".
Always end the objection section with the one closing question from the call-dramaturgy:
"Sollen wir den Kick-off für nächste Woche fixieren?"
