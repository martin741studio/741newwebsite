# Worked example — Little Frenchie (prospect pitch, 2026-10-24)

The step-2.3 hand-run that shaped this skill. Full record: `00_project/workflow-builds/content-format-board/hand-run.md`. Use it as the pattern for "what a good board looks like" — not as text to copy.

## What made it work
1. **Reuse first.** The prospect folder already held intake, demand (search volumes), and the Local-Falcon competitor grid. Nothing was re-researched.
2. **The finding came before the formats.** Live Reddit/blog search showed the restaurant out-reviews competitors who out-rank it (1,421 reviews at 4.8 vs. a competitor at 721) yet never appears in the real "best brunch Canggu/Pererenan" conversation. That gap is the board's §1 and the reason to make video at all. A board that opens with a format list reads as generic advice.
3. **Formats were mapped to the real situation**, not listed: the steak-frites plating as a Single-Scene Hook; the regular arriving by scooter as the moment version; croissant lamination → sear → dining room as a Fast-Cut Story; the owner answering "why steak frites in Bali?" phone-in-hand as Talk-to-Camera; "why real steak frites needs this cut" as Why-We-Do-It — riding on the one real differentiator (the only genuinely French kitchen in the grid, every competitor is a generic "Restaurant/Cafe").
4. **Nothing was assumed about the client.** Readiness (ops/quality/skill at 2× volume) and the on-camera person were marked OPEN QUESTION. The owner's name was on the intake record, so she was named as the candidate — not as a decision.
5. **Research that failed was reported as failed.** Reddit cannot be scraped directly (site blocked for the scraper) — the real quotes came from search-result snippets. A direct brand search found nothing for the Bali location (only the Jakarta sister account) — stated as a finding, not papered over.

## Friction to design around (now in SKILL.md)
- Do not retry a direct Reddit scrape; use search snippets.
- State the gap in plain words before any format talk.
- Ask, never default, for readiness and the on-camera person.
- Internal format names never reach the board — `verify_board.py` blocks them.
