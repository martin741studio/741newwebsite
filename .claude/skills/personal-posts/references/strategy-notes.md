# Strategy notes — from Martin's "Content Marketing Playbook" knowledge base

Source: Martin's NotebookLM notebook (37 sources: platform-algorithm research, creator interviews, academic virality research), queried 2026-10-22. Only the findings that apply to TEXT social posts (this skill's scope) are kept here; video-only findings (Reels pattern interrupts, Shorts looping, script re-hooks) are left out as out of scope. Numbers below are the notebook's synthesis of its sources, not 741's own measured data — treat them as industry research informing technique, not as `claims-need-real-data`-grade facts about any specific post's performance.

## Directly actionable for this skill (folded into `voice-profile.md`)

- **No outbound links in the post body.** Platforms penalize posts with external links in the main text (the notebook's sources cite a 48-68% reach reduction). Put a link in the first comment instead, never the post itself. `scripts/check_lengths.py` now warns if a draft contains a bare URL.
- **"How I" beats "How to."** Speak from what you did and the result you saw, not from prescribing what the reader should do. "Here's what I did and what I saw" builds rapport; "here's what you should do" reads as lecturing and invites pushback. This already matches Martin's own voice (he talks about his own stance, not instructing the reader), but it is now an explicit check.
- **A damaging admission builds trust.** Owning a real flaw or limitation up front, before making a claim, is a credibility signal (not a hedge — a hedge softens a claim, an admission is a separate, specific flaw stated plainly). Optional technique, not mandatory on every post.
- **Numerical specificity beats vague claims.** A real, exact number performs better than a vague version of the same claim. Reinforces the existing style default ("one real example or number per post"); use the exact figure, never round to sound cleaner.
- **Negative or contrarian openings get a stronger hook than positive framing** ("X is a lie" outperforms "how to build better culture"). This matches the voice profile's existing "states the claim first" rule and Martin's own test draft, which already opens negatively.
- **Generic, unedited AI-sounding copy carries a real reach cost**, not just a style complaint. This is the concrete reason the slop gate (step 6) is non-negotiable, not a nice-to-have.

## Useful mental models (not scripted, used as judgment aids when drafting or reviewing a post)

- **SPCL (Status, Power, Credibility, Likeness):** the four things that build a founder's influence. Status = control over scarce resources (real numbers, real scale). Power = advice that actually worked when followed. Credibility = verifiable proof, not assertion. Likeness = staying grounded in Martin's actual values, not a generic "thought leader" voice. Useful as a self-check: does this post lean on at least one of these, honestly?
- **Benign Violation Theory:** something lands as sharp (not just offensive) when it breaks an expectation while staying safe/defensible. Useful for calibrating "polarizing" (the voice profile's own standard) against "reckless" — Martin's "we're all a family" post works because the violation (calling out a common corporate line) is real but the underlying claim is defensible, not cruel.
- **"Give away the secrets, sell the implementation."** Full tactical value in the post itself, no gating. Matches the existing "no outbound link in body" rule and the agency-insight topic lane.
- **Explicitly naming who a post is NOT for** sharpens alignment with the real audience more than trying to appeal to everyone. An optional move for agency-insight and SEO-opinion posts, not a requirement.

## Validates the existing design, no change needed

- Cadence: the notebook's sources put optimal founder cadence at 3-5 substantive posts per week per platform — matches Martin's own "3-4x/week" decision from the original grill.
- Save-worthy, substantive content (a real framework, a real number) outranks chasing likes — matches the existing "no engagement bait" hard no and the claim-proof pairing rule already in the voice profile.

## Noted but not adopted into v1 (flagged for Martin, not assumed)

- **Content repurposing pipeline (Test -> Record -> Contextualize):** post a raw idea as a short X/text post first, then expand only the ideas that actually land into the longer LinkedIn/video version, rather than drafting all 4 platforms simultaneously from one thought every time. Martin's answer (card, 2026-10-22): "Keep current pipeline" — stay with drafting all 4 platforms at once from every thought, revisit test-first repurposing later once there is real post history to judge what "lands" against.
- **Long-tail revival:** reviving a post that performed well with a fresh comment extends its distribution window 14-21 days. Could become a topic-backlog category later ("revisit this post with new context"), not built in v1.
- **Multi-tiered content architecture** (short-form / long-form / owned communities): describes a broader content system than this skill's scope (social posts only, not newsletters or podcasts). Out of scope by design.
