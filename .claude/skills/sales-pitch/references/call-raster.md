# 741 60-Minute Call-Raster & Phase Guide

Owner: Martin Drendel · 741 Studio Reference Architecture

---

## Phase 1: Frame & Agenda Setting (Min 00–05)
- **Goal:** Neutralize tension, establish peer status, obtain explicit conversational leadership.
- **Key Principle:** Never show eagerness to sell. Position call as an exploratory diagnostic consult.
- **Script:**
  > "Lass uns kurz den Ablauf für die Stunde abstecken, damit du den maximalen Mehrwert rausziehst: Ich habe mir deine Auffindbarkeit, Konkurrenz und Website genau angeschaut. Mein Ziel heute ist nicht, dir etwas aufzudrängen, sondern eine ehrliche Diagnose. Wir schauen: Wo stehst du, wo willst du wirtschaftlich hin, und warum greift deine aktuelle Website die Nachfrage noch nicht ab? Wenn am Ende beiderseits das Gefühl da ist: 'Hey, das System passt perfekt', zeige ich dir genau, wie wir das umsetzen. Wenn nicht, nimmst du einfach die genauen Audit-Daten mit. Klingt das fair?"

---

## Phase 2: Deep Discovery & Consequence Diagnosis (Min 05–20)
- **Goal:** Uncover the emotional, operational, and financial reality of their bottleneck.
- **Principles:**
  - Ask solution-neutral, diagnostic questions.
  - Let the prospect describe the pain; do not rush to rescue them.
  - Quantify the Cost of Inaction (Keenan Gap): What does waiting another year cost in lost revenue?

---

## Phase 3: The Proof & System Prescription (Min 20–35)
- **Goal:** Present the tailored solution grounded in Review-Page data without technical feature vomiting.
- **The 3 Pillars:**
  1. *Local Top-3 Ranking Radar:* 49-point geo-grid tracking (Falcon). Proof: Maria Abel 0→100% in 8 weeks.
  2. *Conversion-Showpiece:* Clear patient guidance, mobile speed, direct WhatsApp/Call pathways.
  3. *Relevanz & Content-Cluster:* High-margin treatments isolated and amplified.

---

## Phase 4: The 3-Tier Offer Stack & Price Anchoring (Min 35–45)
- **Goal:** Anchor value against lost opportunity and in-house salaries; present the 3 packages; de-risk with the guarantee.
- **Packages:**
  - *Power Engine (7.999 €):* Full-scale engine with Paid Ads, CRO, and Content.
  - *Core Offer: 90-Tage-Local-SEO (3.699 €):* Recommended package with the Performance Guarantee.
  - *Starter Setup (1.999 €):* Foundation cleanup without guarantee (Downsell).

---

## Phase 5: Systematic Objection Handling (Min 45–55)
- **Goal:** Pre-empt objections with an Accusation Audit, agree & reframe, back up with math and proof.
- **Core Stance:** An objection is a request for security, not rejection.

---

## Phase 6: Seamless Close & Commitment (Min 55–60)
- **Goal:** Lock in agreement, confirm kickoff date in calendar, initiate onboarding.
