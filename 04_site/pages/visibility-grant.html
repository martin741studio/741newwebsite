Terms & Conditions – 741 Visibility Grant

1. Organiser

The giveaway is organised by 741 Digital Solutions LLC (“741.Studio”). The giveaway is in no way connected to Google, Meta or LinkedIn.

2. Eligibility & closing date

Entry is free and takes place via the application form on this page. Eligible to participate are exclusively active owners or responsible practitioners of an already existing, genuinely operating practice or health-profession business in Germany, Austria or Switzerland who already run their own active Google Business Profile (“Google My Business”) for that practice. This promotion is aimed at real practitioners in the listed fields (e.g. physiotherapy, naturopathy, holistic health, occupational therapy, speech therapy, osteopathy, dentistry, health coaching).

Not eligible are, in particular: mere prospects without their own practice, brand-new or not-yet-opened practices, and participants without an existing Google Business Profile. 741.Studio may verify these requirements before awarding the prize (e.g. proof of the practice and the Google profile) and may exclude participants who do not meet them or provide false information from the draw, or revoke the prize afterwards; in that case a re-draw may take place.

The closing date is Oct 31, 2026, 11:59 pm. One entry per practice.

3. Selection of the winner (random draw)

The winning practice is selected from all valid applications via a documented random draw during Nov 3–7, 2026. The draw is recorded and verifiable. The judges’ decision is final; legal recourse is excluded. The winner is notified by email.

4. Prize

The prize is a visibility package worth €5,000 (website relaunch/optimisation, Google Business Profile build-out, local SEO on the most important treatment keywords, review setup, and 90 days of hands-on support). No cash payout or transfer of the prize is possible.

5. Live case study

By accepting the prize, the winning practice agrees that 741.Studio may publicly document the implementation journey as a case study and use it for marketing purposes (practice name, city, result figures, before/after). Sensitive patient data is expressly excluded.

6. Referrals

Participants receive a personal invitation link. If a practice referred via this link wins, the referring practice also receives the prize package. Sharing the link is done exclusively on the participants’ own initiative; 741.Studio does not contact any third parties on behalf of participants.

7. Data & newsletter

The details provided in the form are processed exclusively to run the giveaway and to get in touch (details: privacy policy). Receiving the newsletter is not a condition of entry and only happens with active, separate consent, which can be revoked at any time.

8. Non-winners

All participants who are not drawn receive a free visibility check and the offer of a limited-time grant rate. There is no obligation to purchase anything.
