🟢 Strengths

  • A clear, ownable niche — precision health & feminine leadership for women in midlife.
  • Real depth: a six-pillar methodology and 18 structured service pages.
  • Strong, branded page title and social-share (Open Graph) tags in place.

🟠 Biggest opportunity

Foundations to fix

  • No Google Analytics / Tag Manager — visitors and enquiries are untracked.
  • Default favicon — the unbranded Squarespace icon in the browser tab.
