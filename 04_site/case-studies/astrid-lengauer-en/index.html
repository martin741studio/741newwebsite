Starting point

Strong portfolio, weak visibility.

Creatively excellent, but hard to find on Google: around position 18 for “interior design Vienna”. Profile and website were not keyword-optimised and had no local backlink foundation. The real state from the initial audit:

Google Business Profile

  • Title / description not keyword-optimised
  • Secondary categories incomplete (upholsterer, furniture designer …)
  • Services (moodboard, concept, 3D) not listed
  • Hours not fully covered
  • No review strategy

Website, tech & local presence

  • H1–H3 not keyword-focused
  • Missing meta titles / descriptions per page
  • No service / service-area sub-pages
  • No clean local backlink profile
  • No structured tracking

We measured the real search volume in Vienna & Styria and focused on “interior design / interior architecture Vienna” and the right secondary keywords.

Step 2 · Execution

What we actually did.

Every parameter of our checklist – including around 100 local backlinks as a healthy foundation, Google product stacking and YouTube keyword coverage.

Google Business Profile

The foundation of local visibility

  • Keyword-optimised title & description
  • All categories + services: interior design, moodboard & concept, concept presentation, procurement, project supervision, 3D rendering
  • Hours, booking link, photos & regular posts
Website & On-Page

Technically clean & keyword-focused

  • Mobile optimisation & load time
  • Keyword H1–H3 + meta title/description, schema & rich results
  • Internal linking + service / service-area pages
Reviews

Building trust

  • Review strategy with a QR code in the signature
  • Follow-up templates after appointments
  • A reply to every review
Citations & local backlinks

Found everywhere, connected locally

  • NAP-consistent directories + Apple Maps & Bing Places
  • Meta / Facebook listing built
  • Local partner backlinks in the service area (741 link network)
Google Product Stacking

Reinforcing the entity

  • Linked Google assets (Business Profile, Maps, Sites/Docs/Slides)
  • Consistent entity & location signals
  • Strengthens local relevance & trust
YouTube & content coverage

Keyword coverage across channels

  • Topic content for secondary keywords
  • YouTube to cover long-tail searches
  • Internal links to the core keywords
Tracking & reporting

Making it measurable

  • Google Analytics 4 + Search Console
  • Weekly Google Maps grid tracking (Local Falcon)
  • Progress documented week by week

From position 18 to the top – same designer, same portfolio, finally the visibility to match across the whole Vienna & Styria area.
