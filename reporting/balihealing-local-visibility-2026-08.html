🧭 What this means

1

The ad works — but almost nobody sees it

An 8.0% click-through rate is above average, so the ad and page are good. The problem is only ~12 searches a day for "ozone therapy" in Bali. It's a demand ceiling, not a budget or copy problem.

2

You already own your core on the Map

#1 for "naturopath" across almost the whole Canggu area, and a solid top-3 for ozone. That's excellent — it's driving your strong organic enquiries.

3

The real growth is your other services

You offer acupuncture but rank ~#10 while one clinic owns the map. The same is likely true for TCM, osteopathy and physiotherapy — services you provide but don't yet rank for. That's untapped, capturable visibility.
