# Summary — kamil-oberhausen-audit

I checked physiotherapieoberhausen.com from the outside, like a normal visitor. I looked for the same spam signs found on physiotherapiekraft.de: strange links, strange redirects, strange footer text, hidden pages in the sitemap, and spam in the Google search index.

Result: clean. I found no bad links, no odd redirects, and no spam pages. The sitemap lists only the 7 real pages of the site.

What is missing: this check cannot see inside WordPress (plugins, files, user accounts). A hack can hide there even if the site looks clean from outside.
