# Summary (plain English)

I checked the public pages of physiotherapieoberhausen.com from the outside, like a normal visitor. I did not log in anywhere and did not change anything.

What I found: no fake "verify you are human" screen, no hidden scripts, no spam content, and no broken main links. The site looks clean on the surface.

One thing to check: the TikTok link in the footer points to the account of the sister site that was hacked (@physiotherapie_kraft), and the Instagram link points to an unrelated account name. These may be old copy-paste mistakes, not a hack — but worth a quick look.

What is missing: I could not see the raw page code, only the text a visitor sees. A hidden injection that only shows to search engines or on a second visit would not show up this way. For full certainty, a real malware scanner (Sucuri or VirusTotal) or a Search Console check is still needed.

Decision for Martin: nothing urgent on this site from the outside. Worth asking Kamil whether the TikTok/Instagram footer links are correct, and whether to run a malware scan on this site too, not just the Kraft site.
